Example: Can a Power of Attorney submit documents on behalf of a deceased Account Holder?

Contributed by: Marco Steiner, Senior Expert, Geneva

Question

Can a Power of Attorney submit documents on behalf of a deceased Account Holder?

Answer

No — a Power of Attorney is automatically revoked on the death of the Account Holder under Julius Baer's standard terms, unless the mandate explicitly states otherwise (rare, and requires Legal sign-off to confirm). Once the bank is notified of death, all instructions must come through the estate's legal representative or executor, not the former PoA holder. Escalate any document received from a former PoA post-death to Legal before acting on it.
