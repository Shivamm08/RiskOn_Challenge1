Prohibited Activities and Relationships

Can I open a numbered account?

No, you cannot open a numbered account. The opening of numbered (and anonymous) accounts is explicitly prohibited at Julius Baer.

For the full list of prohibited activities and relationships, refer to Section 1 "Prohibited activities and relationships" of the JBG-2003-00 Private Banking Client Acceptance Policy.

Other prohibited activities (high level)

  • Numbered or anonymous accounts of any kind.
  • Relationships that cannot satisfy standard KYC / client identification requirements.
  • Shell entities without a verifiable underlying beneficial owner.

For edge cases not covered here, escalate to the Business Front Support or your BRM Suitability Lead rather than making a case-by-case judgment call.
