1. For which Service Models is this alert applicable?

This check is applicable for discretionary mandates only (no applicability for advisory mandates).


2. Description of the alert

ESG stands for environmental (e.g. energy and water consumption), social (e.g. employer appeal and supply chain management) and governance (e.g. remuneration policy and company management). It is a set of standards and criteria used to measure a company's impact and performance, which are increasingly being framed by various laws and regulations globally.

The alert compares the ESG category of the discretionary mandate with the ESG Preferences the client has selected in the Client Investment Profile (CIP), as the Discretionary Mandates need to be in line with the ESG preferences chosen by the client. 

There is a point of sale check in the Mandate Solution Designer (MSD) as soon as a Discretionary Mandate is opened. If a discretionary mandate is set-up via AOF rather than MSD, or a client changes their ESG preferences to a higher category, the overnight portfolio monitoring alert in Wealth Navigator notifies the RM of this mismatch.


3. What triggers this alert?

The alert is triggered in case a Discretionary Mandate has been selected, that does not correspond the ESG preference specified in the client’s CIP (e.g. the Discretionary Mandate is subject to Traditional Investments but the client has selected Responsible Investment in the CIP). The alert is triggered in the Client Book as a result of an overnight portfolio risk monitoring.


4. How can this alert be resolved?

You can resolve the alert with one of the following actions:

Client Book (overnight portfolio monitoring alert)

DutyResolving the alert & Documentation (Resolution / Contact Note)

RM must inform the client within 5 business days


RM may discuss with the client to change the Discretionary Mandate (no resolution note required, if change is initiated immediately, but RMs should add a comment on the change of the mandate in a contact note, explaining the reason for the mandate change [i.e. alignment with the ESG preferences in the CIP]). If a proposal is sent to change the Discretionary Mandate, use resolution note “investment proposal sent” (alert will be snoozed for 30 days).

RM may discuss with the client to change the ESG preferences of the client (no resolution note required, but RMs should add a comment on the change of ESG preferences in a contact note, explaining that the ESG preferences have been changed so that the mandate is now in line with the adapted ESG preferences).

If the client neither wants to change the Discretionary Mandate nor the ESG preference in the CIP and accepts the risk:

RM to select "client accepts risk" in the alert justification and document 1) the risks that have been explained, 2) the client’s decision that he wants to keep the mandate/ the ESG preferences and 3) the client's rationale in the contact note (see also examples below).

In case the client was not reached, RM to select "Client not reached" in the alert justification and document 1) date and 2) channel (e.g. email) and 3) the fact that there is no client response yet.


Validity of Resolution Notes (RN)

The Resolution Note is valid until the snooze period ends.

If the alert is retriggered during the snooze period, the client must be contacted and informed again about the new situation. Reference to the first contact is not sufficient.


5. Justification example(s)

 Use case

Example

Overnight portfolio monitoring alert

The client has stated an ESG preference of Responsible in the CIP. However, he has read about our Edelweiss Balanced mandate and would like to open another DM. I have explained to him, that this DM has no ESG consideration and that this would be against his preferences stated in the investment profile. He confirms that he is well aware of this, and would only invest a part of his wealth into this mandate, therefore he accepts this deviation, however, he also re-emphasizes that the preference in the CIP is generally correct.
