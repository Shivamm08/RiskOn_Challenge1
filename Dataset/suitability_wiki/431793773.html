STEP 1: BASIC ASSESSMENT 

Please ensure that there is no conflicting information on the form (e.g., a client/partner stating ample experience in a product category but no corresponding knowledge) or conflicting information that you already have from the client (e.g., a client/partner previously stating that she/he has traded many Hedge Funds but states to have no knowledge). Additionally, indicate the according main source(s) on the 2nd page of the K&E-Form by crossing 1 out of the 4 boxes. In case of no contradictory or conflicting information, the assessment can be finished. If you are unsure about the client’s/partner’s knowledge, you find sample questions for more complex asset classes that you can ask to increase the level of confidence that the client is familiar with the risks and characteristics of a product category. Proceed to the next step for the documentation requirement.

STEP 2: DOCUMENTATION OF THE ASSESSMENT

Once the K&E form is complete and the partner has signed the form, you must document the assessment in a contact note. This contact note reference must also be captured on the form before sending it to CDS. Note: Capture the u-Number of the RM on the form who has performed the assessment even if the contact note is captured by the RMA. This contact note reference replaces the RM signature. Forms without filled in contact note reference will be returned by CDS (for avoidance of doubt, the contact note itself must not be handed in together with the form). For CUR set-ups please check the respective operational guidelines regarding contact notes: 


Please note, also the CIP form itself does no longer require the RM signature but a contact note documenting the profiling conversation and confirm completeness and correctness. Both, the CIP and the K&E documentation can be captured in one note (as it is usually one conversation), 

Example K&E: I have discussed and assessed the CIP and the K&E declared by the client. There are no conflicting statements in the declaration. The high level of Knowledge & Experience is plausible based on the professional background of the client as a former commodity trader.

Example CIP: I have discussed the Client Investor Profile with Mrs. X and confirm the consistency, correctness and completeness and validity of the stated values.
