Background

Under MiFID2, the Client Investment Profile (CIP) and the Knowledge and Experience (K&E) forms are key documents that serves as the foundation for determining whether investment advice is suitable and appropriate for the client. BJBE must ensure account holders / partners have full transparency and access to the latest state of these forms to understand which instruments are deemed appropriate and if the provided investment advice is suitable for them.

Following the CNMV on-site inspection, it was noted that clients of the Advisory Office Spain do not consistently receive copies of signed forms or reliable access to their current content. To address this, an interim manual process has been implemented to provide clients (or authorised representatives) with a copy of the CIP and/or Side Form, and each partner with their completed K&E form.

Please follow the below interim process until the 1st of July 2026 (when an automated solution will be put in place):

A. Provision and storing of CIP and CIP Side Form

I. Applicable scenarios (at account level)

    • An advisory or discretionary mandate is assigned to a portfolio for the first time in an account (i.e. client onboarding, new portfolio opening);
    • Modification of CIP and/or CIP Side Form.

II. Applicable forms

    • 1-1162 Client Investment Profile – Legal entities
    • 1-1169 Client Investment Profile – Private Individuals
    • 1-1185 Client Investment Profile – Life Insurance Companies
    • 1-1293 Client Investment Profile – Side Form
