Solicitation Rules

The Solicitation Framework sets out the applicable Solicitation Rules. The solicitation Types are linked to the Investment Universe Types. In some scenarios/constellations advice is permitted in others no advice is allowed. 

Permitted actions

What does that mean for the different solicitation scenarios in detail?

Actively solicited

→ Basically, only instruments from the offered universe can be actively solicited!

The RM shall not solicit financial instruments from the other Investment Universe Types. However, Advice to Sell/Reduce a certain position is allowed for all instruments irrespective of the Investment Universe (e.g. client needs cash, reduction of overall risk exposure, alignment to strategic or tactical asset allocation, change of investment strategy). Advice to Hold a position is allowed for instruments assigned to the Offered Universe and the Ok to keep Universe only.

Reverse solicited - Client asks for advice on a specific Financial Instrument

If the specific financial instrument is part of: 

  • Offered Universe: the RM is allowed to provide Advice and recommend the client to buy the financial instrument. 

  • Ok to Keep: the RM is allowed to advise to hold, sell, or reduce the financial instrument, but the RM is not allowed to advise to buy resp. increase the position. 
    Example: 

    Client holds fund X and asks if he should increase the position. Inform the client that we have a neutral view and therefore we do not advice to increase the position. 

  • Negative View: the RM must advise to sell or reduce (“advise against”) the financial instrument.
    Example: 

    Client asks if he should invest in the fund X. Advice the client against an investment (Negative Advice!). 

    Client instructs you to buy the fund X. If the client has a Service Model "Advice Premium" or "Advice Exclusive" you advice against the investment, because the overnight monitoring alert will require you to advice the client to sell the instrument. 

  • No Bank View: the RM shall not give any Advice. However, Factual Information may be provided.
    Example: 

    Client asks your opinion on an instrument which is in the No Bank View category (e.g. a bond issued by BJB). You can provide the client with factual information, but no advice is allowed. 

  • Out-of-Monitored Universe: the RM is prohibited to provide Advice to buy or hold the financial instrument. 
    Example:

Client asks you opinion on an instrument, which is in the "out-of-monitored Universe". You inform the client that the instrument is not monitored and hence that the Bank does not have a view on the instrument. Therefore, only factual information may be provided and only to the extent of the client’s request.

If your client has existing positions classified as Out of Monitored: You can advise your client to sell the instrument, but please remember to use as rationale for your advice that we don’t cover it. Advising the client to switch into a comparable instrument in the Offered universe is recommended – with the rationale that the Offered instrument is monitored and we have a clear positive view on it.

Important: Clients can always proceed without Advice. The RM shall in this case inform the client that the transaction is executed as non-advised and solicitation type needs to be switched in DiAS from reverse solicited to unsolicited. The RM can counter-propose a different financial instrument from the offered universe. In this case switch the solicitation type to actively solicited. 

Reverse solicited - Client has a specific Investment Idea in mind and asks for advice

If the client asks the RM for advice on a Specific Investment Idea the RM must chose an instrument from the Offered Universe. 

If no appropriate financial instrument is available in the Offered Universe, the RM is permitted to provide Factual Information on financial instruments from the Ok to Keep, the No Bank View and the Out-of-Monitored Universe under the following conditions:

-  The RM shall only forward the complete list of financial instruments meeting the Specific Investment Idea of the client (a selection would qualify as Investment Advice) 
-  The RM shall clearly state and document, that it is Factual Information and should not be considered as Investment Advice and hence, no suitability assessment has been performed

Important: In case the client asks for advice on one of the financial instruments provided, the RM can inform the client on the Bank's view, but is not allowed to advice to buy or increase the position (see scenario 2.1). In case the client comes back and wants to buy a financial instrument provided, flag the transaction as unsolicited. 

Unsolicited

All financial instruments within all Investment Universe Types are eligible.
