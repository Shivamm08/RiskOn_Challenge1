Summary

Sustainable finance requires us to adapt the way we advise our clients. In advisory, this means:

In all JB locations, ESG Risk Investments are excluded from the offering (universe status "No Bank View"). This means that ESG Risk Investments cannot be advised, even upon the request of the client. Only factual information may be provided.

In JB locations where client ESG preferences are gathered: proactive advice can only be provided on instruments with an ESG category equal or higher than client ESG preferences (e.g. responsible clients can be proactively advised with responsible and sustainable instruments, but not with traditional instruments).

ESG data in Portfolio View

The ESG preference category applicable to the mandate is shown (traditional, responsible, or sustainable).

ESG data in Proposal Tool

The ESG preference category of the mandate is shown under "Investment Parameters" (traditional, responsible, or sustainable).

Additionally, the ESG categories of portfolio holdings are shown (for asset classes covered by the JB ESG Investment Rating Methodology - currently equities, corporate bonds, 3rd party funds and sovereign bonds).

Finally, the breakdown of holdings between the JB ESG categories can be viewed in both as a table and as a pie chart. To view the information, click on either of the "ESG" tabs.

ESG data in the Advisory Session

The ESG preference category of the mandate is shown on the top of the screen (traditional, responsible, or sustainable).

ESG data in Advanced Search

Similar search criteria that will be available on PSP will also be available in the WN Advanced Search (advisory session & proposal tool).

ESG Suitability Checks

ESG suitability checks have been introduced to ensure suitable advice in accordance to client ESG Preferences. These alerts will apply to RM Locations Switzerland, Luxembourg, Spain and Ireland.

For existing positions, an overnight portfolio monitoring alert has been introduced for ESG risk investments and for the ESG category mismatch.

The Personal Investment Recommendation (PIR) and Portfolio Analysis Report (PAR) have ben enhanced to include the ESG category information.
