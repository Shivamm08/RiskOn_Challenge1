By default, 100% of the amount of all Lombard Loans booked to the advisory portfolio is considered as reinvested. However, if the borrowed money is not or only partially invested in this advisory portfolio, i.e. borrowed money was transferred out of the portfolio or reinvested in another portfolio, then this can be specified in the portfolio configuration (under “Loan Configuration”). With the reduction of the reinvested loan amount, the portfolio leverage ratio will be reduced in the consequence and the CPR is lower as well also see more details here. Therefore, comprehensive documentation of the client agreement is required correspondingly:

Example:        

We will increase the existing Lombard loan as previously agreed from 2 to 2.5 Mio. for another 6 months at the rate of x%. The client informed me, that she would need the additional 500k for the downpayment of her new holiday home and that she will reduce back to 2 Mio. after 6 months when her fix-term investment is due. I informed her, that for the risk monitoring we calculate the loan as being reinvested into the portfolio but that we could also exclude it, which would lead to a lower risk validation. She understood and agreed that only the 2 Mio. should be considered as reinvested for the next 6 months. I confirmed that I will enter the configuration this way.


How is a change done in WN:

1. Enter the Portfolio Configuration Screen from the respective portfolio view:


2. Enter “Loan” and insert the amount. If you need to amend additional values in other tabs (e.g. SAA Leeway), continue to adjust all values and only then press “save”.

3. The distribution screen for the Portfolio configuration notification appears where you can configure, preview, and select the language of the client output.

4. The configuration dialogue allows to amend the language again or hide the portfolio number and previews the document. Press “save & close” to continue with the distribution. Note, Loan configuration is only mentioned in the overview table of the confirmation if you actually have one activated. In case of several loans, only amended loans (i.e. loans with a configuration are shown in the document, the others not). 

5. Select the contact partner and distribution channel to share the document. Note that if the contact partner has e-banking, e-mail to me is not available. It is strongly recommended to use an electronic distribution channel where the archiving of the handover is already stored.

6. Complete the contact note by adding additional information on the client request (see points that need to be documented as well as the example above for guidance). In case you hand over the document directly to the client (using “email to me”) ensure that the handover and evidence is archived. For additional support the changes are already pre-filled.


Note that the changes are only done if you finish the process by pressing “submit & finish”.
