JB Governance Score

https://wiki.juliusbaer.com/spaces/fkh/pages/349882021/JB+Governance+Score

In short:

The JB Governance Score evaluates how “good” or “bad” issuers are in the topics of sound management structures, employee relations, remuneration of staff and tax compliance. The score averages several relevant data points from both MSCI and Sustainalytics and classifies issuers in comparison to all issuers in our booked universe.

Short explanation of the calculation:

Good governance is a commonly used term, however, there is no universally accepted definition of good governance, nor any globally agreed mechanism to judge whether governance is "good" or "bad.” The United Nations defined the concept of good governance as consensus oriented, accountable, transparent, responsive, effective and efficient, equitable and inclusive, and follows the rule of law. Furthermore, it seeks to ensure that corruption is minimized, the views of minorities are taken into account and the voices of the most vulnerable people in society are heard in decision-making. It is also responsive both to the present and future needs of society.

The governance Theme Score addresses the question of a corporate issuer's business behaviour, in its pure definition, i.e. in terms of policies, organisation structures, ethics, code of conduct, or accountability.
