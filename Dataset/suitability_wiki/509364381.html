The default bond rating threshold is set at investment grade (BBB-). Lowering of this threshold only makes sense if the client wishes to be actively advised on lower-rated bonds, as lowering the threshold reduces monitoring and can increase the risk for the client that he/she misses a rating change. Document the request of the client and the decision accordingly in a contact note. As a principle always mention 1. the request from the client and the threshold, 2. the reason for an adjustment, 3. the risk disclosure towards the client including consequences of the change and 4. the final agreement with the client

Example:        

The client plans to invest in lower rated US bonds and asked for my recommendation. I informed her, that based on our Advisory Mandate we only recommend Investment Grade Bonds. She asked if she can’t get any recommendation below Investment Grade even if she wanted. I explained to her, that she could lower the bond rating threshold but that this would also apply then for the monitoring of existing bonds. She is fine with it and asked to reduce the threshold to B+.  

Please note: The strategy of the client should also be considered. If a client wants to invest into lower rated bonds with a higher product risk rating, it should be ensured, that from an investment strategy view, there is sufficient risk budget available to prevent any CPR breaches.


How is a change done in WN:

1. Enter the Portfolio Configuration Screen from the respective portfolio view:

2. Open the “Portfolio configuration” and stay on the tab “Mandate” and adjust to the desired monitoring value for the bond rating. If you need to amend additional values in other tabs (e.g. SAA Leeway), continue to adjust all values and only then press “save”.

3. The distribution screen for the Portfolio configuration notification appears where you can configure, preview, and select the language of the client output.

4. The configuration dialogue allows to amend the language again or hide the portfolio number and previews the document. Press “save & close” to continue with the distribution.

5. Select the contact partner and distribution channel to share the document.  The notification must be shared via e-channel but can be simultaneously e-mailed to the RM (e.g. for a client meeting).  

6. In the contact note, the ‘additional notes’ field is already pre-filled with the applied changes. Complete the contact note by adding additional information on describing the client request, see points that need to be documented as well as the example above for guidance. Don’t forget to archive any additional evidence, e.g., full e-mail exchange with the client, accordingly.

Note that the changes are only done if you finish the process by pressing “submit & finish”.
