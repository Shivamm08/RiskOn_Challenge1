1.- Does a higher ESG category imply a higher expected return?

Currently, the official bank view is that there is no clear correlation between performance and ESG classification. It is however fair to assume that issuers with a better ESG rating have less exposure to sustainability risks.

3.- How often is the ESG classification of instruments updated?

The classification of instruments is updated bimonthly. The frequency might change over time and ad hoc runs might be performed when deemed necessary.

4.- Where can we find the rationale of why an instrument is classified an ESG Risk?

A short rationale (which basic screening criteria have failed) can be found on the Excel with the ESG information on instruments available in the ESG dashboard (type EGS into your browser) and PSP.

5.- How is the JB Sustainable Investment Rating Methodology governed?

The methodology is governed by the operational Responsible Investment Committee (RIC). The operational RIC in turn is governed by the strategic RIC . The strategic RIC decides on the how the methodology works overall and how it will be developed in the future. The operational RIC is in charge of reviewing the classification of cases which have been challenged by the organization. The review of raised cases follows a materiality approach, meaning that bigger holdings in the bank will be given priority in the operational RIC. 
The RIC committees are furthermore governed by executive board.

6.- Where can the decisions of the RIC be found?

The old and the new (changed) value for the JB ESG category of the instrument and the reasoning for the change can be found on PSP and on our ESG landing page (type ESG into your browser).

7.- How can the classification of an instrument be challenged?

These requests are handled by the Operational RIC. RMs are kindly requested to contact their Investment Partner / asset class specialist for further details.

8.- Who can challenge ESG categories?

An upgrade of an ESG Risk categorized instrument, or up/downgrade of an instrument categorized as Sustainable, Responsible or Traditional, can be requested by the Boutique Heads, Portfolio Managers, Research Analysts, Investment Specialists, Investment Advisors, members of the OP-RIC, or members of the IWMS and CRO organization, as well as the Sustainability Team.

9.- How long are overrides following an approved ESG challenge valid for?

All overrides need to be validated once a year, or more frequent if so decided by the operational RIC.
