2. Description of the alert

With the classification "Negative View" JB expresses its own view on a specific financial instrument, e.g. for which it expects a weak performance or (partial) capital loss. In this case either a "Solicitation Alert" or a "Negative View" alert is triggert. 

Difference:

  • Solicitation Alerts are session alerts and only triggered in an advised scenario (i.e. actively or reverse solicitation). Solicitation alerts are not triggered overnight (i.e the overnight portfolio monitoring alert).
  • Negative View Alerts are triggered overnight and in the session for unsolicited transaction in Advice Premium, Advice Exclusive and Advice SCS mandates. 


3. What triggers this alert?

A negative view alert can be triggered either:

a) in the Advisory Session as a result of newly unsolicited transactions, or

b) in the Client Book as a result of overnight portfolio risk monitoring if financial instruments in the client portfolio are allocated to the "Negative View" Investment Universe


4. How can this alert be resolved?

You can resolve the alert with one of the following actions:

A. Advisory Session (session alert)

Solicitation Type

Duty

Documentation (Justification / Contact Note)

Actively solicited

n.a.

There is no negative view alert. This scenario is covered by the Solicitation Alert (→Solicitation Alerts)


Reverse solicited

n.a.

There is no negative view alert. This scenario is covered by the Solicitation Alert (→Solicitation Alerts)


Unsolicited

RM/IA must warn the client about the negative view on the instrument and can recommend an instrument from the offered universe instead (business opportunity)

If client accepts the risk and wants to proceed:

RM/IA to document the warning in the alert justification/ contact note as described here: →Justification rationale (dropdown values) for "client accepts risk"

Important: In the event of any data issues in the system or in dedicated permitted use cases (e.g. workarounds) it is possible to overrule the alert and document it according to the defined process →Hard Block Override Procedure


B. Client Book (overnight portfolio monitoring alert)

DutyResolving the alert & Documentation (Resolution / Contact Note)

RM/IA must inform the client within 5 business days


RM/IA to create an investment proposal to sell the entire position and provide it to the client. Use resolution note “investment proposal sent” (alert will be snoozed for 30 days)

If the client does not want to sell the entire position and accepts the risk:

RM/IA to create a resolution note in the client book (“client accepts risk” – also see examples below).

Please note: In case of a single resolution note (use the check Action symbol [  ] in the line of the respective alert), the justification rationale are available to support documentation (→Justification rationale (dropdown values) for "client accepts risk"). However, if you are using “justify all” to close one or several alerts with “client accepts risk”, please note 1) the risks that have been explained, 2) the client’s decision after the explanation and 3) the client's rationale in the contact note.

If the client’s portfolio is enabled for One-way Notification (OWN):

RM/IA to create a resolution note in the client book/portfolio alert overview. Select justification option “client informed” and follow the process in Wealth Navigator to dispatch the portfolio monitoring notification to the client. Further details can be found under →One-way notification for monitoring alerts

If the client could not be reached, RM/IA to select “client not reached” in the alert justification and document 1) the date when the client was approached and 2) the channel in the contact note.


Validity of Resolution Notes (RN)

A Resolution Note for negative view is valid until the snooze period ends.


5. Justification examples

Use Case

Examples

 Session alert

  • The account holder called my ARM and wanted to invest in Square Inc. and Tesla, for which JB has a Negative View. My ARM informed her about the Negative View and warned her not to invest.
  • She decided to not invest in Square, but still decided to buy Tesla. She said investors are too pessimistic about Tesla’s future prospects, as it is not only a car company, but also an energy company, and that she believes in Elon Musk.

Overnight portfolio monitoring alert

  • I informed the authorized signatory that JB has issued a neg. view on Turkey government bonds, implicating potential downside. The portfolio is exposed to one TRY-denominated bond and one USD-denominated bond. I advised her to sell both.
  • She agreed to sell the TRY bond but wanted to hold the USD bond for the time being. We agreed to review the situation again in one month.
