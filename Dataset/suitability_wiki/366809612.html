The “Product Risk Rating” is in-line with industry and regulatory development. The methodology affects all financial instruments and advisory models. This framework provides all necessary information you need to know. We will gladly support you in case of any questions.

The product risk set-up

The Julius Baer Product Risk Rating classifies financial instruments into four risk categories namely “low”, “moderate”, “considerable” and “high”. The classification is derived from the LTV (loan to value) and takes into account different risk factors, such as volatility, CVaR, asset-class based stress test scenarios, credit default risk and liquidity risk, depending on the instrument type.
