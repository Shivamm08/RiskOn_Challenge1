Create Proposal

OVERVIEW OF THE STEPS


1. Open the client for whom you would like to create a proposal via 'Clients'


3. From the 'three dots menu' on the right hand side, you select 'Create Proposal'.


4. Select 'Positions' in order to place your 'buy, subscribe, issue or sell, redeem' positions into this proposal 


5a. Under this section (5) you can add a completely new position onto the respective portfolio:

Select 'Add Position' in order to buy or subscribe and feed the search with either Identifier or Instrument Name then select from the dropdown which instrument you would like to add to the portfolio. Consider Building Blocks (Blocks such as Advisory Equity List and Investment Updates) as well.


5b. Verify the instrument and amend the missing information like 'Type', 'Units' or 'Amount' and the 'Currency' before adding the instrument. After selecting 'Add' it will take you back to the 'Position' page.

Should you not find the wished instrument, click on Advanced Search. Should you still not find the instrument proceed with "Create Instrument" or add a custom instrument, instructions you can find here.


5c. back under 'Positions' you will find the deduction of cash highlighted in light red (reduction of an existing position) and solid green (adding a new instrument)


6a. Under this section (6) you can reduce an existing position:

Select any instrument (in our case Reg. SHS Swiss Life Holding) in the portfolio in order to sell (all holdings), reduce (part of the holdings) or redeem. In our case we will 'sell' all the holdings.


6b. overwrite the actual 220 instruments held with the new balance 'zero' and the graphic will change to a solid red color (selling all holdings of this instrument). And the 'Cash' position will be credited accordingly.


7a. For advice for all buy/increase and sell/reduce transactions in the Proposal. The RM needs to add reasons for advice for all new transactions. 


7b. When proactively providing advice to a client to hold a certain position, it is mandatory to choose a reason.  


8. Please hit the 'Recalculate Notifications' button to re-run the checks and confirm that the distribution of your proposal and sending of the client package to the prospect customer is permitted.


Please Note:
Here on this stage you need to create a PIR.

9. Once the client has accepted/agreed the proposal you can proceed with making the Proposal visible in the session by selecting 'Proposal Details'


10. Under the Proposal Settings you will find the Status 'Visible in Session' select it and proceed by selecting 'Advisory Session'


11. The communication form is been displayed and you need to select the 'Order Giver' and the 'Communication Channel' for the session


12. Expand the left navigation


13. Now you can see the details about all your created Proposals for this portfolio under 'Add proposals' sections which you can easily add to your transaction list by hitting '+'


14.  Your proposed positions has been added into the Transaction basket were you can conduct the 'Suitability & Appropriateness Checks' and finalize the next steps.
