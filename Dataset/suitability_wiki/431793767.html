STEP 1: BASIC ASSESSMENT

Please ensure that there is no conflicting information on the form (e.g., a client stating ample experience in a product category but no corresponding knowledge) or conflicting information that you already have from the client/partner (e.g., a client/partner previously stating that she/he has traded many Hedge Funds but states to have no knowledge).

If the client/partner declares “good” knowledge in basic K&E categories (the ones listed below) and there is no other conflicting information, the assessment can be finished. Continue to step 3 for the documentation requirement. 

  • Money Market Instruments
  • Foreign Exchange
  • Fixed Income: Non-Investment Grade Bonds
  • Fixed Income: Investment Grade Bonds
  • Equity Instruments
  • Traditional Funds
  • Structured Products: principal protected Products
  • Derivatives: Forwards and Swaps


STEP 2: INCREASED FOCUS ASSESSMENT

For the following categories, which bear by the nature of the product a higher risk and complexity, an additional assessment must be done, if the client/partner states that she/he has “good” knowledge:

  • Fixed Income: Complex Bonds
  • Real Estate and Real Estate Investment Trusts
  • Non-Traditional Funds
  • Private Equity and Private Equity Funds
  • Structured Products: non-principal protected (Yield enhancement and participation products)
  • Structured Products: leveraged
  • Derivatives: Exchange Traded Derivatives (ETD)
  • Derivatives: OTC Derivatives
  • Derivatives: Accumulators, Deccumulators, Target accrual redemption forwards (TARFs)
  • Commodities
  • Digital Assets

The following elements can be considered for this assessment:

  1. The client’s/partner’s profession and/or education provide sufficient evidence that the knowledge level “good” is plausible. However, the client’s/partner’s actual position or former profession must include experience in investment instruments and financial markets for being a reasonable rationale. Please note that not any position in a financial institution does automatically qualify for good knowledge.
  2. The RM has served the client already in the past and knows based on previous experience that the client/partner has good knowledge.
  3. The RM asks specific questions about the risks and characteristics of financial instruments and receives adequate answers that prove the client’s/partner’s knowledge level. To support this assessment, please see sample questions you can use here.

Please indicate the according main source(s) in section 2 on the 2nd page of the K&E-form by crossing at least 1 out of the 4 boxes. 

The goal of that assessment is to verify that it is plausible that the client/partner has good knowledge. The European regulation clearly requires financial institutions to not solely rely on the client’s self-declaration.

For further information on how to complete the "For internal use only" box on the 3rd page of the K&E-form, please check the process explained here.

HOW TO PROCEED IF THE ASSESSMENT LEADS TO DOWNGRADING THE KNOWLEDGE?

If the client’s/partner’s knowledge level is not sufficient (e.g., a discussion about risks and characteristics of certain product categories shows knowhow gaps), suggest downgrading to “limited” to ensure that the client/partner receives additional warnings/education when she/he trades the respective product. In a client meeting, you can correct the form before the client/partner signs it. For distant communication the following must be done: 

  • Change the signed form and inform the client/partner about the changes and document the outcome in the contact note -> see next step 3.


STEP 3: DOCUMENTATION OF THE ASSESSMENT

Once the K&E form is complete and the client/partner has signed the form, you must document the assessment in a contact note (the basic and/or the increased focus assessment). This contact note reference must also be captured in the "For internal use only" box on the K&E-form before sending it to CDS. 
Note: Capture the u-number of the relationship manager ("RM") on the form who has performed the assessment even if the contact note is captured by the relationship manager assistant ("RMA"). This contact note reference replaces the RM signature. Forms without filled in contact note reference will be returned by CDS (for avoidance of doubt, the contact note itself must not be handed in together with the form). For CUR set-ups please check the respective operational guidelines regarding contact notes. 

Please note, also the Client Investment Profile ("CIP") form itself does no longer require the RM signature but a contact note documenting the profiling conversation and confirm completeness and correctness. Both, the CIP and the K&E documentation can be captured in one note (as it is usually one conversation), however, for the K&E part some documentation elements are required for the increased focus assessment.

Sample Questions

I have discussed and assessed the K&E declared by the client and confirm the stated K&E levels per category.

I have discussed and assessed the K&E declared by the client. The good knowledge in categories (xyz) is plausible because the client has worked as … in the financial industry for 10 years.

 

I have discussed and assessed the K&E declared by the client. The good knowledge in categories (xyz) is plausible because the client holds a CFA degree.

 

I have discussed and assessed the K&E declared by the client. The good knowledge in categories (xyz) is plausible because I had a long-term relationship with this client at (bank…) in which we traded these instruments frequently. 

 

No change K&E needed:

I have discussed and assessed the K&E declared by the client. The good knowledge in categories (xyz) is plausible. The client could sufficiently explain the characteristics of the instruments and answer questions on risks to my satisfaction.

 

Change of K&E needed

I have discussed and assessed the K&E declared by the client. However, we decided to adjust the knowledge in (xyz) from “good” to “limited” as the client does not yet feel sufficiently familiar with all risks involved. A confirmation is sent via e-mail on dd.mm.yy.

  • Example of contact note for the CIP: 

"I have discussed the Client Investor Profile with Mrs. X and confirm the consistency, correctness and completeness and validity of the stated values."
