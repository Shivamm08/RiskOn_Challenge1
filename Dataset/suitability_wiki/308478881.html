2. What does this alert do?

  • Checks upcoming maturities (triggered 5 days before the maturity)
  • Monitoring Focus: on Position
  • Triggered if an upcoming maturity is due within the next five business days

3. How do I proceed?

  • Bonds and structured products with a maturity within the next 5 business days will trigger an alert.
  • For structured products, no reinvestment ideas are provided yet.
  • For new ideas refer to the Product & Service Portal, Product Offering, section Investable Bonds.
