What is the BMO Rule Engine?

The BMO Rule Engine includes several packages, responsible for checking different aspects of a trade. 

Implemented rule packages

​​Rule package​​​​Description
​Distribution rules​Check whether a financial instrument is allowed for distribution
Tax rules​Describes the product taxation of a financial instrument (Income tax, Capital Gains Tax)​
Fund providers define certain restrictions for their share classes, which are provided by Investment Navigator and implemented in the rule framework​
​Global Policy Rule​s​Financial instrument related restrictions due to Julius Baer policies
​Service Model Compliance check​Checks what solicitation scenarios are allowed within the existing mandate / cross-border scenario​
​Returns the documents that have to be handed out to the client (pre-trade)


Using the BMO Web Front End, you can manually check the distribution, tax and service model compliance rules for any context and instrument.


You can find a comprehensive documentation of all rules here: Rules Documentation.
