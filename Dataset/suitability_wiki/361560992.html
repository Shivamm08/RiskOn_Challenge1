Life Insurance Companies, Pension Solution Providers and Fiduciary Companies

Scope

The Client Investment Profile (CIP) and the Knowledge & Experience (K&E) set the basis for suitable and consistent high-quality advice to all our clients.

They are integral and important parts of the process step ‘Your Needs’ and have to be assessed in close and personal collaboration with the client. In all cases where a life insurance company, a pension solution provider as for vested benefit accounts (‘Freizügigkeitskonti’) or a certain type of fiduciary companies like Julius Baer Fiduciaria S.r.L. (or another Italian fiduciary company) is the account holder and which mandated Julius Baer to manage assets for the benefit and according to the needs of its own client (the account’s beneficial owner), the account holder’s own personal requirements are not relevant to fulfill the statutory requirements. In such cases, the beneficial owner’s personal requirements are relevant.

Additional CIP form (CIPLIC) as tailor-made solution

In order to cover the particularity of such setups, a new CIP (CIPLIC) has been created. This CIPLIC collects the necessary information in order to be able to propose an investment strategy that matches with the beneficial owner’s risk profile and investment objectives. By signing this document, the account holder confirms that its content represents the personal requirements of the beneficial owner.

Important informtion

Please ensure that for all other types of fiduciary companies like trusts, foundations, offshore structures etc., as well as for pension funds the Client Investment Profile for legal entities (CIPLE) should be used, and NOT the CIPLIC.

CIPLIC specific attributes

The CIPLIC is an assessment of the beneficial owner's investment profile based on the information provided by the account holder (whilst the CIPLE is profiling the account holder).


1 Additional line on page 2 indicating the beneficial owner

Only in the following cases, the beneficial owner has to be stated in the second line and is allowed to sign the CIPLIC form.

  • Vested benefit accounts with a supplementary agreement with Julius Baer, or

  • Beneficial owners of the fiduciary companies with an appropriate authorization (ACFID, ACF3P or LPSTF2)

3 Who has to sign the form?

The CIPLIC must be signed by the AH or - as stated above - by the beneficial owner.


4 How many CIPLIC do I need for a business relationship (ZR)?

In a normal case, there is one beneficial owner per ZR, and therefore one CIPLIC per ZR.

Exceptions for pension solution providers as for vested benefit accounts ('Freizügigkeitskonti')

As the pension solution providers as for vested benefit accounts ('Freizügigkeitskonti') are regulated under the BVV2, these structures have restricted availability in the selection of the investment objectives and of the investment strategies. Furthermore, the pension solution providers cannot actively leverage the portfolio.

a) The investment objective 'Dynamic growth' and the 'Cash flow generation' can NOT be selected.
b) The investment strategy 'Growth' and 'Capital gain' can NOT be selected.

Correct labelling of the business relation in CRM

The fields ‘Legal Type’ and ‘Special form’ of the legal entity of the ZR have to be correctly labelled in CRM as a pre-requisite to trigger the correct CIP document. For this, the accordant designation (such as ‘Life Insurance’, ‘Pension Solution Provider’ or ‘Static Fiduciary’) must be set in the relevant fields as illustrated on pages 3 and 4. The appropriate data can be captured by the responsible RM/RMA. Certain situations - such as open workflows of changes in the system - do not allow modifications such as in the field ‘Special forms’ on partner level. If this occurs, the responsible unit of Client Documentation Services may be contacted.
