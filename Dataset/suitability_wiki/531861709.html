To ensure compliance with recording obligations, use of Webex for client meetings is only allowed when call in option is used by the bank employee. To activate recording of the Webex conference, you need to proceed as follows:

  1. Switch from Computer audio to Call-in
2. Choose the call-in number appropriate for your location:  3. Dial in via the number for your location, followed by the access codes, when prompted by the Webex application:


As always, after the meeting please remember to store a Client Contact Note in TAP, following the usual 5 W rule:

  • Who: involved parties, both external and internal
  • What and why: decision, discussed content, who initiated the contact
  • Where and when: communication channel (for Webex: Internet), date and time

If you're not sure your Webex meeting got recorded correctly, feel free to reach out to Compliance or FRM to check.

If for any reason recording was not possible, please notify Compliance proactively and make sure that contact note from the meeting includes all applicable information indicated in point 7 of LUX-1106-00 Recording of Telephone and other Electronic Conversations within the European Economic Area.
