I. Background & Scope

In Italy, there is the regulatory obligation to use subscription forms provided by fund companies in cases when the following conditions are met:

      • Client category: retail and elective professional clients
      • Solicitation scenario: any
      • Type of transaction: FIRST buy, subscription
      • Type of financial instruments: foreign UCITs funds

III. Pre-trade package - Where to find all the documents?


A. Pre-trade cost disclosure & PRIIP KID

The pre-trade cost disclosure will be generated in Wealth Navigator directly in the session as well as the PRIIP KID will be available as part of the documents list in the Wealth Navigator session. Please download them from there. 

B. Fund subscription form

Please download the fund subscription form from FundInfo website - https://www.fundinfo.com/, by following the next steps:

1. In the search bar of FundInfo website type the ISIN of the foreign mutual fund and click "ENTER":


2. Then click on the "ADDITIONAL DOCUMENTS" tab:


3. You will find the fund subscription form under "SUB" tab.



4. You can then download the fund subscription form and provide it to the client as part of the pre-trade package.
