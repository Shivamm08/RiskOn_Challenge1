Lombard loan costs consist of interest payments and early termination fees (if applicable). They have been so far counted as negative "Income" and therefore considered in the “Return before costs” (but not explicitly stated as "Costs").

Starting with the reporting period 2023, Lombard Loan Costs - which qualify as MiFID ancillary services - are displayed explicitly as cost bookings in the Annual Cost & Charges Summary and in the Itemized Breakdown Report. For this purpose a new group of costs, namely "Lombard loan related costs", has been introduced.

Special case: non-accounted loans

In rare cases Lombard loans are set up extra-accounted. That means that cash and loan are not booked in the same portfolio when setting them up in OTMS (“Leverage” = No). As these costs are not included in P/L of a client, Lombard loans with this set-up are shown as pro-memoria positions in the Annual Cost & Charges Summary and Itemized Breakdown Report (underneath the accounted costs).
