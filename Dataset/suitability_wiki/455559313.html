1. WHAT ARE THE CLIENT CLASSIFICATIONS UNDER MiFID?

Under MiFID, banks are obliged to classify their clients (domiciled in the EEA only) according to precisely defined legal criteria into three categories:

  • Retail Clients
  • Professional Clients
  • Eligible Counterparties

These classifications are designed to ensure that our clients are treated in a manner that corresponds to their knowledge and experience of financial instruments and is in accordance with the type, frequency and volume of the transactions involved. All new clients will be informed about their classification. Existing clients will be informed only if their existing classification changes.

Attention: The Bank must strictly refrain from incentivising, inducing or pressuring a Retail Client to request to be treated as an MIFID Elected Professional.

RETAIL CLIENTS

Retail Clients are afforded the most regulatory protection regarding their investment possibilities and information provided to them. The Bank decided to consider all PB clients as ‘Retail Clients’ in terms of MiFID unless they choose to opt-up to the “Professional Client” status.

Regulatory protection afforded to Retail Clients includes:

  • All pre-trade checks

  • Full cost transparency

  • Suitability report about risks and advice given on instrument level

  • 10% loss notification

PROFESSIONAL CLIENTS

The level of protection for Professional Clients is lower than for Retail Clients. They are considered to have sufficient knowledge and experience to make their own investment decisions and to appropriately assess the associated risks. MiFID distinguishes between two types of Professional Clients: (i) Elected Professionals and (ii) Per Se Professionals.

Main differences to the afforded regulatory protection for Retail Clients:

  • Fewer limitations on advice

  • Less documentation to receive (e.g. suitability report)

  • Broader investment universe


i) ELECTED PROFESSIONALii) PER SE PROFESSIONAL 

A request for a re-classification to an Elected Professional must always be initiated by the client and must be done in writing. Relationship managers are forbidden from promoting opting-up.

To classify a client as an Elected Professional, the client must meet two of the following three criteria1:

  • Over the course of a year ,the client has carried out on average ten transactions of a substantial size per calendar quarter
  • Portfolio value exceeds EUR 500'0001
  • Client has worked in or has experience in the financial sector for over one year

1 The (second) criterium as for the portfolio value to exceed EUR 500 000 must always be fulfilled. For joint accounts/collective accounts, each Account Holder needs to have bankable assets of EUR 500’000.

Per Se Professionals are classified by the relationship manager. The client has at any time the option to apply for a re-classification to the higher level of protection.

One of the following criteria must be met:

  • An entity required to be authorised or regulated to operate in the financial markets by virtue of being a credit institution, an investment firm, an insurance company, a collective investment scheme or the management company of such a scheme, a pension fund or the management company of a pension fund, a commodity or commodity derivatives dealer, a local or another authorised or regulated financial institution.

  • A large undertaking meeting two of the following size requirements on an entity base:

    • a balance sheet total of EUR 20'000'000 or more,

    • net turnover of EUR 40'000'000 or more,

    • own funds of EUR 2'000'000 or more.

  • A national or regional government, a public body that manages public debt, a central bank, an international institution or another similar international organisation.

  • Another institutional investor whose main activity is to invest in financial instruments, including entities dedicated to the securitisation of assets or other financing transactions.

ELIGIBLE COUNTERPARTIES

This category is reserved for legal entities subject to authorisation or supervision, large companies, governments, central banks and international or supranational organisations. They are offered the lowest level of protection. These clients are considered to be the most mature investors and are presumed to be able to assess and manage the risks involved in their investment advice obtained.

Eligible Counterparties are classified by the relationship manager. The client has at any time the option to apply for a re-classification as a Retail Client or Per Se Professional as defined under MiFID.

One of the following criteria must be met:

  • Investment firms,
  • Credit institution,
  • Insurance companies,
  • UCITS and their management companies,
  • Pension funds and their management companies,
  • other financial institutions authorised or regulated under Union law or under the national law of a Member State,
  • National governments and their corresponding offices including public bodies that deal with public debt at national level,
  • Central banks,
  • Supranational organisations.

2. WHAT ARE THE CRITERIA FOR RE-CLASSIFICATIONS?

Please bear in mind that the approver can only review the requests on the basis of current documentation. Therefore, please ensure up-to-date information in CRM, CLM, KYC (amongst others). This will contribute to an efficient processing.

a) MiFID Elected Professional

Two out of three criteria must be fulfilled. The second criterium (asset requirement) always must be fulfilled.

CriteriaRequired information
  1. Average of ten transactions

Consequently, the K&E profile must reflect the respective trading activity. Therefore:

  • The K&E profile must reflect good knowledge in the vast majority of asset classes (i.e. 11 or more).
  • The K&E profile must reflect the experience (i.e. more than 5 trades) in the vast majority of asset classes (i.e. 11 or more), OR the section Source of knowledge/experience (below K&E grid) must reflect the trading experience.

If the client does not have a sufficient K&E profile, please follow this guidance.

2. Bankable assets of EUR 500'000 (must always be fulfilled)
  • If the asset requirement is not fulfilled with in-house bankable assets, ensure that the asset breakdown is reflected in CLM Wealth Profile.

Please bear in mind that i) only assets where the client is Account Holder can be considered, ii) for joint accounts, each Account Holder must individually fulfill the requirement, iii) amongst others, pension and real estate assets cannot be considered.

3. Professional background in the financial sector

Consequently, the K&E profile and the KYC report must reflect the experience. Therefore:

  • The K&E profile must reflect good knowledge in the vast majority of asset classes (i.e. 11 or more)
  • The section Source of knowledge/experience (below K&E grid) must reflect the professional experience as well.
  • The KYC report must reflect the professional experience in the financial sector.

If the client does not have a sufficient K&E profile, please follow this guidance.

Elected Professionals, Per Se Professionals, Eligible Counterparties can apply at any time for re-classification to the higher level of protection available. If any of these clients wish to be re-classified as MiFID Retail Client or if the client does not meet the requirements anymore ("Change in Circumstance" such as divorce), the reclassification can be started by signing the form (Form ID CH-01690). Further information can be found here: CH-5031-00 Client Classification.

For bank-initiated downgradings as a consequence of the regular client classification review, please consult FAQ14.
