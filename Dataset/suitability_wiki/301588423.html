Introduction

Within the Suitability Framework, the Client Investment Profile (CIP) is the starting point for tailored advice and individual investment solutions. It is important to understand what is unique about the client’s personal financial situation. The aim of the CIP is to get a full picture of the client’s investment needs as well as the client’s service expectations. Having our clients profiled in a structured approach is key and supports the transformation of client satisfaction into loyalty and advocacy.

The CIP contains any investment related information in order to determine the clients’ investment objectives, financial risk ability, risk tolerance and knowledge and experience in product categories. In locations where the framework for Sustainable Finance is rolled out, also ESG-Preferences can be selected. As a result, the corresponding investment strategy is determined.

Therefore, the CIP needs to be assessed in close and personal collaboration together with the client (sole mailing of the forms for completion by the client only is not deemed as appropriate) and re-confirmed or re-assessed - depending on local regulation - every two years (CIP Renewal).

Scope

For the scope triggering in CLM, please visit: Service scope for CIP and K&E - CLM User Guide - Confluence. Further detailed information on client constellations can be found below:

All clients have to be profiled, except:

  • Intermediary end-clients: such as end-clients of External Financial Advisers (EFA) or External Asset Managers (EAM) - whereby it is necessary to document the knowledge & experience of the EFA end-client by completing the form 'Knowledge & Experience'

  • Global custody clients

  • Trade Basic (formerly Execution Only) clients – whereby it is necessary to document the knowledge and experience of the client by completing the form ‘Knowledge and Experience’

  • Non-Trading-Account (NTA) clients

Important: If you are of the opinion that a 'CIP data required-task' should not be in place or there should be a task but you do not have one in your CLM task manager, please raise an IT-Ticket for CLM. If you want to learn more about the CIP data required-task, please visit: Task Categories - CLM User Guide - Confluence.

Applicability 

There are different versions available for each of the JB Booking Centers and RML. Please use the one which is applicable to your constellation according to CLM/CRM, CRM AOF or any other local inventory.

Many ZRs – many CIPs?

For ZR's with identical account holder constellation, one single CIP can be completed. Otherwise, each ZR requires one CIP.
