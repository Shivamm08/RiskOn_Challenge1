This guidance applies to all controls executed by the Monitoring & Control Execution (MCE) team.

Key principles for remediating deficiencies

General Remarks

  1. Deficiencies are always raised to the RM/IA of the BR. In the exceptional case that another RM conducted the session on your behalf, you may contact the task creator to reassign the detected deficiency.
    Note: If the session was conducted by the A(R)M, the RM remains accountable for any identified deficiencies or gaps in documentation.
  2. Generally, you are granted two weeks for the task completion.
  3. If - on an exceptional basis - you require an extension of the due date (e.g., due to absence), please contact the task creator in advance.
  4. Remediate deficiencies as described under ‘Control Remarks’ in the CLM task and complete the task only once all required remediation steps have been properly finalized.
  5. Monitor open tasks regularly.

Severity levels are final and non-negotiable

  1. The severity of the deficiencies (i.e. minor, moderate and major) is not negotiable. These are governed by established guidelines and have been approved by relevant committees. 

Communicate exclusively in CLM Task comments

  1. Provide any feedback on tasks directly in the CLM Task comment section. This ensures full traceability and compliance. Please refrain from reaching out to the control performer via phone, chat and/or e-mail. Furthermore, please do not contact other MCE team members to re-evaluate the control results.
  2. Any relevant documentation/attachment must be archived under the corresponding ZR and referred to accordingly in the comment.
  3. Evidence criteria:
    • If new evidence shows that required information was already available in official bank systems at the time of control, MCE will reassess the finding and adjust the assessment accordingly.
    • Archiving pre-existing documentation (created before the task) after the deficiency is raised assessment will result in reclassification as a “minor defect" - provided the content fully resolves the issue.
    • Creating new records ex-post (e.g., adding a contact note after detection) - even though required - does not reduce the severity of the original deficiency.
    • Providing no new or relevant input in a second feedback round will lead MCE to close the case without further notice. Any feedback must be fact-based and properly justified.

Task closure

  1. Tasks without interaction or updated comments within one month, will be automatically closed by MCE - unless client remediation is pending.
  2. MCE reserves the right to close the task once it is evident that remediation has been completed and no further corrective actions are feasible, even after the first feedback - unless remediation with the client is needed.
  3. Once a task is marked as ‘closed’, it is finalized. Challenges of Scorecard relevant evaluations can only be initiated via the official Challenge Process.
