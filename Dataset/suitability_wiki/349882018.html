Short explanation of the calculation:

With the Global Norms Score, it is safeguarded that corporate issuers operate in accordance with globally recognized norms around human rights, labour rights and responsible business practice. If a corporate issuer breaches these globally accepted norms around human rights, the issue will be considered an ESG Risk. Moreover, the Global Norms scores considers the involvement of a corporate issuer in weapons, both conventional and controversial, where any involvement in controversial weapons leads to a classification of ESG Risk.
