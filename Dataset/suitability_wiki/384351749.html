KIDs for PRIIPs

https://wiki.juliusbaer.com/spaces/fkh/pages/384351749/KIDs+for+PRIIPs

1. Introduction & Application

 

  • What is a KID for PRIIPS?
    • A 'Key Information Document' ('KID') is a 3 page information document, explaining the risks, cost and performance scenarios of a particular product in a standardized format. 
    • The KID needs to be produced for financial instruments classified as Packaged Retail Investment and Insurance Products ('PRIIPS').
    • Please note that this page governs the rules implemented for the KID's and not the special prospectus rule applied for non-traditional-funds (NTF's).
  • What is a PRIIP?
    • A Packaged Retail Investment and Insurance Product ("PRIIPS") includes financial instruments with an underlying, such as funds, derivatives, structured products, bonds with a derivative component (such as convertible and CoCo bonds). 
  • What clients are in scope?
    • FINSA clients: If investment advice is provided to a FINSA private client, a KID must be provided pre-trade.
    • MiFID clients (EEA/UK domiciled):
      • MiFID retail clients: A PRIIPS-KID need to be provided pre-trade for advised and unsolicited transactions.
      • MiFID professionals: A PRIIPS-KID needs to be provided pre-trade for advised transactions only, if pre-trade cost disclosure is not complete. If KID is not available, however costs are properly disclosed, see further guidance on . 
    • For clients domiciled in Germany a PIB ("Produktinformationsblatt") has to be provided for equities and bonds (i.e. plain vanilla bonds) in case the client is advised to purchase such products. 
  • When do I need to send a KID?
    • See KID distribution rules below ->
    • Only for "buy" transactions you have to provide the client with a KID pre-trade.
    • If a financial instrument does not have a KID (e.g. US ETF's) EU retail clients may not invest in this product, even if the transaction is unsolicited. The PRIIPs Regulation does exclude EU resident retail clients from investments in such products.
    • The following bank internal rule applies: NO KID → NO TRADE!
  • Which KID version needs to be sent in which constellation?
    • See KID distribution rules below →
  • Is there an equivalent document to the KID?
    • No, there is no equivalent document to the KID, e.g. prospectus, factsheets and term sheets are not equivalent and cannot be handed out instead of a KID. 
  • What if the client wants to invest into an NTF? 
    • If a client wishes to invest into an NTF, the relevant product information, incl. the associated elevated risks, must be provided. Accordingly, the RM must upload at least a prospectus (incl. supplement, if available) to Wealth Navigator. The terms “private placement memorandum” or “offering memorandum” are typically used as synonyms for “prospectus” and are equivalent. In the case of an unsolicited NTF transaction, if a prospectus is not available from public sources (e.g. Morningstar, Fundinfo, etc.) or cannot otherwise be obtained with reasonable effort, the provision of alternative documents such as (i) the KID, (ii) the Annual or Semi-Annual Report and (iii) the Fund Factsheet, is considered sufficient.
  • Are there cases for which a generic KID can be provided?
    • Yes, for  exchange traded derivatives ('ETDs') a generic KID (made available by the respective exchange) must be provided.
    • Links to exchanges are available here.
    • Based on the fact that generic KID's are static and therefore rarely updated, the KID can be provided once a year, provided the client agrees to it. The agreement must be documented in a qualified contact note. The alert can then be closed with the justification "Document already provided" with an explanation in the mandatory free text field when and via which channel the generic KID has been provided to the . 
    • Note: Generic KID's must not be used for Structured Products!
  • What happens if the distribution fee is hidden (untoggled) in the Derivatives Toolbox (DTB)?
    • Only a Term Sheet will be generated and no KID will be available.
    • Please note that you may not proceed in cases, for which the provision of a KID is required. See KID distribution rules below -> . 
  • Are KID's provided in e-banking?
    • Yes, since January 18th, 2021 KIDs are provided pre-trade in e-banking for all RM locations EEA or in case the account holder domicile is in the EEA.

How it looks like in e-Banking


The KID is displayed and can be downloaded by the client.If we do not have a disclosure document for a certain  trade, the client will be blocked in e-banking to proceed with the trade. Please note, for US ETF no KID is available, hence MIFID retail clients will be blocked from executing such a trade. For more information on the BMO and MDDA rules on e-banking consult the FAQ.

3. Alert Handling

  • How to proceed if the product information document (i.e. KID, Prospectus, Factsheet, ...) is not automatically available in Wealth Navigator (MDDA alert is triggered) or in a case of a WN outage?
    • If the Mandatory Disclosure Document Alert (MDDA) is triggered, or WN is technically not available, the product information documents can be created or retrieved in different tools (further info can be found here). 
    • In case an MDDA alert is triggered, the latest version of the product information document must be uploaded in WN manually. If the KID is not available, the trade cannot be executed (no KID no trade). Note that you should not resolve the alert by clicking "Document already provided" if the product information has not already been delivered to the client. Further information can be found under alert handling → link
    • In the event of a WN outage, the product information document must be sent manually to the client. A copy of the same document must be archived and documented accordingly in the contact note.

4. Special Casesspecialcases

4.1. Handling of PRIIPS-KID MDDA alert for EEA/UK Professional clients for advised (actively and reverse solicited) transaction

  1. Check pre-trade cost disclosure document, if product inherent costs are available (i.e. both upfront charge and issuer charge).
  2. → if yes: close MDDA with "document already provided" and write a detailed description in the alert justification, that any subsequent control can understand that full cost disclosure has been provided, e.g.: "MiFID Professional client. No KID is available but verified, that the cost disclosure is complete (transaction costs 1.35% and ongoing costs 0.16% indicative). Cost disclosure distributed pre trade to client via e-banking."
  3. →  if no:
    1. Check with Markets if required cost information can be uploaded in the respective Markets systems so that pre-trade cost disclosure can be produced in Wealth Navigator. Once done, reopen the Wealth Navigator session and check pre-trade cost disclosure document, if product inherent costs are available.
    2. If Markets is not in the position to specify the costs for the pre-trade cost disclosure, your are not able to proceed → no pre-trade cost disclosure no trade. 

4.2. Handling of UK KID MDDA alert for UK Professional clients in active and reverse solicited transactions

There is no UK regulatory requirement which would prescribe provision of a UK KID to professional clients. 

However, product inherent costs need to be disclosed pre-trade. Wealth Navigator is currently requiring a UK KID to ensure proper disclosure of product inherent costs (backup in case pre-trade cost disclosure could not have been rendered). As EEA PRIIPs KID can be used as an equivalent document to the UK KID (for professional clients only), cost disclosure can also be ensured by distributing an EEA-PRIIPs-KID.

How to proceed?

In case an MDDA is triggered, which requires a UK KID, and a client is a UK-domiciled professional client*, you are allowed to upload:

  1. an EEA PRIIPS KID (check in PSP → link)
  2. for ETDs a generic KID (link can be found on suitability wiki → link)

* Professional Flag must be visible in the respective systems (CRM/Wealth Navigator or other relevant system in the RM location). Clients still in the opting-up process are not considered professional clients until approval has been received. Further info can be found here: 


4.3. UK KID for UK Retail client in ETD transactions

It is a regulatory requirement to provide UK-domiciled retail client with an UK KID for PRIIPS, including for Exchange-Traded Derivatives (ETDs). It has been observed however that the UK KID is generally not produced by the respective stock exchanges (subject to three exceptions – see below). In order to be able to sell ETDs to UK retail clients when UK KID is not available, the Generic KID and an additional "UK Supplement" is required (UK Supplement was prepared by JBI and it covers the delta between EEA KID and UK KID. This is why the use of the UK Supplement in combination with the EEA KID was considered, on a risk-based approach, an equivalent to the UK KID itself).

When MDDA alert is triggered which requires UK KID for UK retail client, you can proceed as follows:

4.4 Exception to pre-trade provision of KID and pre-trade cost disclosure document for EEA/UK clients for unsolicited transactions

In general, all regulatory documents must be timely provided to the client/order giver. When a pre-trade provision of KID and cost disclosure document is not possible, the RM can proceed with a trade only on an exceptional basis, provided that all conditions below are cumulatively fulfilled AND documented accordingly in the contact note of the advisory session immediately after the advisory session.

General conditions:

  1. Client has no electronic communication channel and has an approved e-banking exception.
  2. No advice is provided – only unsolicited transactions are allowed.
  3. Client explicitly consents to receiving the regulatory document(s) after the transaction has been executed. RM documents the consent.
  4. RM documents regulatory document(s) delivery to the client immediately after the trade entry (attach evidence of the documents provided and the used communication channel)

Additional conditions for KID:

  1. RM has confirmed that a valid KID exists. If no KID exists at all (and none planned), the product must not be sold to retail investors.
  2. RM clearly informs client that (i) KID cannot be timely provided, (ii) client has the right to delay the transaction until the review of the regulatory document(s) and (iii) about the risks of deciding without full documentation

Additional conditions for pre-trade cost disclosure:

  1. For MiFID clients, timely cost transparency remains mandatory. RM verbally explains the costs associated with the transaction prior to execution (e.g., one-off charges, ongoing charges, performance-based fees etc.) and documents it accordingly.

Example of CCN: Client called to execute investment in XYZ (ISIN: AB123456789) without any advice and underlined urgent market timing. Client has no electronic communication channel available and an approved e-banking exception on file. Verified that a valid KID exists and informed client that transaction should be delayed in order to receive and read the KID before the trade is placed. Client explicitly consented to receive KID after trade. I underlined the risks of deciding on a trade without full documentation. Verbal cost disclosure completed: Purchase fee: 0.15%, Ongoing Charges (TER): 0.30% p.a., No performance or exit fees. Trade executed. KID and pre-trade cost disclosure dispatched same-day via Swiss Post; evidence saved in Ondemand.
