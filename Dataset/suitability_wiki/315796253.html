A. Scope

The Knowledge & Experience form ("K&E form") and the Client Investment Profile ("CIP") set the basis for the provision of suitable and consistent high-quality investment advice and discretionary portfolio management to our clients.

They contain information on the knowledge and experience of the order giver as well as on the investment objectives and financial situation of the client that BJBE must use to test and ensure suitability prior to providing these investment services.

In addition, the CIP comprises also the CIP Side Form (1-1293) for ESG additional preferences, as explained within this section.

MiFID2 requires that such information remains accurate and up to date, which is why the CIP and CIP Side Form have to be renewed every 2 years.

B. Step-by-step guide - Renewal Process


Step 1. Tracking the validity of the CIP

90 days before the expiration date of the CIP and the CIP Side Form, the Relationship-Manager ("RM") is notified with the quarterly "Missing Documents List" XLS that these are going to expire soon. When the CIP and Side Form are mentioned for the first time in the "Missing Documents List" XLS, this triggers the task for the RM to renew the CIP. As of November 2024, a CIP Renewal Robot will also run a daily job to identify whether a certain CIP will expire in 60 days (i.e. please see Step 2 below for more details).

Until it is successfully renewed, the CIP and CIP Side Form will remain on the "Missing Documents List" XLS with:

  • DOC STATUS = “WILL EXPIRE SOON” + "MISSING SINCE XX DATE = (Expiration date)", or
  • DOC STATUS = “EXPIRED” + "MISSING SINCE XX DATE = (number of days overdue)".

At the deadline, i.e. if the CIP becomes expired, the task is escalated to the Team Head of the RM.

The date of renewal of the CIP and the CIP Side Form is the same, i.e. once a CIP is about to expire, both the CIP and the CIP Side Form will appear in the missing documents list and are to be renewed at the same time.

Step 2: RM reaches out to the client*

As aforementioned, as of November 2024, there will be a CIP Renewal robot performing the below tasks for the RM:
- tracking the validity of the CIP (i.e. see Step 1);
- preparing the CIP Renewal letter (i.e. see Step 2A);
- preparing the client-ready e-mail (i.e. see Step 2B); and 
- renewing the CIP in TAP (i.e. see Step 3.1).

That means that you might see some cases in the "Missing Documents List" for which the letter will only be prepared 30 days later.

*N.B! The CIP renewal should be always aligned with the account holder in case of individual and joint accounts and with the authorized representatives in the case of legal entity accounts.


Upon detecting some CIPs expiring in 60 days, the robot will:

Step 2A. Customise the CIP renewal letter template (with all information of the existing, soon-to-be-expired CIP that BJBE currently has on file) and will prepare a standard client-ready email draft with the client details and contacts.

Step 2B. Send the CIP Renewal client-ready e-mail(s) to the RM with the CIP Renewal letter(s) attached for all impacted clients. Please note: this means that the RM and RMAs are no longer required to prepare the CIP Renewal letter and client e-mail by themselves even if the case still appears in the mentioned list. The preparation will be done by a robot.

This client-ready email will be sent to RM and the team only once and will include one or more attachments, depending on the number of clients whose CIPs are expiring in 60 days.

Once the RM and the team receive the client-ready email, the RM should reach out to each client renewing the CIP by following the below steps:



Step 2C. Open each and every attachment of the e-mail mentioned in Step 2B (see below more details on how each client-ready email looks like).



Step 2D. Confirm that client contact details are correct.

Step 2E. Perform a 4-eyes check of the client-ready email and CIP renewal letter prepared by the robot: the RM reviews the existing, soon-to-be-expired CIP information on file provided by the robot in the CIP Renewal Letter by considering the following questions:

  • Does the client have different "investment objectives"?
  • Have there been any changes in the client's personal circumstances that may require an adaptation in the "overall risk tolerance" or "overall financial risk ability" scores?
  • Is there a mismatch between the "investment strategy" agreed on in the CIP and the mandate strategy(ies) of the client?
  • (In case the client has never provided ESG information in the CIP and/or the CIP Side Form)Has the client mentioned preferences for sustainable investments in the past? 

If all of the above questions can be answered with "no", the RM can go ahead with Steps 2F and 2G below:

Step 2F. Include the robot in “Bcc” (RPABmLuxembourg1@juliusbaer.com) of the e-mail to be sent to the client, in case the RM wishes that the CIP is automatically renewed in TAP, with the automatic creation of a contact note (i.e see Step 3.2 below).

Step 2G. Click on “send”.


By doing this, the RM is contacting the affected clients on the "Missing Documents List" XLS before the CIP and the CIP Side Form expire and informing them about the information which is currently reflected in his/her/its existing, soon-to-be-expired CIP and CIP Side Form, following the regulatory obligation.

Two important notes: 

1. For clients who only allow physical dispatch as communication channel, the robot will still prepare the letter and will inform the RM via email.

Step 3.1: CIP validity gets extended 



Once Steps 2F and 2G are done, the robot will renew the existing CIP by pushing the renewal button in TAP . The robot will also perform Step 3.2 (documentation) (please see below). 

For the cases where the client is contacted via physical letter and not via email, the RM can follow the same process after sending the letter to the client and send an email to the robot so that it automatically performs Step 3.1 (i.e. automatic renewal of the CIP in TAP).

The CIP is then automatically validated and published without changes.

In relation to ESG, clients that have never provided any preferences will be classified as “Traditional Investors” and as having no preference for “Minimum proportions of alignment with SFDR and EU Taxonomy”. By clicking on the CIP renewal button in TAP, the new ESG preferences will be automatically populated with existing preferences.

Step 3.2: RM updates client contact

As aforementioned in Steps 2F and 3.1, if included in “bcc” of the email sent to the client, the robot will automatically renew the CIP in TAP on behalf of the RM, which includes the creation of a contact note.



In order to be fully compliant, the robot will also edit the contact note by attaching the e-mail the RM sent to the client. Therefore, it is extremely important that the RMs does not forget to include the robot e-mail address in "bcc".

Step 4: Client reacts

The client reacts within 30 days and communicates to the RM that his/her/its situation has changed. A new version of both the CIP and the CIP Side Form must be completed and signed by the client. The RM ensures the CIP and the CIP Side Form are consistent and of high quality with the dedicated checklist found here. The newly completed documents are handled like new documents and overwrite the existing CIP and CIP Side Form. The RM submits these to CDS, which ensure these are complete with the dedicated checklist found here, enters and publishes the data it contains on TAP, and archives both in FIO.

Note: When it comes to the CIP Side Form, not all clients are eligible to select these additional ESG preferences. Please refer to this Suitability Wiki page or get in touch with the Investment Advisory / Portfolio Management teams to find more details.

Step 5: RM documents client reaction

If the client has reacted and provided feedback, the RM documents it in a TAP contact note based on the channel of communication via which the CIP renewal was discussed and agreed upon with the client:

  • If via email, by attaching the email exchange with the client;
  • If via phone call, by indicating the details needed to easily locate the phone call with the client: (i) who at BJBE called which account participant, (ii) from the RM’s perspective, whether it was a JB landline or JB cell phone line call, and (iii) the date and time of the phone call; and
  • If via face-to-face meeting, a description of the discussion.
