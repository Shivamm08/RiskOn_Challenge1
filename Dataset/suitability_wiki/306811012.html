2. Description of the “Manufacturer Target Market” and “Negative Manufacturer Target Market” checks and alerts

The “Target Market test” consists in:

  • Identifying whether the client in question is part of the positive, out of positive or even negative target market of the product, either as defined by the manufacturer and/or internally by the bank.
  • Doing the “Manufacturer Target Market”, “Negative Manufacturer Target Market” and “Distributor Target Market” checks.

1. The “Manufacturer Target Market” (‘MTM’) check: product data with the manufacturer’s definition of the positive target market is compared against client data to determine whether the client is part of the positive or out of positive target market, as per this MTM matrix.

=> When the client is found to be outside the positive manufacturer target market, DiAS triggers an “Outside Positive Manufacturer Target Market” alert.

2. The “Negative Manufacturer Target Market” (‘-MTM’) check: product data with the manufacturer’s definition of the negative target market is compared against client data to determine whether the client is part of the negative target market, as per this -MTM matrix.

=> When the client is found in the negative manufacturer target market, DiAS triggers an “Negative Manufacturer Target Market” alert.

3. The “Distributor Target Market” (‘DTM’) check: based on the asset class that the product has been recorded in the internal systems as belonging to as well as on client data, the client is determined to be either in the positive or out of positive target market, as per this DTM matrix (EEA) or this DTM matrix (CH).

In the following sections, you can find which client data (i.e. categories) are evaluated as part of the MTM and -MTM checks.

4     What are the potential outcomes of the Target Market Assessment?

For each and every of the above categories, a dedicated MTM check and -MTM check is performed.

The possible check results are:

  • OK
  • Soft Block (“S”), which means that the client is found to be outside the positive manufacturer target market (in the case of the MTM check) or in the negative manufacturer target market (in the case of the -MTM check)
  • Hard Block (“H”), which means that the client is found to be outside the positive manufacturer target market (in the case of the MTM check) or in the negative manufacturer target market (in the case of the -MTM check) and, unless an underlying data issue, the distribution shall not go ahead
  • N/A, which means that there is missing data, so the check is not performed


5     How can an “Outside Positive Manufacturer Target Market” or “Negative Manufacturer Target Market” alert be resolved?

If the overall result of the check is “Soft Block”, proceeding is possible by selecting one of the following justification options:

  • Diversification
  • Hedging
  • Client’s sophistication
  • Client’s financial situation
  • Local market differences
  • Data Exception

In case of a “Hard Block”, the only possibility to proceed is to choose “Data Exception”.

Please be careful when selecting a justification option and do this only in case it is appropriate.
