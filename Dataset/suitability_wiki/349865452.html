Sustainability filters

As of May 23rd 2022, a new Sustainability filter has been added to PSP product search. Currently the filters can be used to narrow down the search results, but the individual parameters are not shown on product details page, except for the MSCI ESG rating and MSCI controversy flag.

The Sustainability filter has several sub-categories, which are:

  • JB ESG Category (values: Sustainable Investment, Responsible Investment, Traditional Investment, ESG Risk* and None)
  • JB ESG Scores: Climate Score, Natural Capital Score, Human Capital Score, Value Score and Governance score, Global Norms Score
  • MSCI Data: MSCI ESG Rating (AAA-CCC) and MSCI Controversy Flag (green, yellow, orange, red)
  • Sustainable Finance Disclosure Regulation (SFDR) alignment, JB view: Yes, No and None
  • EU Taxonomy: EU Taxonomy Alignment (Yes, No, None) and % EU Taxonomy Alignment (several percentage buckets to choose from)

*As a reminder, instruments categorised as ESG Risk have been excluded from the Offered universe. Those instruments which were previously either Offered or Monitored now have universe status No Bank View. This means that they cannot be advised, not even by request from a client. Only factual information may be provided.

The Sustainability filter can be accessed under "Common Navigators" on the top left of the search screen:


The individual filters can be used to narrow down the search results. Please note that several Sustainability filters can be used at the same time (e.g. ESG Category & JB ESG Scores), but the individual selections within the Sustainability filter must be made before clicking "Apply Filters".

  • JB ESG category: Sustainable Investment
  • JB Value Score: 0
  • JB Research rating: Buy


On the product page, the sustainability rating of the instrument is expressed with the MSCI ESG Rating and MSCI controversy flag. However, please note that these ratings do not reflect the results of the JB Sustainable Investment Rating Methodology; MSCI ratings are used as input for ESG classification, but represent only a part of the overall process.

If you would need instrument-level information on the ESG categorisation, or the JB Scores for an instrument , please refer to the ESG instrument data excel. This excel file acts as a supplement to PSP, until all PSP functionalities have been implemented.


ESG Data in PSP

A new ESG tab in PSP replaces the Excel file from the Sustainable Finance Intranet page. With this implementation Front employees are enabled to obtain specific ESG Data directly in the detail pages on PSP.
The following ESG data is available in PSP for Equities, Bonds, Funds and in-scope Structured Products & Derivatives:

  •  JB ESG Category
  •  JB ESG Scores (not available for Structured Products & Derivatives)
  •  ESG Raw Data (MSCI)
  •  EU Action Plan Data 
  • Data on Principal Adverse Impacts (PAIs) (see details below)


Principal Adverse Impact (PAI) Indicator Table:

PAI Indicators are a set of ESG-related indicators as defined by the Sustainable Finance Disclosure Regulation (SFDR), that cover Greenhouse Gas Emissions, Biodiversity, Water, Waste as well Social & Employee Matters.
PAI Indicators help to understand the impact of investments on the above stated ESG areas, and enable comparability as companies need to report these data points in the same way
PAI Indicators are also integrated into the JB ESG Methodology via JB Theme Scores. The JB Theme Score for which each PAI Indicators serves as an input, is indicated in the column on the very right of the table.
