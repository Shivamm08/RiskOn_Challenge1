Strengthening conduct at point of sale

Ongoing observations have underscored the need for a more comprehensive and standardized approach to implementing the Advisory KRI Scorecard concept across all Booking Centers (BCs) and RM Locations (RMLs). This will strengthen our risk management framework for the advisory process. By rolling out a global Advisory KRI Scorecard methodology, we can expect several benefits, including:

  • Improved effectiveness of risk assessments
  • Enhanced steering of advisory conduct behaviour
  • Fairer and more equal evaluation of RMs across locations
  • Better identification of areas for improvement and development of targeted mitigation strategies

Global Advisory KRI Scorecard Concept

In an effort to streamline and standardise risk management practices across the organization, the Advisory Key Risk Indicator (KRI) Scorecard is based on two globally applicable components. These revised components aim to ensure compliance with regulatory requirements and adherence to contractual promises.

  • Component 1: Client Suitability (CSU) Key Control Results
    This component assesses the monthly performance of Relationship Managers (RMs) in ensuring client suitability at the point of sale. By evaluating Client Suitability (CSU) key control results, the organization can verify its compliance with relevant regulations governing the advisory process.
  • Component 2: Overdue Risk Notifications (ORN)
    This component tracks instances where DiAS alerts are not justified within a defined working period. The purpose of this metric is to monitor adherence to contractual promises and policies, ensuring that the organisation upholds its commitments to clients.


Location-Specific Components
While the two core components will remain consistent globally, there may be instances where additional location-specific components are introduced on an exceptional basis or to address specific local regulatory requirements. These supplementary components will be carefully evaluated and implemented only when necessary to ensure ongoing compliance and risk management excellence.

Component 1 | CSU Control ResultsComp1_CSU

Preliminary remarks

  • The component Client Suitability (CSU) Control Results, will carry a weight of 70% in the overall scorecard (with the exception of RMLs Chile and Uruguay, where it will count 100%).
  • The KRI component is based on all active local key controls mapped to one of the three existing Client Suitability (CSU) global minimum controls (GMC)
  • RMs not covered by one of the monthly performed controls are rated “accepted”
  • Some RMs being more controlled than others are considered in the applied logic 
  • RMs and THs are informed about major and moderate key control findings by FRM or local control functions before results are logged and reported.
  • Final logging of RM single CSU test execution results shifted by 2 months to consider the control remediation phase after deficiencies have been addressed to the Front - challenge via official challenge process later still possible.

Reporting Cycle and underlying control data

The final recording of individual CSU control results for Relationship Managers has been delayed by two months in consideration of the control remediation phase which follows after deficiencies having been addressed to the Front; however, the option to dispute the KRI scorecard results through the formal challenge process remains available (see "Challenge Process" below).

In rare cases, key control executions may be delayed. These delays won't be immediately reflected in GMIS but will be updated with the next Global MIS upload, ensuring data accuracy. 

Component 2  | Overdue Risk NotificationsComp2_ORN

Preliminary remarks

  • The component Overdue Risk Notifications (ORN) will account for 30% of the total scorecard weight (excluding RMLs Chile and Uruguay)
  • This KRI component enforces RMs to adhere to the policy requirement to handle business critical portfolio alerts within 5 days
  • This contractual / service aspect is currently not covered by a key control



  • The formula takes a grace of 1 overdue risk notification (ORN) in case of new portfolios added during the month.
  • If the IA fails to handle alerts (except OIS alert) correctly in Advice Exclusive / Advanced / IA mandate direct, the RM is not responsible. In such cases, the overdue alerts are excluded from the RMs Advisory KRI Scorecard.
