How to search information in the suitability wiki

https://wiki.juliusbaer.com/spaces/fkh/pages/309660779/How+to+search+information+in+the+suitability+wiki

This guide will help you to understand how you can best find information in the suitability wiki.

Step-by-step guide

1. Type suitability into your browser

2. How do I search information?

There are two ways to search & find information in the wiki. 

2.1 Use the Tree Structure

Either you search in the tree structure which you find on the left side of the landing page.

2.2. Use the search function

Or you use the search function on the top right side of the page.


When you click into the search field, you will find the following page:


1. Click on the space button 

2. Tick the box suitability wiki


3. Type into the search field what you are looking for

4. Choose from the search results
