2. Description of the alert

Bonds are monitored with a credit rating provided by the rating agencies. JB uses Moody’s, S&P and Fitch.

For its clients JB has defined an internal minimum threshold for a bond rating, called “Investment Grade”:

  • Moody’s: Baa3
  • S&P: BBB-
  • Fitch:  BBB

The below table illustrates the ratings assigned by the three rating agencies:

Whenever a rating of one of these rating agencies is unavailable or missing, the ZKB rating is used as the official rating source for bonds denominated in Swiss Franc (CHF) or bonds issued by a Swiss issuer. You may find the terms and conditions regarding minimum bond rating in the service model contract in the section portfolio monitoring.

The client can change the default configuration and lower the minimum credit rating threshold (see further information on →“How to amend the bond rating”)


3. What triggers this alert?

The alert is triggered, if the bond rating is below the minimum bond rating threshold. A “worst of three” concept is applied, i.e. the lowest rating of Moody’s, S&P, Fitch or ev. ZKB (if one rating is missing and if available) is applied as the threshold.

The alert can be triggered either:

a) in the Advisory Session as a result of a newly proposed transaction, or

b) in the Client Book as a result of overnight portfolio risk monitoring, e.g. due to a change of the external bond rating


4. How can this alert be resolved?

You can resolve the alert with one of the following actions:


A. Advisory Session (session alert)

Solicitation Type

Duty

Documentation (Justification / Contact Note)

Actively solicited

RM/IA must not proceed with the transaction (as long as the alert is shown), because the investment advice is unsuitable


Reverse solicited

RM/IA must advice against the transaction before its execution if it results in a breach

If client accepts the risk and wants to proceed:

RM/IA to document the client’s decision and rationale in the alert justification/ contact note as it is described here: →Justification rationale (dropdown values) for "client accepts risk"

Unsolicited

RM/IA must warn the order giver of the breach before executing the transaction

If client accepts the risk and wants to proceed:

RM/IA to document the warning in the alert justification/ contact note as it is described here: →Justification rationale (dropdown values) for "client accepts risk"

Important: In the event of any data issues in the system or in dedicated permitted use cases (e.g. workarounds) it is possible to overrule the alert and document it according to the defined process →Hard Block Override Procedure


B. Client Book (overnight portfolio monitoring alert)

DutyResolving the alert & Documentation (Resolution / Contact Note)

RM/IA must inform the client within 5 business days



RM/IA may create an investment proposal to address selling the position and provide it to the client. Use resolution note “investment proposal sent” (alert will be snoozed for 30 days)

If the client does not want to sell the position and accepts the risk:

RM/IA to create a resolution note in the client book (“client accepts risk” – also see examples below).

Please note: In case of a single resolution note (use the check Action symbol [  ] in the line of the respective alert), the justification rationale are available to support documentation (→Justification rationale (dropdown values) for "client accepts risk"). However, if you are using “justify all” to close one or several alerts with “client accepts risk”, please note 1) the risks that have been explained, 2) the client’s decision after the explanation and 3) the client's rationale in the contact note.

If the client’s portfolio is enabled for One-way Notification (OWN):

RM/IA to create a resolution note in the client book/portfolio alert overview. Select justification option “client informed” and follow the process in Wealth Navigator to dispatch the portfolio monitoring notification to the client. Further details can be found under →One-way notification for monitoring alerts

If the client expresses a specific need, RM/IA can lower the client’s bond rating threshold permanently in the portfolio configuration →

If the client could not be reached, RM/IA to select “client not reached” in the alert justification and document 1) the date when the client was approached and 2) the channel in the contact note


Validity of Resolution Notes (RN)

A Resolution Note for this alert is valid until 

  • the snooze period ends or
  • a new transaction with a rating below the Investment Grade has been executed


5. Justification example(s)

 Use case

Examples

Overnight portfolio monitoring alert

Example 1:

I informed the account holder by email that the Bubble Finance PLC -2069 bond is downgraded to BB-, which is below the portfolio minimum threshold of BBB-. He confirmed today over the phone that he wants to keep the position. 


Example 2:

The account holder called me and asked for a 1m CHF Lombard loan. During the call I told him that an overnight alert popped up and that the Republic of Jamaica bond he transferred from another Bank is rated B+. The minimum bond rating threshold of his portfolio is currently set to BBB-. He informed me, that he would like to keep the bond and intends to buy additional bonds with lower ratings as they offer additional income. He asked me, if I could also recommend bonds below investment grade. I informed him that the minimum bond rating threshold is contractually agreed, but can be customized to his needs to be able to provide advice. Therefore, he requests to lower the rating to B. I informed him, that we will apply the new threshold for the monitoring of the entire portfolio going forward, which he accepts and takes note.

Changes in mandate configuration

Example 1:

The Minimum Bond Rating has been changed from BBB-to B. I notified the account holder that several bonds in the portfolio are rated below the minimum bond threshold BBB-. The account holder decided to hold these bonds and requested me to decrease the minimum bond threshold to B. Rationale: He noted that he plans to keep investing in low-rated bonds due to the low yield environment


Example 2:

The Minimum Bond Rating has been changed from BBB-to B. As documented earlier in the resolution note on [date, time], the account holder requested me to decrease the minimum bond threshold. Rationale: He is planning to increase exposure in lower rated USD-denominated
