1. For which Service Models is this alert triggered?

  • JB Advice Premium
  • JB Advice Exclusive (former Advice Advanced)
  • JB Advice Light (former Advice Basic)
  • Investment Advisory Mandate
  • Investment Service Mandate
  • Product & Service Recommendation
  • Special Client Services
  • Portfolio Monitoring
  • Self Directed Clients

2. What does this alert do?

The alert appears if the total of positions in accounts reference currency (Risk Currency) is below the Advisory Service Model minimum listed below.
