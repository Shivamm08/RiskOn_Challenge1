Assessing the client’s knowledge and experience (K&E) level in terms of investment instruments is the centrepiece of any investor protection regulation globally. Understanding and considering the client’s knowhow in financial instruments is crucial to prevent the client from taking unintended risks and to avoid misconceptions.


WE ALREADY CAPTURE K&E - WHY DO WE NEED A CHANGE?

In the past years new products and asset classes with new characteristics and risks have emerged. Therefore, also the regulators (e.g., ESMA) request financial institutions to apply a more granular approach when assessing and documenting the K&E levels of a client. To fulfil the regulatory demand on one hand and to consider the changed investment landscape on the other the bank has decided to apply a new global minimum standard and gradually introduce a new K&E form.
