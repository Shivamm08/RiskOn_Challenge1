Structured Products rules for Client Domicile Bahrain

Structured Products are now being checked against a preset list of issuers allowed by the local regulator.

If the product is not issued by one of them, an alert is displayed:

The issuer of this product is not approved by the local regulator (Central Bank of Bahrain). 

If the product is issued by one of the issuers listed below, a minimum investment of 100'000 USD is required and will be notified with the following alert:

Please check if the total minimum initial investment amount is at least 100’000 USD.
