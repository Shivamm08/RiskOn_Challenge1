Component | CSU Control Results (CSU)

Note: This component accounts for 70% of the final scorecard result.

Monthly score

The monthly score for this Advisory KRI component is based on the defined calculation logic described in detail in the CSU Calculation Logic, where your monthly key control test execution results get weighted and scored.

The relevant information about your monthly score for this component you find in GMIS by selecting the component "Client Suitability Key Control Results (CSU)".

Your monthly score is presented in GMIS as colour code (Points) or Score (1 to 3).

Annual Score

The annual score is made up of the sum of all monthly scores. 

The following scores are valid for each of your monthly results:

1 = Accepted 

2 = Raised (1 penalty point, i.e. point above accepted)

3 = Severe (2 penalty points)

The best possible annual score is 12 ( e.g. 1= accepted / green rating) x 12 months)


Acceptable Score Range 

An annual score of 12 to 17 points is considered "accepted". 

→ Up to 5 "penalty points" (points accumulated beyond the monthly score of 1) are granted per year for an "accepted" score.


Non-acceptable Score Range

An annual score of 18 to 29 points is considered "raised". 

→ 6 to 17 "penalty points" per year lead to a "raised" score.

For example, if by the end of June you have already accumulated 6 "penalty points", your final annual result will be at least "Raised".


An annual score of > 29 points is considered "severe". 

→ 18 to 24 "penalty points" per year lead to a "severe" score.

The YTD result score is made up of the sum of all monthly scores, divided by the number of months. The following thresholds apply:

  • Accepted: Less than 1.5
  • Raised: 1.5 or greater, but less than 2.5
  • Severe: 2.5 or greater, up to 3.0


Component | Overdue Risk Notifications (ORN)

Note: This component accounts for 30% of the final scorecard result.

Monthly score

The monthly score for this Advisory KRI component is based on the defined calculation logic described in detail in the ORN Calculation Logic, where your overdue risk notifications get weighted and scored.

The relevant information about your monthly score for this component you find in GMIS by selecting the component "Overdue Risk Notification (ORN)".

Your monthly score is presented in GMIS as colour code (Points) or Score (in%).


The same score thresholds apply for the YTD and annual component rating.

Overall | Advisory KRI Scorecard Rating

For each of the two Advisory scorecard components, a monthly rating is provided, to which the following scores are applied:

1 = Accepted 

2 = Raised 

3 = Severe

The scores of each component are weighted and summed up. According to the applied thresholds, this results in the following possible combinations (see table and applied thresholds below):

Example

If an RM is considered "accepted" in the CSU component but receives a "Raised" rating in the ORN component, their overall scorecard rating will still be "accepted" as the weighted average will be (1 x 0.7) + (2 x 0.3) = 1.3, which is below the 1.5 threshold.

Impact on variable compensation

The impact for RMs of not-accepted KRIs on their variable compensation is as follows:

  • A deduction of 5.0% is applied for each red (severe) KRI 
  • A deduction of 2.5% is applied for each amber (raised) KRI 
  • The deduction is doubled for repetitive cases (i.e., minimum of same colour rating within two subsequent years) 

For all RMs, the final deduction is the sum of the deductions of each KRI. The final deduction cannot exceed 25% (subject to local deviation), it is applied to the allocated variable compensation (including all different types of variable pay, e.g., subsidies) prior to pay-out.
