What is Portfolio Quality Monitoring?

The Portfolio Quality Monitor is enabled in Wealth Navigator which complements the risk alert view and supports Relationship Managers in assessing the overall portfolio quality, based on six portfolio-and position-based quality elements.

The overall portfolio quality, as well as the six quality elements range from 1 to 9, and are divided into four quality ratings: Excellent, Good, Fair and Care

Frequently asked questions:


Will I see the contributing positions to a poor portfolio quality element?

  • Yes! 

Are there any portfolio constellations for which the Portfolio Quality Monitor is not appropriate?

  • In reality situations can occur which leads to extreme portfolios, e.g. portfolios which are not invested yet/anymore or portfolios which are only used to segregate assets. For such portfolios, the portfolio quality is not meaningful.

Do I have to communicate Portfolio Quality to my clients?

  • Currently, Portfolio Quality does not need to be communicated to your clients.

Will I receive an alert if my Portfolio Quality has a rating below >10%?

  • As of today, a poor Portfolio Quality will not trigger any form of alert.
