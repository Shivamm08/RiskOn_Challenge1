Scope

The CIP Renewal is an elementary regulatory requirement. The Bank’s minimum standards for executing the CIP renewal is derived from the global Suitability Policy (JBG-2070-00 Investment Suitability and Appropriateness v.5).

The Client Investment Profile (CIP) and the Knowledge & Experience (K&E) set the basis for suitable and consistent high-quality advice to all our clients. They must be assessed in close and personal collaboration with the client.

To comply with suitability and appropriateness rules it is important that the CIP is always up to date. Therefore, the CIP Renewal needs to be carried out every two years in the form of a so called 'negative confirmation'. Depending on local regulations, it might be necessary to perform the CIP renewal on a yearly basis and/or to gather a new CIP on a regular basis - please consult your local suitability policies and guidelines for proper procedures.

Please note that for locations without CLM and/or with higher regulatory requirements, the process might be different and eventually check your local requirements..

For RM locations with CLM, the 'CIP Renewal Summary' and the 'CIP Renewal Cover Letter' are obtained in CLM directly via the CIP Renewal Process. Please use the word version of the client letter only if you face IT-issues with CLM (see below).

Step-by-step guide for CIP Renewal Process in CLM

Scope: BC CH all RML excluding RML UK

The negative confirmation approach is only allowed for clients who have previously answered ALL questions in the CIP. For clients with a filled indication section in the old CIP forms (CIPPI/CIPLE) and/or not fully completed CIP form, please gather a new valid and complete CIP form. A CIP Renewal for incomplete CIPs is not allowed.

For a proper audit trail and ensuring the regulatory requirements, please make sure that the provision of the CIP Summary AND the cover letter is duly documented as indicated below.

30 days before the above-mentioned two year period is over, the Relationship Manager (RM) or the Account Manager (AM) in case of CUR receives a CIP Renewal Task within the CLM Task Manager to renew the client’s investment profile. 7 days prior to the deadline as well as on the due date, the RM/AM receive a reminder via e-mail. 14 days after being overdue, the task is escalated to the Team Head.

The provisioning as well as the archiving of the evidences must manually be documented in a CRM contact note including the attachment of the CIP summary and cover letter documents.

By finishing the CIP Renewal, the CIP is automatically extended by 2 years.

Special case: Hold Mail clients

Please be informed that for Hold Mail clients, the RM needs to actively contact and inform the client about the renewal process and the CIP summary once the work item is generated. This can be done either with a client meeting, a phone call or by emailing the client. Please minute the client communication in CRM.

Special case: deceased clients

For deceased clients, kindly complete the task in CLM without distributing the documents and document it in a manual contact note in CRM.

Clients’ reaction

Based on the client feedback, two different approaches apply:

a) if the client reacts and requests to update his/her profile by filling in a new CIP and/or K&E form/s with the RM, these forms will be scanned by CDS. CLM then automatically updates the fields in the CIP section

b) if the client does not react (negative approach): the pre-existing CIP data remains valid for another 2 years
