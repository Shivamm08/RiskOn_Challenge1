Configuration of ESG Preferences on Portfolio Level in Wealth Navigator

https://wiki.juliusbaer.com/spaces/fkh/pages/361532731/Configuration+of+ESG+Preferences+on+Portfolio+Level+in+Wealth+Navigator

The ESG preference chosen by clients must always be seen both on a ZR/CIP and on a portfolio level. Portfolios can have a higher ESG preference than the one stated by the client in the CIP. The reverse, however, is not possible. If a client does not specify a ESG preference for a portfolio, it will automatically become the same as the preference expressed in the Client Investment Profile. Should a client want to increase the ESG preference (e.g. from traditional to responsible) on a portfolio level, this change can be conducted in Wealth Navigator as follows (if there are two or more portfolios under a given ZR):


1. In the "ESG configuration" tab, the client ESG preference for the portfolio can be changed in the dropdown menu. Make sure to save after changing the preference. 


2. A change in preference will prompt the creation of a contact note:

Note: It is mandatory to select an account holder as the order giver, i.e. the change of the ESG preferences on Portfolio level must be agreed upon with an account holder (not POA). The changes will be applied immediately to all places where it is displayed.


3. Once the preference for the portfolio has been changed, the change will be reflected in the overview in Wealth Navigator. The blue box shows the new portfolio preference.
