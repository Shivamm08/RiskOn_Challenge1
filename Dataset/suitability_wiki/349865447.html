ESG Suitability Check in MSD for Discretionary Mandates

The below mentioned changes apply for the following RML/BC constellations: BC CH / RML CH, DE, LU, BC GG / RML CH, BC LU / RML LU, ESP, IRL.

  1. The ESG preferences (TI, RI, or SI) of the client will be matched against the JB ESG Category (TI, RI or SI) of the DMs in MSD (BC CH/GG & BC LU). 
  2. The min. proportion preferences for sustainable investment according to SFDR / EU Taxonomy of the client will be matched against the proportions for such investments within the DMs (BC LU only)

For existing clients the ESG preferences (TI, RI or SI) will be sourced from CLM/CRM.

In addition, the JB ESG Category (TI, RI or SI) for the DMs will be displayed as well.

DMs with a JB ESG Category that is the same or higher than the ESG preferences of the client are allowed to be processed in MSD. DMs with a JB ESG category (e.g. TI) that is lower than the ESG preferences (e.g. RI) of the client are only allowed to be processed, in case the ESG preferences of the client get changed (e.g. from RI to TI) as a follow-up task via CIP (see info text in the blue box in the screenshot below).

For prospect clients the ESG preferences have to be entered via a dedicated ESG preferences drop-down menu (TI, RI, or SI).


For BC LU, RMs will also be alerted in cases the selected DM, does not meet the client's specified preferences for sustainable investment proportions according to SFDR / EU Taxonomy.


Abbreviations: TI = Traditional Investment, RI = Responsible Investment, SI = Sustainable Investment, RML = RM Location, BC = Booking Center
