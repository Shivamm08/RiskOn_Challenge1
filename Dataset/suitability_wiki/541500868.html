Direct Private Investments ("DPI")

https://wiki.juliusbaer.com/spaces/fkh/pages/541500868/Direct+Private+Investments+DPI

Markets facilitates transactions between Product Providers on the one hand and Investors meeting specific criteria. The subscription process is as follows:


  1. DPI sends opportunities to RM. The RM introduction Email (link) contains - besides the introduction - 2 client mailings (Email-1 and Email-2). Email-1 contains a teaser and the confidentiality statement. Email-2 contains the Fee Disclosure and VDR access.
  2. RM checks a) if client is a professional client and b) selling restrictions of the fund. The selling restrictions are provided in the RM instruction Email. If all is fine, RM forwards Email-1 to the client(s), but must not provide investment advice.
  3. In case of interest client has to send back the singed NDA
  4.     
    1.  RM performs a Wealth Navigator “Custom Instrument” Session (select: Non-Traditional-Fund – and either "Hedge Fund" or "Private Equity" (definition can be found here: link)). MDDA alert can be closed with “document already provided”. Add the following comment: “KID not regulatory required for professional clients. Product Costs disclosed in PPM and subscription form made available in the VDR”.
    2.  If no alerts have been triggered or could have been solved, the RM sends Email-2 to the client (includes detailed transaction description, the product providers Non-Disclosure Agreement, the Swiss Banking Secrecy Waiver and the Fee Disclosure Letter)

  5. Client confirms interest and access to VDR. The Private Placement Memorandum "PPM" and subscription form are available in the VDR. Both documents disclose the product fees of the SPV.

  6. Clients performs Due Diligence and signs subscription form.

  7. RM to check signature and record a client contact note. Further RM completes the “Pre-Execution Checklist” (link) which requires the specification of the Wealth Navigator Session ID and forwards the checklist to the DPI desk.

  8. DPI instructs the RM on how trade should be entered
