While designing new mandate solutions in MSD, the initiator of the solution needs to always adhere to any pre-contractual warnings showing up on the screen. One of these warnings can be, that the Overall Investment Strategy (OIS) of the proposed portfolio/s is not in line with the CIP-strategy. If such warnings in orange frames appear, please ALWAYS follow the instructions and do not offer any deviating strategies on active solicitation basis. If on a reverse solicitation basis, a client explicitly asks for having a deviating strategies in their portfolio, please check your local policies of the advisory location if this is allowed - for RML CH for example, this is allowed according to CH-2070-00 Suitability and Appropriateness v.5. If such deviations are allowed from a regulatory- and/or policy perspective, please create a CCN (client contact note) containing the following elements: 

  • Risk warning: Documentation of the fact that RM explained towards the client the risks of not having the same strategy in the mandate as in the CIP (e.g. possible higher gains come along with possible higher losses; explanation of the concrete differences between the 5 strategies)
  • Client's decision to keep the current strategy: Documentation of the client's decision after the risk warning took place (e.g. client followed the explanation from the RM about the risks and characteristics of the deviating strategy and wants to keep the divergent strategy even though)
  • Rationale: Documentation of why the client wants to have deviating strategies in comparison to the CIP (e.g. client follows different strategies with other banks and explicitly wants to focus on a certain strategy at JB; core-satellite portfolio; portfolio with 'Liebhaberaktie', etc.)

Important: The above mentioned elements for justification must always be discussed pre-contract - via MSD/MOC or in any CCN.
