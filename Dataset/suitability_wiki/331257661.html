When does a PIB need to be provided?

The provision of a PIB is mandatory in the following constellation (all criteria need to be fulfilled):

  • RM Location = all 
  • Client Domicile = Germany
  • Client Classification = Retail
  • Solicitation Type = solicited or reverse-solicited
  • Product Type = Equities or straight Bonds (plain vanilla bonds). Complex bonds require a PRIIPS KID 
  • Transaction Type = buy transaction only

Regulatory Background

The product information sheet for equities and bonds (PIB) is a German legal requirement which EU law does not provide.
According to Section 31 (3a) of the German Securities Trading Act (WpHG), investment services companies must provide their customers with a short and easy-to-understand product information sheet in good time before entering into a transaction for each financial instrument they recommend to buy. This requirement does not apply to professional customers.
