Client ESG Preferences

https://wiki.juliusbaer.com/spaces/fkh/pages/348091027/Client+ESG+Preferences

Starting in July 2022 clients with RM locations in Switzerland, Eurohub and Germany will be offered to express their ESG preferences according to the JB Sustainable Investment Rating Methodology. Clients will be offered to choose to become either Traditional, Responsible or Sustainable investors. This choice will affect the universe that they can be advised on and the discretionary mandates that they have. For more specific information please read the article on the implications on our offering. Furthermore, these clients can also ask to get information on the EU Taxonomy alignment of their investments. This, however, has no implications on the offering. 

New clients and clients which have to renew their Client Investment Profile (CIP) in the abovementioned locations will have to be onboarded with the new CIP including a page on ESG preferences and Taxonomy alignment. Below you can find the added suitability questions:


In addition to above stated basic ESG preferences, as of September 2022 clients booked in Luxembourg and Germany have the ability to determine additional ESG preferences. These additional ESG preferences relate to minimum proportions of sustainable investment according to a) Sustainable Finance Disclosure Regulation (SFDR) b) the EU Taxonomy.
These additional ESG preferences are gathered via an additional Side Form outside the Client Investment Profile.
Clients have the following choices:

  • None
  • Basic
  • Moderate
  • High

Mapped to the choices Basic, Moderate and High are percentage values for minimum proportions of sustainable investments according to SFDR / EU Taxonomy. These values are available to client via link in the Side Form (juliusbaer.com/fileadmin/legal/julius-baer-minimum-proportion-threshold-information-en.pdf)



Please note that the offering of managing an advisory portfolio according to these additional ESG preferences is limited to Advice Exclusive direct clients.
