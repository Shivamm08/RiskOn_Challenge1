Portfolio Cost Simulation

https://wiki.juliusbaer.com/spaces/fkh/pages/382699606/Portfolio+Cost+Simulation
  • What is the Portfolio Cost Simulation ("PCS")?
    • PCS is a mandatory cost disclosure document which must be handed out pre-contractually to full MiFID clients to provide them with the indication of the costs and charges related to a mandate
  • What clients are in scope?
    • Full MiFID clients,
      This includes clients domiciled in Germany if served out of RML CH or all clients if served out of the RML in the EEA/UK, who:
      • have a new mandate, i.e.
        • are newly onboarded, or
        • switch from one mandate to another, or
        • conclude an additional mandate, or
      • have an existing mandate which is subject to the pricing-relevant changes resulting both in the increase or decrease in applicable costs and charges
  • What mandates are in scope?
    • All advisory/discretionary and execution-only mandates
  • How is the PCS created and delivered to the client?
    • Portfolio Cost Simulation is created and delivered to the client manually by the RM
    • The Web-based PCS must be used in order to produce a cost simulation specifically for the invested amount, service and pricing model
    • Web-based PCS as well additional guidelines and FAQ can be found under the following link: MIFID II: PORTFOLIO COST SIMULATION
  • How must the provision of the PCS be documented?
    • The provision of the PCS must be documented in a contact note (CRM)
    • The PCS must be archived manually in OnDemand. In case the PCS is provided via e-mail, please archive the e-mail by using the archiving function in the Outlook (including all attachments)
