2. Description of the alert

The JB Distributor Target Market (“JB DTM”) defines the permitted distribution scope for certain financial instruments (on instrument classes level), as not all financial instruments are equally suitable for all clients due to product risk or complexity (e.g. AT1, Hedge Funds, Private Equity, Digital Assets). For example, Hedge Funds or Private Equity Instruments are not intended for private/retail clients. 

The JB DTM checks the distribution scope defined for a financial instrument type (e.g. Digital Assets) against the client attributes (Client Classification, Financial Risk Ability, Time Horizon and Investment Strategy). If the client instruments do not match with the distribution scope, a JB DTM alert is triggered, informing the RM that the product cannot be advised nor sold (even upon an unsolicited request) to a specific client. 

Please find further information under →JB Distributor Target Market Concept.   


3. What triggers this alert?

The JB DTM alert is triggered in the Advisory Session for buy transactions independent of the solicitation type (actively, reverse and unsolicited), if the client attributes are not meeting the defined JB DTM e.g.

  1. Client Classification
    1. Target Market: Elected Professional or Per Se Professional or Institutional*
    2. Current Situation: Private (Retail)
  2. Financial Risk Ability
    1. Target Market: High
    2. Current Situation: Considerable
  3. Investment Horizon
    1. Target Market: 4 to 10 years or more than 10 years
    2. Current Situation: 1 to 4 years
  4. Investment Strategy 
    1. Target Market: Balanced or Growth or Capital Gain
    2. Current Situation: Fixed Income

*or according to the local client classification (e.g. Israeli qualified investor)

The alert shows all the attributes, which do not match, when clicking on the  button.


4. How can this alert be resolved?

You can resolve the alert with one of the following actions:

Advisory Session (session alert)

Actively solicited

Reverse solicited and

Unsolicited

RM/IA must not proceed with the transaction as long as the alert is shown, because JB does not want to advise respectively sell this product to this client category. 


If the client wants to buy this instrument, RM/IA shall consider:

  • Changing the client classification (but only if specifically requested by the client and if conditions for professional classification are fulfilled)
  • Obtaining a new CIP 
    • if the product requires a higher risk tolerance than permitted by the current CIP
    • if the product requires a longer time horizon than permitted by the current CIP
  • Signing a new contract if the client wants to change to a higher risk investment strategy                                                                                                                                       

Important: If you are of the opinion that the alert has been triggered wrongly (based on wrong product data / FI Classification of the product e.g. mutual fund is triggering a retail client block), please reach out to →Business Front Support (**1166 #4). BFS will clarify the FI Classification.

  • If the FI Classification is set wrongly and immediate re-classification is possible, you can cancel the current Advisory Session and start a new one. JB DTM alert should not be triggered anymore. 
  • If the FI Classification is set wrongly, but cannot be corrected, BFS confirms wrong classification and will allow you to override the alert. Please proceed according to the defined process →Hard Block Override Procedure
  • If the FI Classification is correct, do not proceed with the transaction!
