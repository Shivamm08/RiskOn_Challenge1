1. Scoping - client view


a) FIDLEG clients: Pre-trade cost disclosure is not required in case of FIDLEG clients but can be provided upon client's request (DiAS will produce and archive the document only if it is explicitly requested during the DiAS session, i.e. the box must be manually ticked) 


b) MiFID clients (Full MiFID and MiFID "Add-on"): In case of MiFID retail clients, a pre-trade cost disclosure is required prior to any transaction (both unsolicited and advised). In case of MiFID professional clients, a pre-trade cost disclosure is required prior to any advised transaction. It is automatically included in the client's package in DiAS (i.e. the box is ticked by default).

DiAS Technical Issue

If DiAS does not generate a pre-trade cost disclosure due to a technical issue, the following alert pops-up: "Document distribution failed". In such cases there is no manual action required and the DiAS session can be continued.

The rectification is done by the post-trade transaction confirmation. In addition, product-inherent costs are disclosed pre-trade in the PRIIPS KID.

If the information on the ongoing product costs is missing, pre-trade cost disclosure makes reference to KID for information on these costs. Otherwise, full disclosure of transaction/upfront fees and ongoing product costs in the pre-trade cost disclosure itself.

Pre-trade cost disclosure document also contains the issuer charges (fee which compensates the manufacturer for structuring the product) and the upfront charges (fee which compensates the private bank for distributing the product). Both, the issuer charge and the upfront charge are part of the price which the client pays.

In addition, there is an automated cost disclosure for OTC equity derivatives which are available in Derivatives Toolbox or OTC Equity Options Toolbox.

Special Pricing Conditions
  • Client’s pricing conditions (either standard or special conditions) as reflected in the client’s-specific brokerage fee code are considered for the estimated fee calculation in the pre-trade cost disclosure
  • One-off pricing agreements with the client which are not reflected in the client’s-specific brokerage fee code and require pre-trade manual fee adjustment are not considered in the pre-trade cost disclosure - this can lead to difference between fees estimated in the pre-trade cost disclosure (based on client’s-specific brokerage fee code) and the actual fees charged which are visible in the post-trade trade confirmation => please inform the client accordingly and explain the difference
  • In any case, pre-trade cost disclosure must be sent to MiFID clients via an e-channel (e-banking or e-mail) for all clients booked in Switzerland / Guernsey

For avoidance of doubt, the client’s acknowledgement of any adjusted transaction fees which are not in line with the price levels agreed with the client (i.e. reflected in the client’s-specific brokerage fee code) must be duly documented prior to transaction.


Ongoing product costs

Ongoing product costs include (i) TER and additional costs (e.g. transaction costs) for funds and (ii) management fee for AMCs. They are relevant only in case of buy transactions, as they cease to apply after the product is sold.

FX conversion (implicit FX costs)

Whenever possible it is recommended to avoid buying instruments in one currency and debit another currency account (which triggers a FX conversion costs for the client). It is recommended to set up currency accounts (ideally at the point of account opening) for all major currencies that the client may wish to trade in, to prevent avoidable FX conversion costs.
