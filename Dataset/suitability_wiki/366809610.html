How does it work?

Methodology framework for the calculation of product risk

JB PRR general framework recognizes three key risk dimensions:  market risk, default (credit) risk and liquidity risk. Default risk is a risk dimension relevant only for bonds or structured products subject to issuer or counterparty risk of default. Market and default risks are expressed in ratings whereas the liquidity risk is applied via a penalty.




How is the market risk calculated?

The framework

By calculating the normal and extreme market risks, combining them and deriving the market risk rating from the market risk score.




How is the credit risk calculated?

The framework for most financial instruments

By deriving the credit quality rating from the Moody’s, S&P, ZKB and Fitch ratings or the instrument’s credit spread. If no credit quality rating is available, the credit spread is used as a fallback to assess the credit risk rating.




How is the liquidity risk calculated?

The framework

By determining how many days will be required to sell large position (equities: 10 Mio CHF, bonds: 75 Mio CHF) subject to a limit of 2.5% on total transaction costs.




How is the product risk calculated?

Simplified calculation method

By taking the higher risk rating between market and credit risk and adding the liquidity risk penalty.

Impact on my client's portfolio

Potential changes old vs new methodology in %

Depending on the types of instruments in the portfolio, the consolidated product risk can increase or decrease, some examples below:


  • Invested mostly in equities

Most equities will get a higher product risk mainly due to the inclusion of stress tests and the comparability to other asset classes.



  • Invested mostly in bonds

Most bonds will change its product risk, mostly due to the inclusion of market risk components, compared to a credit focused method in the past.


  • Invested mostly in funds

The risk rating of funds will reflect better the underlying asset classes. Bond funds will generally decrease in risk whereas equity funds increase.


  • Invested mostly in structured products

Product risk for structured products will be closer aligned to its structure and/or underlying instruments.
