"Master Suitability controls in 5 minutes and secure your success!"

This guide will support you with simple rules to streamline your daily tasks and significantly reduce potential errors on Suitability topics.

2wo general observations

  1. Performing Suitability checks in Wealth Navigator (WN) after a client decision was made is too late. Ensure that your Suitability sessions are performed in WN before a client decision is made.

  2. A(R)Ms often perform the advisory sessions or handle CLM Task Manager deficiencies on behalf of the RMs. If the WN user is not the (client facing) RM, it must be ensured that the whole session details are clearly understood and documented. In case of alerts or CLM Task Manager deficiencies, the A(R)M must explicitly inform the RM to clear the alert/deficiency correctly. (see here)

6ix rules to avoid control deficiencies

  1. Communicate alerts to the client before proceeding with the order entry. Use the appropriate justification type(s) and document the interaction accordingly in the alert justification/contact note.
  2. Do not oversteer alerts using the Hard Block Override Procedure "HOP" unless you have a valid HOP use case (see here) and have documented it accordingly.
  3. “No KID, no trade!” – If you are requested to upload/provide a product information document or another mandatory document, do not proceed without ensuring that the correct document was duly provided to the order giver in timely manner.
  4. Always use electronic communication channels (‘e-banking’ or registered ‘e-mail’) to ensure timely provision of documents to the client and automated archiving of evidence in the system.
  5. Archive all e-mails (incl. attachments) with client interactions in CLM/OnDemand.
  6. Always complete the contact note with detailed information about the client interaction, especially the initiation of advisory ideas, so that an independent third party can understand the context.

4our use cases to always keep in mind

  1. Do not sell instruments to retail/private clients if they are not intended for them. Clients must be correctly classified as Professionals to proceed with such transaction.
  2. Increasing an existing position is not a valid reason to oversteer an alert. Furthermore, please note that active advice must never lead to a suitability breach, and it cannot be justified.
  3. Note that there are no KIDs for US ETFs. Hence, MiFID Retail Clients cannot invest as the provision of the KID is a regulatory requirement.
  4. Always keep the CIP and K&E information up to date and complete. It is mandatory to gather a K&E form for all partners that act as order givers. In case there is no K&E form yet, alerts will be repeatedly triggered in the advisory tool.
