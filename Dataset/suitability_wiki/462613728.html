List of all business-critical alerts in Wealth Navigator (WN). Please note, that not all alerts are relevant for every service model and advisory location.

  • CPR is the weighted average of the Product Risk Ratings (PRR) of positions in a portfolio
  • CPR takes leverage into consideration
  • Reduce high-PRR positions
  • Update investment strategy
  • Request methodology exception
  • Adjust the respective amount for leverage calculation

  • Exposure towards a single issuer in the portfolio exceeds the threshold
  • Reduce exposure to an issuer
  • Increase threshold in WN portfolio configuration
  • Resolution note/ justification
  • Limit exposure to a single issuer, protects from issuer default, ensures diversification
Contractual agreement
3

Concentration Risk on Single Position

  • Exposure towards a single instrument exceeds the threshold
  • Thresholds are defined per asset class
  • Reduce position below the threshold
  • Resolution note/ justification
  • Ensures sufficient portfolio diversification
Contractual agreement
4

  • Guides the WN user on correct universe handling for solicited orders
  • Advice to buy is only permitted for offered instruments. Check universe status before providing advice
  • Ensures adherence of our solicitation guidelines
  • Clients benefit from due diligence and ongoing monitoring of offered instruments
Product Governance  (internal)
5

  • Buy orders for instruments with a negative view
  • Ongoing monitoring of positions with a negative view
  • Do not advise clients to buy / increase these positions. If a client wants to buy, inform about Negative View
  • Resolution note/justification
  • Negative View instruments are expected to underperform in relative terms
  • Rating changes are brought to the attention of clients
Contractual agreement
6

Strategic Asset Allocation (SAA)

  • Exposure to an asset class deviates from the leeways of the client’s mandate strategy
  • Portfolio rebalancing
  • Adapt mandate strategy
  • Resolution note/justification
  • For Advice Advanced: WN portfolio configuration
  • Portfolio is aligned to the mandate strategy (over / under-exposure)
  • SAA is the most important determinant for expected returns
Contractual agreement
7
  • The bond rating is below the minimum threshold (lowest rating applies)
  • Sell the position
  • Adjust bond rating threshold in WN portfolio configuration
  • Resolution note / justification
  • Ensures client is informed of bonds below the threshold and related risks
Contractual agreement
8

Knowledge & Experience (K&E)

  • Order giver’s K&E for buy orders in applicable instrument categories
  • Educate (solicited) or warn (unsolicited) the order giver before placing the orders
  • Provide education material (solicited)
  • Protects clients from products they don’t understand
  • Informs RMs about gaps in the client’s K&E
Regulatory required
9
  • Compares CIP strategy with the mandate strategy of the underlying portfolio (if multiple portfolios: considered on gross mkt. value-weighted basis)
  • Adapt CIP
  • Adapt mandate strategy
  • Review portfolio constellation (in case of multiple portfolios)
  • Resolution note
  • Ensures the mandate strategy / strategies are aligned with the CIP
Contractual agreement
10Mandatory Document Disclosure (MDD)
  • Relevant documents provided to the client (KID, PID, Cost disclosure, etc.) are missing
  • Rules consider client classification and domicile
  • upload missing mandatory document as per alert, before placing the order
  • Ensure regulatory compliance and provision of information to client
Regulatory required
11
  • Contractual obligation to provide a PAR once per year
  • Alert is triggered 300 days after the last PAR was provided
  • Generate PAR or Personal Investment Recommendation (PIR) in WN and share with the client
  • Create a contact note
  • Reminds the RM/IA that a PAR needs to be provided
    • Clients receive portfolio monitoring results at least once per year
Contractual agreement
12
  • Checks if the intended investment matches with the defined Target Market
  • Change client classification
  • Obtain new CIP
  • Signing new contract with riskier Investment Strategy
  • Adherence to JB defined risk appetite & compliance with MiFID
  • Manage the risk of potential mis-selling
Regulatory required and Product Governance (internal)
13
  • Checks if a more appropriate share class is available 
  • By selling proposed share class
  • Serve the client in its best interest (duty of care)
Product Governance (internal)
14
  • Checks if client has signed the "Digital Assets Special Terms & Conditions Waiver"
  • By letting the client sign the waiver
  • Ensures proper risk disclosure to the client before investing in Digital Assets
Product Governance (internal)
15
  • Checks if the client is domiciled in a country in which the distribution of Digital Assets is banned.
  • Hard Block for some countries, which cannot be resolved. 
  • In case of a Soft block, see more info under the detailed description of the alert.
  • Ensures that local regulation is not breached
Product Governance (internal)
16
  • Advised buy orders for instruments classified as ESG risk (exclusion list, controversies, bad-ESG scoring by third-parties, etc.)
  • Advice to buy not permitted – check instrument before advising
  • ESG risk instruments can only be bought unsolicited
  • ESG risk instruments may have an elevated risk of environmental, social or governance-related events, which could negatively impact their price
Regulatory required
17
  • Checks if ESG Category of the product meets the ESG preferences selected by the client in the CIP
  • Select another product that meets the ESG preferences
  • Ensures that investments are aligned with the ESG Preferences of the client
Regulatory required
18
  • Checks if an ESG category is assigned to the financial instrument
  • Warn the client that ESG suitability check cannot be performed
  • Informs the client, that ESG suitability check cannot be performed
Regulatory required
19
  • Checks if - if a client selects additional ESG preferences - the client's advisory portfolio alignment to Sustainable Investments according to SFDR / EU Taxonomy is in line with the corresponding threshold defined by the client's ESG preference.
  • Advised: Client adapts minimum proportion preference for this trade only
  • Non-advised: Inform the client about deviation and gather acceptance of deviation
  • Informs the client and the RM about breach of required minimum proportion preference
Regulatory required
20

  • Checks if the ESG category of the discretionary mandate is in line with the ESG Preference of the client's CIP
  • Change discretionary mandate
  • Change ESG preference in the CIP
  • Client Accepts Risk with acc. rationale
  • Informs the RM/client about the fact that the selected DM does not meet the client's ESG preferences
Regulatory required
21
  • Checks if the investments adhere to the restricted number of funds (3 for RML CH, 1 for RML UK) within JB Fund Access Mandates
  • General: No justification option
  • In session: Hard block for buying additional funds
  • Overnight: Reducing the existing number of positions
  • Adhering to contractual duty of having limited numbers of funds in this mandate
Contractual agreement
22
  • Checks if the selected fund is part of the relevant PSP recommendation lists („In-house and Partner Funds“) for JB Fund Access Mandates
  • General: No justification option
  • Actively solicited: Not proceed
  • Reverse solicited: Propose allowed instrument
  • Unsolicited: Propose allowed instrument and change to "reverse solicitation"
  • Adhering to contractual duty of having In-house or Partner Funds in this mandate
Contractual agreement
23Fair value (UK Consumer Duty)
  • Checks the fair value information that the manufacturer of the fund has provided for the selected fund
  • Fund does not represent fair value:
    • Retail Client → Hard block for all solicitations
    • Professional Client → Hard block for actively solicited, client accepts risk for reverse solicited and client informed for unsolicited 
  • Fair value is not available → Manual check for all clients and all solicitations
  • Informs the RM if the fund represent fair value 
Regulatory required 
24JB Distributor Target Market
  • Checks if product can be distributed to a certain client according to the defined distribution rules
  • General: No justification option
  • Product can only be soled to client, if 
  • Client request a change in client classification
  • Circumstances changed, which allow to obtain a new CIP (new Financial Risk Tolerance / Time Horizon)
  • Client is willing to sign a new contract with a riskier investment strategy 
  • Supports the RM in avoiding mis-selling of products to a client for which products are not intended
Product Governance (internal)
25BMO Alerts
  • The BMO Rule Engine includes several packages, responsible for checking different aspects of a trade.
  • According to alert description
  • Ensures that distribution and tax rules, fund selling restrictions, mandatory document requirements etc. are complied with.
