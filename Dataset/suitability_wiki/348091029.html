As part of the rollout of the new methodology and the client ESG preference gathering (in selected locations) clients booked in CH, GG or LU with RMs in Switzerland, Eurohub and Germany, have received on 8th of July 2022 a separate report including information on the ESG performance of their portfolios. 

Such a mass-distribution of the ESG report was repeated Q4 2023, which additionally included clients with RMs in the UK.

As of mid-September 2023 RMs can order the ESG report for their clients at any time and/or in regular intervals. 

This ESG report contains the following sections:

  • Portfolio ESG Summary: The first page of the report contains an overview of the portfolio (incl. asset class breakdown), the ESG categories overall and for equities, bonds & funds, as well as a short explanation of the ESG categories.
    Additionally, clients can see which percentage of their investments in each of the categories (overall portfolio, equities, fixed income, funds) are part of the JB investment universe.
    As of Q2 2024 positions in structured products & derivatives will also be included.


  • Leaders and Laggards: The second page of the report contains an overview of the equities & corporate bond positions with the highest ESG category and the lowest ESG category and showing further JB ESG scores and the MSCI ESG Rating.
    Additionally, positions that are part of the JB investment universe are marked with an asterisk.
    As of Q2 2024 positions in structured products & derivatives will also be included.


  • Largest Positions: The third page of the report has similar information to the second page, but about the 10 largest positions in the portfolio.
    As of Q2 2024 positions in structured products & derivatives will also be included.


  • Portfolio overview by ESG score: Clients can see how their equity & corporate bond holdings are distributed according to JB ESG scores and their MSCI rating.



  • Leaders and Laggards Funds: An overview of the fund positions with the highest ESG category and the lowest ESG category, as well as their JB ESG scores and the MSCI ESG Rating. Please note that this page was only part of Q4 2023 mass-distribution if the client's portfolio contained at least three fund positions.
    Additionally, positions that are part of the JB investment universe are marked with an asterisk.
    As of Q2 2024 positions in structured products & derivatives will also be included.

  • Largest Positions: Shows similar information as the previous page, but about the 10 largest fund positions in the portfolio.
    As of Q2 2024 positions in structured products & derivatives will also be included.

  • Climate Score: Shows a breakdown of the portfolio along the estimated contribution to temperature rise, the portfolio-aggregated estimated temperature rise, as well as the Julius Baer climate score for the five largest positions, and climate score ‘leaders’ and ‘laggards’
    Additionally, positions that are part of the JB investment universe are marked with an asterisk.

  • Climate targets: Shows a breakdown of your portfolio holdings distribution along companies with net-zero targets and targets approved by the Science Based Target initiative (SBTi), as well as the individual footprint equivalent of the portfolio relative to the Paris Agreement-aligned footprint, the global average footprint, and the G7 country average footprints.
