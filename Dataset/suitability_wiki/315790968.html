Technical Failure in Wealth Navigator

This guide describes how you need to proceed in case the document distribution of respective mandatory documents failed in Wealth Navigator.

(relevant for RML CH & EEA)

Step-by-step guide

  • How do I notice in the Advisory Session that client documents were not distributed?

In case document distribution failed, Wealth Navigator informs you when closing the Advisory Session as follows:

  • How do I know which documents are mandatory and which of them have not been distributed?

In the Wealth Navigator History of a client's portfolio you find within the selected Advisory Session  under >DOCUMENTS respective mandatory documents marked.
Additionally, the distribution channel and status of all attached documents to the client packages is provided. 

 


  • What do I need to consider, in case documents were not distributed?

  • How do I need to document the manual provision of the regulatory documents?

It depends on the manner of dispatch you select. The documentation of the manual dispatch is important to create a corresponding evidence that the bank fulfilled the regulatory duties.


a) E-mail

    Create an automatic qualified customer contact note by archiving the sent e-mail including attachments in OnDemand via the archiving function in Outlook (BJBE: archiving in a TAP Contact note).


b) E-banking

    Create a qualified customer contact note in CRM (BJBE: TAP) referring to the technical issue in Wealth Navigator and mention that the documents were sent to the client’s e-banking.


c) Postal delivery or fax

    Create a qualified customer contact note in CRM (BJBE: TAP) referring to the technical issue in Wealth Navigator and archive copies of the sent documents in OnDemand - REGI-Dossier (BJBE: FIO/FileNet).
