RML Switzerland (and RML Guernsey)

In general, the whole process is split into three parts: 

  1. Document ("Documents" on CLM Partner profile)
  2. Client Classification Task ("Client classifications" on CLM Partner profile)
  3. Confirmation Task (after client classification approval only)

4 a) Requesting clarificationThe information in CLM is insufficient (e.g. K&E is insufficient and/or asset information is not sufficient). Please address and remediate the deficiency before resubmitting.
4 b) Rejection (discard request)

In CLM, the RM and Risk Management have the possibility to reject (discard request). Therewith, the pending request will be removed.


Examples: Client has not enough assets, client is not Account Holder (FAQ1), Wrong form was signed and uploaded (FAQ2), etc.

4 c) ApprovalThe request was approved. The new status of the task: Pending notification





4 a)Requesting clarification (action required)
  • Click on View Details
  • Then, you will see the whole process/workflow between RM and Risk Management. Please review the comment and the information provided.
  • Only once suggested action/remediation was performed, resubmit the task (see step 3). Without addressing the deficiency, the task cannot be approved.
  • If K&E profile is insufficient, follow the instructions given in the rejection comment and updated the K&E in a qualified contact according to this guidance.

 

4 b)Rejection where no re-classification/remediating action is possible

Both, the RM and the Risk Management Team has the option to reject (discard request) the case. This will remove the pending request.

Example: If you click on View Details and assess that the client will not meet the asset requirement even after updating CLM Wealth Profile (step 4a), you can click on Reject (discard request) to remove the case.

Further information of reasons for frequent rejections can be be found on FAQ page (specifically: FAQs 1, 2 and 3).


4 c)Approval
  • The client classification was approved. The new status of the task is Pending notification. 
  • After the approval, there is a regulatory duty to inform the client about the new status (see blue box on the right). Proceed with Step 5.
