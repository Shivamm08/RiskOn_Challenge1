Concept

Under MiFID II (UK, LU, IE, ES, IT, PT and CH for clients domiciled in Germany):

  • Product manufacturers must define the potential target market (positive and negative) as well as the distribution strategy for the product, and
  • Prior to distributing the product, distributors must identify the actual target market (positive and negative) within their own client base, and then follow the defined distribution strategy when distributing it, with the overarching objective of ensuring that the product reaches its defined positive target market. This identification of the actual positive and negative target market is an assessment that must performed irrespective of the investment service via which the product is distributed and independently of the suitability or appropriateness test to be performed.
  • This is called the MiFID Target Market Concept.
  • The MiFID Target Market Concept includes the JB Distributor Target Market concept, respectively checks both the MiFID / Manufacturer Target Market and JB Distributor Target Market

Other Locations (CH, GG, AE, BH, ZA, IS, UY, CL) - with BC CH, MC, GG:

  • In other locations there is no regulatory requirement to define a Target Market and check adhere to it, when distributing products. 
  • However, JB has chosen to apply distribution rules on a voluntary basis, guided by risk considerations, as not all financial instruments are equally suitable for every client. 
  • This is called the JB Distributor Target Market Concept
