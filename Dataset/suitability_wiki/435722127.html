Instruction for proper Order Placement of KODA | KODD Transactions within the Advisory Process

Please be advised to strictly adhere to the following three steps which are in detail described in the following:

STEP 1: DIAS SIMULATION SESSION

1.Perform pre-trade DiAS simulation session on both the underlying ETF and the KODA | KODD instrument

2. Correctly resolve all session alerts that may arise and justify alerts accordingly (where applicable). Do not proceed if trade is deemed as unsuitable for the client. Please be advised that execution of an order is prohibited and the session to be canceled in case of a BMO distribution restriction alert.

3. Proceed as normal till step "Order Entry"

4. Do NOT process the order! Proceed to step "Contact Note"

5. In the "Contact Note" under "Additional Notes" remark that the performed session was to simulate the purchase/sell of the ETF via a KODA | KODD. Finish the contact note as usual and send it 

6. From the "History" tab save a screenshot of the DiAS simulation session containing all information

STEP 2: ORDER ENTRY MARKETS

7. Via e-mail with the screenshot as attachment, reach out to stp.advisory@juliusbaer.com to execute the KODA | KODD trade

8. Once the trade is executed, you will receive by e-mail the valor of the KODA | KODD from Markets Platform Sales.

STEP 3:  ORDER ENTRY OTMS | DIAS SESSION

9. Open OTMS and navigate to the tab "SPOS"

10. Under "Open Private Placements" find the traded KODA | KODD

11. Create an order from OTMS as per usual and move to DiAS session to be performed now on the instrument (KODA/ KODD) transaction

12. As per usual complete the steps in DiAS (incl. proper alert handling and provision of disclosure documents) until step "Order Entry". Process the order at step "Order Entry" and move on to "Contact Note"

13. In step "Contact Note" under "Additional Notes" remark that this session was performed for the execution of the KODA/KODD on ETF. Reference here the Session ID from the simulation session performed previously on the underlying ETF and the KODA | KODD instrument (Session ID can be found in the "History" tab, )


14. Send the "Contact Note"

15. All email exchange with Division Markets incl. respective attachments is to be archived in OnDemand.
