Factual Information

https://wiki.juliusbaer.com/spaces/fkh/pages/368744771/Factual+Information

Overview

  • When dealing with instruments for which JB does not provide a view, you can only provide your client factual information
  • Factual information is that which has been made available to the general public, such as corporate news, financial filings and market prices of instruments
  • This information should only be provided upon client request – if the Bank initiates the discussion it could be considered as advice

What counts as factual information

  • The financial statements, or corporate news regarding a certain company
  • The analyst consensus view (rating & target price) for a certain stock
  • Ratios derived from the financial statements (e.g. price/earnings)
  • Publicly available information on the characteristics of an instrument, without expressing a view on future performance (e.g., credit rating, maturity, bid/ask of a bond)
  • Legal documents (e.g. KID, factsheet, prospectus) for an instrument
  • Technical analysis if it only concerns price trends and no trading recommendation.
  • Client education material (for K&E)

Note: only provide this upon client request & to the extent of their request. Otherwise it may count as advice.

  1. Even factual information, if provided without a specific request, or by highlighting specific information could be considered as advice – especially as clients have an advisory mandate in place, which implies that BJB provides clients personalised investment advice. Therefore, it is important to document these client requests and to act only to the extent of what the client has requested.

What counts as investment advice:

  • Recommendation to buy/increase, hold, reduce/sell a financial instrument
  • Expressing an opinion about an instrument 
  • Making a selection of instruments, based on the client’s preferences /profile (e.g. selecting only a few bonds from PSP)
  • Providing more information than what the client requested
  • Providing information from the Bank’s initiative without a documented client request (i.e. if a client asks you to provide next quarter’s financials once released, make sure you document it)

Factual information on PSP

Instrument-related information on PSP is factual information.
