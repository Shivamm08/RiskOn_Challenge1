Research Material, Marketing Material

Sharing Research with a client and client wants to invest

Research is not considered investment advice. If research is shared without any reference to the client’s needs/portfolio, the subsequent transaction is considered unsolicited.

Forwarding Marketing Material to a client without reference to the client’s needs/portfolio

Marketing is not considered investment advice. If Marketing is shared without any reference to the client’s needs/portfolio, the subsequent transaction is considered unsolicited.

Forwarding Marketing Material to client with reference to the client’s needs/portfolio, e.g. “could be interesting for you”, “this would further diversify your portfolio”

Marketing is not considered investment advice. However, if Marketing Material is shared with a reference to the client’s portfolio or needs it is considered actively solicited. Please note that the call of action must “I recommend to buy” not be explicitly expressed.

Active solicitation becomes reverse solicitation

RM recommends an investment and client wants to change the investment amount (client wants to increase the amount and investment becomes unsuitable)

In case the client changes investment parameters (amount/underlying), the actively solicited investment becomes reverse solicited. Please note, that suitability checks need to be performed against and depending on the result the RM might need to advice against the investment. 

Sharing lists of financial instruments

Sharing a list with all US-Treasury-Bonds maturing in the near term or unfiltered “Top Picks” list

Sharing unfiltered lists is considered unsolicited. As soon as RM makes a selection and shares only a short list of potential bonds, than it is considered investment advice.

It is not allowed at all to share lists which have the remark 'for internal use only'.

Sharing a list with all offered bonds

Sharing unfiltered lists is considered unsolicited. As soon as RM makes a selection and shares only a short list of potential bonds, than it is considered investment advice.

It is not allowed at all to share any lists which have the remark 'for internal use only'.

Sharing a list with 5 selected bonds

Performing a selection and only share specific bonds to one or a selected group of clients is considered actively solicited as it contains an advice element. 

It is not allowed at all to share lists which have the remark 'for internal use only'.

Portfolio monitoring

Informing client about a monitoring alert

Monitoring alerts are considered factual information. If monitoring alerts are shared without any reference to the client’s portfolio/need’s and without any suggestions, the subsequent transaction is considered unsolicited

Informing client about a monitoring alert and propose new financial instrument (switch)

A switch proposal contains a call for action and must therefore be marked as actively solicited, to perform relevant suitability checks

Specified vs. unspecified client request

Client wants to invest in American Tech Shares. RM proposed 2 shares.

Client approaches the RM with a specific investment idea providing 3 criteria's 1) country, 2) industry 3) asset class. Responding to this idea is considered reverse solicited. The provision of 3 criteria's is relevant to create a reverse solicitation scenario, as no active advice is allowed for JB Advice Light Mandates in cross border situations.

The RM may ask open-ended questions to clarify/narrow down the clients request. 

Client wants to invest in a financial instrument on which JB has a negative view. RM informs the client about the neg. view.

Due to the fact, that client has not asked for the RM’s opinion, the RM is solely required to inform the client, that JB has a negative view on this instrument and can mark the transaction as unsolicited. The warning is documented in the PIR/Suitability Report.

Client wants to invest in a financial instrument on which JB has a negative view. RM advices the client against the transaction.

If the RM wants to advice against such a transaction, the RM needs to change the solicitation type to reverse solicited.  Doing this it is documented in the PIR/Suitability Report that the RM advised against the transaction.

Structured products

RM recommends a structured products with 5 underlying's and the client asks to replace one of them.

In case the client changes parameters of initially actively solicited transaction, this is considered reverse solicited.

RM recommends a structured products with 5 underlying's and the client asks to replace one with an out of monitored instrument.

The solicitation type refers to the instrument as a whole and if one underlying is out of monitored, the SP becomes out of monitored. Therefore you are not able to provide any advice incl. advice against. The transaction needs to be flagged as unsolicited.
