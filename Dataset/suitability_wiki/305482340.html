How to update the K&E-levels of an order giver (WN/DiAS, CLM, CRM-systems)

https://wiki.juliusbaer.com/spaces/fkh/pages/305482340/How+to+update+the+K+E-levels+of+an+order+giver+WN+DiAS+CLM+CRM-systems

Introduction


The purpose of detecting the K&E-level of each order giver is to asses the understanding of the general functionalities and especially the risks associated with the respective asset class. 

If an order giver stated on the K&E-form, that he/she does not have sufficient experience in a certain asset class, there is the possibility for the RM/IA to lift the experience after the order giver having executed enough trades within the according asset class.

If an order giver stated on the K&E-form, that she/he does not have sufficient knowledge about a certain asset class, there is the possibility for the RM/IA, to educate the client accordingly so that the knowledge-level can be lifted. Therefore, as a result of a successful education, the order giver’s knowledge can be set to ‘good’. 

As a global JB minimum standard (GMS), a Personal Investment Recommendation can only be provided if the order giver has adequate knowledge of the respective asset class. Neither ‘none’ nor ‘limited’ knowledge is considered to be sufficient to provide a Personal Investment Recommendation.

How to update the knowledge level (K-level)


WN/DiAS supports the RM/IA in the process of updating the knowledge level in the case a client has 'none' or 'limited' knowledge for an asset class for which advice shall be provided (see here for the process via WN/DiAS). Alternatively, this updating process can also be done manually outside of an advisory session in WN/DiAS. 

Important to know: A successful education can only be permanently stored in CLM/any CRM system, if there is already a signed K&E-form in place. For clients/order givers who have never signed a K&E form, a successful education cannot permanently stored in the system: A signed K&E form will be required.

Step-by-step guide for updating the K-level outside of an advisory session in WN/DiAS

Step 1:  Update knowledge of the order giver


Existing knowledge = none or limited

If the order giver has no knowledge at all, education has to be provided by going through the respective client education material with the order giver by phone or on the occasion of a meeting (‘live’ education). Education in form of provision of education material by mail or e-mail solely is not applicable in this case.

The appropriate education material can be found .


Step 2: Documentation of the provided education

A client contact note has to be created in CRM/CLM containing the following information
at least:

  • in which instrument group the client has been educated
  • how the client has been educated (phone, meeting, mail/e-mail)
  • a confirmation that the required education has been provided and the order giver has understood and no further questions remain.

Confirming client education means:

  • that the order giver understands the content of the respective education document
  • that the order giver has been made aware that its knowledge level on the respective instrument will be set to ‘good’


Step 3: Amendment of the change in the knowledge level of CLM

The change in the order giver’s knowledge level has to be set to ‘good’ in CLM manually.
Path: Partner Level → Investment Profile → Knowledge and Experience 

How to update the experience level (E-level)


Within a WN/DiAS session, there is no possibility to directly update the E-level - in case of a low E-level, the RM/IA needs to either warn the order giver (unsolicited) or to consider this circumstance within the advice. 

Step-by-step guide for updating the E-level

Step 1:  Update experience of the order giver


Existing experience = 'not at all' or 'less than 5 times'

If the order giver has no experience at all or traded less than 5 times over the past 3 years, the according level can be increased if the minimum number of trades within the according asset class have been executed with invested amounts of more than CHF/USD/EUR 10'000. 


Step 2: Documentation of the amendment of the E-level

A client contact note has to be created in CRM/CLM containing the following information
at least:

  • Discussion with the order giver to increase the E-level (qualified contact)
  • Which trades in which size led to the fact to increase the level > trades do not need to be executed within JB - the holistic experience of the order giver is crucial


Step 3: Amendment of the change in the experience level

The change in the order giver’s experience level has to be set to ‘less than 5 times' or '5 or more times' in CLM manually
Path: Partner Level → Investment Profile → Knowledge and Experience
