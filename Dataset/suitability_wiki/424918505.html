STEP 1: BASIC ASSESSMENT

Please ensure that there is no conflicting information on the form (e.g., a client stating ample experience in a product category but no corresponding knowledge) or conflicting information that you already have from the client (e.g., a client previously stating that she/he has traded many Hedge Funds but states to have no knowledge).

If the client declares max. “good” knowledge in basic K&E categories (the ones listed below) and "none" or only "limited" in any other category, and in the absence of any other conflicting information, the assessment can be finished. Continue to step 3. 

  • Money Market Instruments
  • Foreign Exchange 
  • Fixed Income: Non-Investment Grade Bonds
  • Fixed Income: Investment Grade Bonds
  • Equity Instruments
  • Traditional Funds
  • Structured Products: principal protected Products
  • Derivatives: Forwards and Swaps

STEP 2: INCREASED FOCUS ASSESSMENT

For the following categories, which bear by the nature of the product a higher risk and complexity, an additional assessment must be done, if the client states that she/he has “good” knowledge:

  • Fixed Income: Complex Bonds
  • Real Estate and Real Estate Investment Trusts
  • Non-Traditional Funds
  • Private Equity and Private Equity Funds
  • Structured Products: non-principal protected (Yield enhancement and participation products)
  • Structured Products: leveraged
  • Derivatives: Exchange Traded Derivatives (ETD)
  • Derivatives: OTC Derivatives
  • Derivatives: Accumulators, Decumulators, Target accrual redemption forwards (TARFs)
  • Commodities
  • Digital Assets

The following elements can be considered for this assessment:

  1. The client’s profession and/or education provide sufficient evidence that the knowledge level “good” is plausible. However, the client’s actual position or former profession must include experience in investment instruments and financial markets for being a reasonable rationale. Please note that not any position in a financial institution does automatically qualify for good knowledge.
  2. The RM has served the client already in the past and knows based on previous experience that the client has good knowledge.
  3. The RM asks specific questions about the risks and characteristics of financial instruments and receives adequate answers that proof the client’s knowledge level. To support this assessment, please see sample questions at the end of this article.

Please ask the client to indicate the according main source(s) on the 2nd page of the K&E-Form by crossing 1 out of the 4 boxes.

The goal of that assessment is to verify that it is plausible that the client/partner has good knowledge. The European regulation clearly requires financial institutions to not solely rely on the client’s self-declaration.

HOW TO PROCEED IF THE ASSESSMENT LEADS TO DOWNGRADING THE KNOWLEDGE?

If the client’s knowledge level is not sufficient (e.g., a discussion about risks and characteristics of certain product categories shows knowhow gaps), suggest downgrading to “limited” to ensure that the client receives additional warnings/education when she/he trades the respective product. In a client meeting, you can correct the form before the client signs it. For distant communication the following must be done: 

  • Change the signed form and inform the client about the changes on a recorded channel (phone or e-mail) AND document the outcome in the contact note -> see next step 3

STEP 3: DOCUMENTATION OF THE ASSESSMENT

Once the K&E form is complete and the client has signed the form, you must document the assessment in a contact note. A contact note reference must also be captured on the form before sending it to CDS (u-number, date and time of contact note). Note: Capture the u-Number of the RM on the form who has performed the assessment even if the contact note is captured by the RMA. This contact note reference replaces the RM signature going forward. Forms without filled in contact note reference will be returned by CDS (for avoidance of doubt, the contact note itself must not be handed in together with the form). 

For new account openings: Since the account is not yet opened, you can not yet capture the contact note. The following process is to be applied: Note the date of the contact where the RM has obtained and assessed the K&E on the form with the respective u-number of the RM. Once the account is opened, please fill in the contact note with the required content of the assessment. Bear in mind that this is an important documentation requirement and also subject to the control framework.



Examples of sufficient documentation in contact note (choose contact type “CIP and KE assessment/renewal”):

K&E Categories -> only basic 

K&E Categories -> with increased focus (MIFID)

Profession / previous experience

Sample Questions

I have discussed and assessed the K&E declared by the client and confirm the stated K&E levels per category.

Profession/education: 

I have discussed and assessed the K&E declared by the client. The good knowledge in categories (xyz) is plausible because the client has worked as … in the financial industry for 10 years.

 I have discussed and assessed the K&E declared by the client. The good knowledge in categories (xyz) is plausible because the client holds a CFA degree.

 Previous experience:

I have discussed and assessed the K&E declared by the client. The good knowledge in categories (xyz) is plausible because I had a long-term relationship with this client at (bank…) in which we traded these instruments frequently (4-5 times per quarter).  

No change K&E needed:

I have discussed and assessed the K&E declared by the client. The good knowledge in categories (xyz) is plausible. The client could sufficiently explain the characteristics of the instruments and answer questions on risks to my satisfaction.

 Change of K&E needed

I have discussed and assessed the K&E declared by the client. However, we decided to adjust the knowledge in (xyz) from “good” to “limited” as the client does not yet feel sufficiently familiar with all risks involved. A confirmation is sent via e-mail on dd.mm.yy. 


Additional note to the documentation on the CIP. On the CIP the RM signature is also replaced by a contact note reference. Purpose of this contact note is to confirm that the CIP has been properly (i.e. correctly and completely) filled in and reviewed by the RM. As CIP and K&E are mainly discussed in the same meeting, the confirmation can also be inserted in the same contact note. To verify the CIP please consult the respective checklist: here. 

Example of contact note for the CIP: 

I have discussed the Client Investor Profile with Mrs. X and confirm the consistency, correctness and completeness and validity of the stated values.
