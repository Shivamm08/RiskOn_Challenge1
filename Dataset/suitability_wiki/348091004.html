Basic Screening & ESG Risk Investments

https://wiki.juliusbaer.com/spaces/fkh/pages/348091004/Basic+Screening+ESG+Risk+Investments

The classification of financial instruments through the JB ESG Investment Rating Methodology starts with the basic screening, whose objective is to determine if an instrument poses a critical ESG Risk. If an instrument fails the basic screening, it is classified as an ESG Risk Investment and not analysed further. The indicators used in the screening depend on the asset class. The below description covers Corporates (equities & corporate bonds).

The most important thing to keep in mind is that classification as ESG Risk Investment is triggered if any of the indicators fail.

Certain corporate issuers might be considered an ESG risk, even though the underlying data does not result in an automated ESG Risk classification. This for example can be the case for certain corporate issuers that are considered a severe ESG reputational risk. Here, the Responsible Investment Committee (RIC) has the authority to override the classification of an instrument on a case-by-case basis. The RIC has the authority to both downgrade and upgrade instruments.

In general, any instrument classified as an ESG Risk Investment, will automatically be excluded from all discretionary mandates and will not be allowed to be advised in advisory mandates.

Currently, only a few percent of instruments in our booked universe are considered as an ESG Risk.
