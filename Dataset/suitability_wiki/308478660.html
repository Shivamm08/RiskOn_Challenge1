2. Description of the alert

With the Solicitation-alert the Investment Universe of a financial instrument is shown along with a respective handling instruction. It ensures that only offered financial instruments are recommended to clients.  Please find further information under →Solicitation Framework.   


3. What triggers this alert?

The Solicitation-alert is triggered in the Advisory Session

  1. for advised (actively and reverse solicited) buy transactions, if
  2. the financial instrument is outside of the "offered" Investment Universe

Each investment universe has a dedicated alert in place, which instructs the RM if and how advice can be provided. 


Unsolicited buy transactions are either

  • Covered by the Negative View-alert (→Negative View) or
  • Non-critical alerts (business opportunity)


4. How can this alert be resolved?

You can resolve the alert with one of the following actions:

Advisory Session (session alert)

Solicitation TypeDuty (depending on the status of the Investment Universe)Documentation (Justification / Contact Note)
Actively solicited

RM/IA must not proceed with the transaction as long as the alert is shown, because JB does not have a positive opinion and does not want to recommend this instrument.



In case the alert regards an instrument without an Investment Universe status “None”

RM/IA to check manually with internal experts such as the Instrument Class Owner, if the instrument is "Offered".


Policy exception

Under the conditions stated in policy →CH-G-2083-00 Exemption from the Financial Instruments Solicitation Policy for Investment Advisors v.3  IA may request an exception to the policy CH-2083-00 for financial instruments in the "Ok to Keep" and "Outside of Monitored" Investment Universe. If the exception is granted the IA is allowed to actively advice.

5. Where to find the Investment Universe status of an instrument

The Investment Universe status of an instrument can be found from one of the below-mentioned sources:

a) Equities, Bonds, Money Market, Funds, Structured Products in subscription or issued: -> Wealth Navigator Advanced Search or from the Products & Services Portal (PSP) product search

b) New Issue Bonds, New Issue Money Market Instruments & IPOs: -> OTMS (for BC CH & GUE) and for selected instruments on the marketing publications (subscribe via the subscription tool)

c) Structured Products in the Derivatives Toolbox:

    • Configure and click “get price”
    • Once the product is priced, the universe status is displayed on the right side, below the payoff

Important: If the Investment Universe status of an instrument is not found in these sources, it should be treated as Out of Monitored.
