What is the background?

Increased regulatory requirements and recent risk findings act as catalysts to strengthen systems support and control framework.

Additionally, in order to retain retrocessions from third party funds in advisory mandates, the Bank must demonstrate that we offer clients the most appropriate share class in their individual context.

Business risks:

  • Client refunds if share class was too expensive

  • Violation of fund distribution agreement

  • Reputational risk for overcharging clients

Regulatory risks:

  • Conflict of interest-related fines

  • Ban of retrocessions in general

  • Banking license at risk

Applicability

The SCSL applies to in-house and third-party investment funds. The SCSL compares share classes within the same investment funds, but not share classes between two different funds. 

How does SCSL work?

How to handle new BUYs / Subscriptions?

  • Actively solicited: RM must select the most appropriate share class
  • Reverse solicited: RM must inform the client if a more appropriate share class is available and advice against the less appropriate share class
  • Unsolicited: RM must inform that there is a more appropriate share class

Alert Handling - what justification option do I have?

In case of a notification you have three options:

  1. Create a new transaction and delete the initial transaction
  2. Select "HOP” in case of data issues (please see respective HOP guidance) or 
  3. Select “Client confirms” (for reverse solicited transactions only)


In case of "client confirms" please select the respective rationale:

  • Client confirms – other -> in case the client is informed that this is not the most appropriate share class but client still wants to buy this one because of (add rational of the client to the comment).
  • Client confirms – Client preferences (distribution) -> in case the client is informed that this is not the most appropriate share class but client still wants to buy this one because of preference (distribution consideration).
  • Client confirms – Client preferences (tax) → in case the client is informed that this is not the most appropriate share class but client still wants to buy this one because of preference (tax consideration).

For further information regarding alert handling go to: Alert Handling - Share Class Selection Logic

What to consider?

  • Retrocession loaded share classes can be advised: but only if they are the most appropriate one according to DiAS. The key is to always check in DiAS.

  • DiAS as a support tool: Before contacting your client to solicit an investment fund, enter the order with the ISIN and quantity in DiAS, it will immediately show which share class is the most suitable for your client.

  • JB Publications & investment amount: The share class mentioned on marketing material is typically the one with the lowest minimum investment amount. Therefore, it may not be the most appropriate one for your client (as the actual investment amount may be high enough to qualify for another share class).

  • Fund Distribution agreements: Please note that retrocessions are only received from funds where JB has a distribution agreement in place.
