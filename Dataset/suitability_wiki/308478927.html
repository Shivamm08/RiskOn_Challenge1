2. What does this alert do?

Checks if the client is overexposed/underexposed in an asset class compared to the tactical asset allocation

  • Monitoring: focus on position
  • Triggered if the allocation of one or more asset classes is outside the currently recommended bandwidth

The Tactical Asset Allocation (TAA) is a sub-check of the SAA risk notification. Its purpose is to show deviations (+/-10% or capped at SAA upper or lower band) from the bank’s current tactical positioning. The TAA check is not communicated via email and only displayed in the Wealth Navigator and the client output.
