General definition of Investment Advice

  • Investment advice refers to personalized recommendations provided to a client regarding one or more financial instrument transactions. These recommendations can be made either
    • at the client's request (reverse solicitation) or
    • at the initiative of the bank's client-facing staff (active solicitation)
  • A recommendation constitutes investment advice if it involves a specific call to action, such as buying or increasing positions, holding, selling or reducing positions, switching from one financial instrument to another, or advising against a particular course of action suggested by the client
  • To clarify, client-facing staff who simply forward general marketing materials or research reports prepared by the bank to a client are not providing investment advice, as long as they do not reference the client's individual portfolio or provide additional guidance on specific actions to take. This distinction applies only to materials prepared by the bank for general distribution, as opposed to customized documentation created specifically for one or more clients
