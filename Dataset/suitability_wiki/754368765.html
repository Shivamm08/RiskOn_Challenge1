How do I raise a KRI Scorecard challenge?

Reach out to your business support or business management team to initiate the challenge process.

  1. Open the Challenge form and check if your challenge is a ‘standard case’ in the List of Standard Cases
  2. Complete the form and attach the necessary evidence. Standard cases can be raised any time during the year. 
    Initiate the standard challenge process as soon as possible to avoid potential bottlenecks towards year-end.
  3. Submit the form to the next approver (in line with the approvals on p.2) 

More information on the challenge process and as well on the form and timeline can be found on the official KRI Scorecard intranet page: Intranet - KRI scorecard

What is an accepted KRI Scorecard "Standard Cases"?

‘Standard’ cases are pre-defined cases for each KRI Scorecard that are accepted during the year and corrected in the subsequent upload, provided the necessary approvals and evidence 
are obtained.
