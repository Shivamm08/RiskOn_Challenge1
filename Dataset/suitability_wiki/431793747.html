Assessing the client’s knowledge and experience (K&E) level in terms of investment instruments is the centerpiece of any investor protection regulation globally. Understanding and considering the client’s knowhow in financial instruments is crucial to prevent the client from taking unintended risks and to avoid misconceptions.


WE ALREADY CAPTURE K&E - WHY DO WE NEED A CHANGE?

In the past years new products and asset classes with new characteristics and risks have emerged. Therefore, also the regulators (e.g., ESMA) request financial institutions to apply a more granular approach when assessing and documenting the K&E levels of a client. To fulfil the regulatory demand on one hand and to consider the changed investment landscape on the other the bank has decided to apply a new global minimum standard and gradually introduce a new K&E form.

The following guideline explains the changes and impacts going live from Nov 20, 2023. 


Applicability of this guideline: The processes described in this guidance apply to all RMLs (except RML UK) booking into Booking Center Switzerland and Guernsey. The processes cover the forms, CLM and DiAS. For locations that do not yet use DiAS, only the forms changes might be applicable. Where any processes deviate or are only applicable for some RMLs, the exceptions are specifically highlighted.
