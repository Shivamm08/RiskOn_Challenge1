4) Education of a client in and outside a DiAS session

https://wiki.juliusbaer.com/spaces/fkh/pages/431793783/4+Education+of+a+client+in+and+outside+a+DiAS+session

When an order giver has no or only limited Knowledge in an instrument category, the order giver needs to be warned that the knowledge is insufficient (unsolicited orders) or educated on the impacted instrument category (solicited orders). 

Education involves explaining the risks and characteristics of the affected category to the order giver and additionally sending the respective information material (client education material – link). Sending of the client education material only for self-study is not sufficient.

EDUCATION DURING A DIAS SESSION: 

DiAS highlights any missing Knowledge in the Session. If you are educating a client/partner during a DiAS Session, the education also consists of explaining the risks and characteristics of the affected category to the client/partner and additionally sending the respective information material. DiAS further provides an opportunity to confirm the Education.

In the first step, the RM receives a warning that no recommendation must be made before ensuring that the client/partner is educated. Please be informed that after this education the Knowledge will be updated permanently to “good”:

Note: As already today, there is also an eduction triggered if no K&E information of the order giver is available (due to missing form). In such cases the education is not stored in CLM but a completed form from the order giver is required.

As second step, the education material must be sent together with the further mandatory documents (if any) to the client. 

Afterwards, the successful education can be confirmed in DiAS:

DOCUMENTATION

If the successful education is confirmed in DiAS, no new K&E form has to be obtained from the partner (except for locations mentioned below) but the education that took place during the session must be duly documented in the contact note at the end of the session mentioning the following attributes:

  • In which instrument class the partner has been educated
  • How the partner has been educated (phone/mail/meeting etc.)
  • Confirmation of the required education and that the partner has understood the content including the permanent update to “good” in our files


Example: Mr. XY called and was interested in Barrier-Reverse Convertibles on ABC, however as he has only limited knowledge in non-principal protected Structured Products, I have explained the risks and characteristics of this asset category and transmitted the education material by e-mail. He confirmed that he understood and has not additional questions. I informed him that I will also update the Knowledge&Experience in his profile to good, to which he agreed during the conversation.

EDUCATION OUTSIDE A DIAS SESSION: 

If the RM is discussing new instrument categories with an order giver that has not yet good Knowledge in this new instrument category, outside a DiAS session, the education must be part of this conversation. After the successful education (including hand over of the Education material), the K&E can be updated in CLM (as per existing process - where required via CUR manager):

Path: Partner level -> Investment profile -> Knowledge & Experience

The conversation including the outcome and upgrade needs to be documented in a contact note (see example above). See here for more details. 

Best practice: If an order giver repeatedly places orders for instruments, belonging to a category in which he/she has insufficient knowledge and/or experience stated in our files, it is beneficial to assess, if the order giver has obtained knowledge and experience in the meantime and if an increase the levels (following beforementioned process) should be considered. 

Exception applicable to Advisory offices: Dubai, Bahrain, Qatar, South Africa and Chile

 Due to local requirements the update of a K&E (in and outside of DiAS) always requires a new signed K&E form. Please ensure, that you obtain a new K&E form after an update via DiAS or CLM reflecting the most actual K&E level of a partner.
