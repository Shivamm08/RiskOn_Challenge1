Knowledge & Experience form - "For internal use only" checklist

https://wiki.juliusbaer.com/spaces/fkh/pages/647212482/Knowledge+Experience+form+-+For+internal+use+only+checklist

The Knowledge & Experience (K&E) form contains a “For internal use only” box on the last page to be used by the Front to document the assessment and validation of the data declared by potential order givers (“POGs”) without the need to create a client contact note “CCN” for that. For further information on that please check the Suitability Wiki page on the MIFID Standard (applicable to RML CH / GG for EEA/UK-domiciled clients). 

Tutorial video: How to fill out the assessment & validation checklist of the K&E form?


1.Basic Assessment

(You must tick this box in any case, and in respect of both complex* financial instrument types and non-complex* financial instrument types, regardless of level of knowledge declared.)

5 incomplete Tick this box if there is no conflict information in the K&E form (e.g. the partner declared ample experience in a financial instrument type but no corresponding knowledge; or e. g. you know the partner has experience in a financial instrument type, and yet (s)he declared no knowledge or experience).

2.Increased Focus Assessment (applicable only to "full MiFID" and "MiFID add-on" clients)

(Attention!) Box to be ticked only if (1) the partner declared "good" knowledge in any of the complex* financial instrument types and (2) at least one of the below boxes is ticked.)

6 incomplete 2a. (Attention!) Tick this box if the partner's educational and professional background, as reflected in his/her KYC data, validates the declared "good" knowledge. 7 incomplete 2b. (Attention!) Tick this box if you served the partner in the past and, based on his/her experience, you confirm the "good" knowledge. 8 incomplete 2c. (Attention!) Tick this box if you asked the partner the sample questions here on the asset class(es) in question and, given the correct answers, you can confirm his/her understanding of the characteristics and risks of such asset class(es), and therefore the "good" knowledge.

3.Tick this box if the source of knowledge and experience declared by the partner in section 2 of the K&E form is consistent with his/her KYC data.
