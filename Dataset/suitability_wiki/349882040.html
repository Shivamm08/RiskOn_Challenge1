Ensuring an issuer/company is not on the JB Blacklist is another check which is part of the basic screening within the JB Sustainable Investment Rating Methodology. Companies on the Blacklist are mainly those involved in the production or sale of controversial weapons such as nuclear, biological and chemical weapons (it also includes controversial weapons such as cluster munitions).

The Blacklist is aligned with external research such as e.g. the ‘exclusion list’ of the Swiss Association for Responsible Investments and/or other published lists. Furthermore, companies which are part of a sanction or embargo list also are considered blacklisted. Any blacklisted company is automatically considered an ESG risk. This decision cannot be overruled.
