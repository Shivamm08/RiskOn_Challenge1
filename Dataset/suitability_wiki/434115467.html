1. What is DAC?

DAC= Direct Access to Markets enables an offering for professional clients to directly contact the market specialists on the trading floor. 

DAC Service at BJBE is only possible on a pure execution-only basis, means no advice is allowed by the Markets trader. 

DAC is only allowed for professional clients (client classification according to MIFID II: professional or eligible counterparty) and the minimum AuM amounts must be fulfilled. More details can be found in the document under point 2.
