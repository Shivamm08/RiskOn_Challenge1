Key Objectives

CSU-01 CIP& K&E

 

 

  • A complete CIP exists (advisory service model, discretionary service model)
  • A complete K&E for each order giver exists (advisory service model, discretionary service model, execution only service model)
  • CIP was periodically reviewed at least every two years (advisory  service model, discretionary service model)

CIP Review

  • verifies that the min. bi-annual CIP review process has been properly performed

Availability of Client Investment Profile / K&E form for order givers

  • Availability of CIP is ensured
  • Availability of K&E form for order givers is ensured

+ Location specific key controls

  • Verifies adherence to additional local suitability requirements

CSU-02
SUITABLE INVESTMENT ADVICE & DOCUMENTATION OF INVESTMENT ADVICE AND ACTIONS

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

Advised transactions

  • Control that Advice is suitable and appropriate including provision of relevant client disclosure documents and adequate documentation

Scope: advisory service model

Non-advised transactions

  • Control that for unsolicited transactions no advice was provided including adequate documentation thereof

Scope: advisory service model, execution only service mode

Portfolio Monitoring actions

Portfolio Monitoring alerts and Portfolio Configuration changes are disclosed to the client and adequately documented

Scope: advisory service model

DIAS session alert justification

  • verifies the quality of the risk notification documentation for business-critical alerts regarding comprehensibility and material correctness of the justification type and comment.

Knowledge & Experience

  • Verifies correct K&E alert handling for advised and unsolicited transactions

Regulatory Documents

  • verifies adherence to the regulatory requirements by checking whether the correct necessary documents were provided to the order giver in time, where it is not automatically ensured by DiAS

Transaction flagging

  • Verifies that the solicitation scenario in DiAS correspond to the documentation of the trade and that in unsolicited cases no advice was provided

Quality control on DiAS monitoring (overnight) alerts

  • Verifies adequate documentation of suitability monitoring alerts within portfolio

Suitability and appropriateness outside DiAS

  • verifies adherence to the “DiAS fallback scenario” respectively to the manual performance and documentation of required regulatory, suitability checks for transactions that were directly performed outside DIAS and that are not subject to any exception for DiAS usage.
  • Verifies if respective DIAS session was performed for all trades placed with Markets directly

DIAS Portfolio Configuration Changes

  • Verifies appropriateness and proportionality of portfolio configuration change and that the client was informed in written about changes.
  • Further it assures completeness of the documentation

+ Local specific key controls

  • Verifies adherence to additional local suitability requirements

CSU-03 CLIENT CLASSIFICATION 

 

 

Control that

  • All clients are classified correctly according to local client classification requirements
  • Client Classification was periodically reviewed according to local policy and/or local requirements

For locations with third party booked assets: Third party booked assets are in scope of the GMC

Client classification approval

  • All clients are classified correctly according to local client classification requirements

Client Classification Review

  • Client Classification was periodically reviewed according to local policy and/or local requirements

+ Local specific key controls

  • Verifies adherence to additional local suitability requirements
