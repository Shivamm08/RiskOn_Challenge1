Bond Rating Threshold

  • The Minimum Bond Rating has been changed from BBB-to B.
  • I notified the account holder that several bonds in the portfolio are rated below the minimum bond threshold BBB-
  • The account holder decided to hold these bonds and requested me to decrease the minimum bond threshold to B
  • Rationale: He noted that he plans to keep investing in low-rated bonds due to the low yield environment
  • The Minimum Bond Rating has been changed from BBB-to B
  • As documented earlier in the resolution note on [date, time], the account holder requested me to decrease the minimum bond threshold
  • Rationale: He is planning to increase exposure in lower rated USD-denominated bonds

Issuer Concentration Risk

  • As documented in the resolution note on [date, time], it was agreed with the account holder to increase the issuer concentration risk threshold to 30%
  • The account holder also acknowledged that the change will apply to all issuers going forward
  • Rationale: she informed me that she is comfortable with higher concentrations to single issuers and does not want to be informed if a concentration is 30% or less
  • The account holder wants to decrease the risk in his portfolio by rotating from equities and corporate bonds into US treasury bills, notes and bonds and therefore have a 30% concentration in one issuer
  • I informed the account holder about the issuer concentration threshold of 20% and he requested that I change it to 35%. I informed him that this will apply to all issuers, which he acknowledged
  • Rationale: Client believes that risk assets will go down in the short term. He considers US treasuries to be free from default risk

Strategic Asset Allocation Exposure

  • The client requested me to increase the maximum bandwidth for fixed income to [x%]
  • As the client prefers high yield bonds, changing the strategy of the mandate is not feasible, because of the high Product Risk Rating associated with these instruments
  • The client requested to change the bandwidths for fixed income to [min. 10%, max 50%] and alternative investments to [min. 0%, max. 50%]
  • He has identified interesting opportunities in those asset classes and also wants to decrease his exposure to equities
Loan Configuration
  • The client has a loan of 1 Mio. in his/her advisory but only reinvests 500k, the other 500k are used for other liquidity needs.
  • I have notified the client that I will document this decision in our systems and going forward only 500k are considered for the leverage calculation.
  • The client understands the impact and consequences and we agreed to set the expiry date to the end of the duration of the loan (6 month) and reconsider again when rolling over the loan
  • The client has a loan of 1 Mio. in portfolio xxx but the loan is not reinvested in this portfolio but in his/her other advisory portfolio zzz.
  • I have informed the client that for such cases we can not consider the loan for the leverage calculation systematically and I recommended to book the loan in the same portfolio where it is reinvested.
  • The client understands the limitation but prefers to keep the loan separately booked in portfolio xxx, acknowledging that the leverage effect and increased risk exposure in advisory portfolio zzz  cannot be systematically considered.
  • I adjust the loan configuration in portfolio xxx for the agreed 12 month.
