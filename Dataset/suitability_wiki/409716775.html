1. General Information

All ETD orders (exchange traded Options and Futures) are entered via Wealth Navigator/TAP and routed to JB CH, from there via UBS SisKey to UBS. UBS transmits all BJBE orders to the relevant exchanges (e.g. EUREX, CME) for execution. 

2. Block of order entry for not trained RM, RMAs - requirement of an ETD training 

Order entry for RM/RMAs is only possible if an ETD training has been completed (a block will be implemented as soon as the current trainings have been performed). If you would like to be activated for TAP order entry, please follow the next steps. 

In addition, please also note that order entry is no longer possible if you have not entered any ETD orders for 12 months. Order entry is blocked in these cases.

For RM/RMAs to be activated for order entry in TAP - for those who have not participated in the current trainings:  

  • Order entry is only possible if an ETD training has been passed
  • Training contains of 2 parts: a.) ETD Knowledge Training and b.) TAP order entry, regulatory and contractual requirements
  • Please watch the video recording of the 2 parts and sent an e-mail to IWMS EuroHub that you have watched and understood the video recordings

3. Which ETD instruments are tradable and which ETDs need special approvals? 

In general the following products according to the JBG-G-2060-00 can be traded:

JBG-G-2060-00 Approval Exchanges, Accepted and Prohibited Asset Classes & Product Types v.3 (navexone.eu)

However there are some local specifics which must be regarded: 

  • Commodity Futures/Options with physical delivery:  Commodity Futures with physical delivery (e.g. oil, copper, coffee) are in general not allowed and need a special exemption approval by BoMD of BJBE.  For the BoMD approval a valid business case must be given. Physical delivery of the underlying must be avoided in any case (also in case of physical gold). Please choose if possible a contract with cash-settlement instead of physical delivery. 
  • Futures and Options in Cryptocurrencies (e.g. at CME): Are not allowed. There is no exemption approval possible. 
  • "Exotic" ETD underlyings: must be pre-checked with Eurohub Markets/IWMS

6. Manual Provision of PRIIP/KID - not supported by Wealth Navigator 

Wealth Navigator does not automatically create nor add the PRIIP/KID to the client package. Hence, the PRIIP/KIDs have to be provided manually to the client. 

Since 21 of May, the pre-trade Cost disclosure will be automatically generated by Wealth Navigator. In case you should receive a notification in Wealth Navigator that the "pre-trade cost disclosure is not possible" please get in contact with bfs. 


Please follow the described steps - only required for PRIIP/KIDs: 

  • If your client expressively agrees, then you can agree on an annual process.

           This means that the client receives - before the first trade - all required documents and these documents have to be refreshed every 12 months at the latest. The client has to confirm to this agreement in writing.

           The RM/RMA/IA has to document this in TAP and has also to ensure that the whole correspondence with the client is properly archived.

           The RM/RMA/IA has to monitor this refresher requirement manually and on your own responsibility. 

          If your client should not agree to this annual process, then you have to provide the PRIIP/KID before each trade. You have to manually upload the PRIIP/KID within the Wealth Navigator session.

7. Limitation of Futures for retail clients domiciled in Germany - only allowed for hedging purposes

  • from 01 January 2023, the German regulator has restricted futures to retail clients domiciled in Germany
  • Means all retail clients domiciled in Germany Futures are only allowed to trade futures for hedging purposes 
  • The client must give before each transaction the confirmation that the future is for hedging purposes. 
  • The RM/IA has to properly document the hedging purposes of the client within the Wealth Navigator session
  • New administrative act can be followed under the following link:  BaFin - Press release - General Administrative Act – Product intervention regarding Futures

8. No expiration report is provided for futures

Please regard that you  do not receive any automatic expiration list for ETD futures.

You soly receive an expiration list for all physically settled commodity futures which are limited and require by the way a special approval by BoMD. So, you have to duly monitor the expiration dates from your side carefully and to initiate the required steps (close, rollover) before the expiration. We recommend to monitor them in Wealth Navigator by checking the alerts on the total overview of all of your clients. You will see the maturity 5 business days before.

9. ETD Error Corrections 

The following process is EXCLUSIVELY applicable for corrections of ETDs transactions

  • RM/RMA/IA calls BFS team to inform about error trade
  • BFS instructs RM/RMA/IA to cover the error trade via the client account itself (only allowed for ETD trades, all other trade corrections remain with Mistake Team)
  • BFS informs Derivatives Processing Team, Risk Management Luxembourg and all involved departments about the upcoming correction booking
  • Responsibility of RM/RMA/IA:

          - RM/RMA/IA enters the correction trade on the client account in Wealth Navigator/TAP

          - RM/RMA shares its screen with the IA team Luxembourg to ensure 4 eye principle while entering the correction trade → involvement of IA team has to be properly documented in TAP, please also explicitly document the name of the                     Investment Advisor who has ensured with you the 4 eye principle

             In case of a required correction trade of IA team,  IA team shares its screen with BFS → involvement of IA team (also mentioning the name of the BFS colleague who ensured 4 eye principle) has to be properly documented in TAP

              Outside of BJBE business hours (no availability of IA team or BFS team) the 4-eyes principle must be ensured with one of your colleagues and you must properly document it

          - RM/RMA/IA documents the performed correction trade incl. the 4 eye principle in TAP. All documents have to be uploaded.

          - RM/RMA/IA fills out OpRisk form 8-1017 immediately; all required information and approvals according to the form have to be filled out/provided. Op Risk form will be provided to Risk Management Luxembourg

          - If an adjustment of the execution price is required, the RM/RMA/IA has to provide a screenshot from Bloomberg (or any other recognized pricing system).

             Please note that the IA Team Luxembourg will in NO EVENT be liable for any erroneous trade corrections and will in no event assume any profit or loss implications for RM/RMA. If the origin of the error trading lies in the

             IA team, the responsibility for transferring P&L impacts certainly lies with the IA team.

  • Derivatives Processing team performs corrections on client account, correction bookings are visible for the client. Ex-gratia payments are not allowed!
  • Applicable Policy: LUX-2233-00 Operational Risk Incident Policy
