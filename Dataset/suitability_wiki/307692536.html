2. Description of the alert

JB ensures that the investment strategy agreed in the contract (portfolio level) is in line with the overall investment strategy in the Client Investment Profile (CIP). For that, portfolios with a mandate strategy (e.g. Advice Light, Advice Premium etc.) are compared to the strategy documented in the CIP.

For ZRs with multiple portfolios, the comparison is done based on a “Gross market-value-weighted rating": Sum of all positions in portfolio at market value, where position = Quantity x current market price, including accrued interest. Only long (i.e. positive) positions are considered for this value. Market value for negative positions is set to 0 (zero).

The sum of the weighted portfolio ratings is compared to the threshold of the CIP strategy (i.e. the highest value of the CIP strategy range). 

An alert is triggered if the weighted portfolio(s) is/are higher than the CIP strategy threshold.

3. What triggers this alert?

The OIS alert is triggered in the Client Book as a result of overnight portfolio risk monitoring, when the portfolio strategy (or the weighted portfolio rating) is higher than the CIP strategy.

4. How can this alert be resolved?

You can resolve the alert with one of the following actions:


Client Book (overnight portfolio monitoring alert)

DutyResolving the alert & Documentation (Resolution / Contact Note)

RM must inform the client within 5 business days


RM/IA to create an investment proposal to amend the setup of the portfolio constellation. For portfolio shifting simulation purposes, the OIS calculator (see below) can be used. If the client requires time to consider the proposal, the RM/IA can snooze the alert for 30 days. RM/IA to select “investment proposal sent” in the alert justification and document 1) the date when the proposal was sent and 2) the channel in the contact note.

If the client accepts the risk, RM/IA to select "client accepts risk" in the alert justification and document 1) the risks that have been explained (deviation between portfolio and CIP strategy), 2) the client’s decision after the explanation and 3) the client's rationale in the contact note.*

If the client could not be reached, RM/IA to select “client not reached” in the alert justification and document 1) the date when the client was approached and 2) the channel in the contact note.

Alternatively, the client may sign

  • a new or adapted contract with a strategy that is in line with the one of the CIP. To simulate whether the newly chosen strategy is in line with the CIP the OIS calculator (see below) can be used, MOC (Modular Offering Configurator) or MSD (Mandate Solution Designer)
  • an adapted CIP which is in line with the effective risk profile and risk appetite of the client

*Important: For all client contracts signed as of mid-2025 the above-mentioned elements for justification must already be discussed pre-contract - via MSD, MOC or in any CCN. If this was the case, the resolution note of the OIS can refer to the CCN in which this is clearly stated.


Validity of Resolution Notes (RN)

A Resolution Note for the OIS alert is valid until 

  • the snooze period ends or
  • a new transaction increases the deviation between the portfolio and the CIP strategy

Already existing mandate

I informed the client that the investment strategy 'Capital Gain' of their mandate is higher than the strategy of the CIP 'balanced' and explained the risk that this may lead to the fact that higher gains are possible but also higher losses. I therefore recommended to change the strategy of this mandate to 'balanced' and offered a new contract.

The client understands and acknowledged this, but did not want to make any changes of the strategy nor the mandate, because of their core-satellite approach in conjunction with portfolio 01.02 under the same ZR/BR.
