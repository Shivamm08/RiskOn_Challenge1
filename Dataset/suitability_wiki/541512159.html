Scope

  • Applicability: All RMs of Julius Baer Wealth Advisors (India) Private Limited.
  • Accounts in scope: Accounts with distribution service model and advisory service model
  • Key control in scope: The RMs control results of BaerGRC Local Control number - CTRL-0000007942 are sourced (please note that control findings related to the control objective "Review of RM Outgoing Emails" are not considered for the Advisory KRI Scorecard)

Measured parameters

  • Control to check unauthorized advice to Clients
  • Sampling methodology is designed to include all Relationship Managers in a year.
  • Control Process includes sample review of Client Contact Notes and outgoing emails.
  • RMs not included in the sample for a quarter will be rated “Green”.
  • Each case of unauthorized advice will be treated as an exception. Unauthorized advice excludes incidental advice given by RM for Mutual Funds to Distribution clients. Advice for any other product by RM to Distribution clients is an exception. Also, advice for any products by RM to Advisory clients is an exception.
  • An exception will be rated as a “moderate” finding for the purpose of GMIS reporting.
  • In exceptional cases, where the exception is found to be severe, India CRO may rate an exception as “major”.

Measurement period

  • Monthly
  • Reporting period shifted by 3 months to facilitate review and challenge of control results

Example for GMIS reporting: For trades executed in October, control execution will be in December, Data challenge in January, and GMIS upload in February.

Result calculation methodology

Step 1

  • RMs monthly key control test execution results are weighted (higher weight for more critical defects), i.e.
    • No findings = pass
    • Exceptions = moderate
    • Severe findings (decision of CRO) = major
  • The weighted score is ‘represented’ as a percentage vs. worst possible score
  • ‘Pass’ findings weighted ‘0’
  • The worst possible score equals the total no. of controls executed per RM times the max weight (i.e. weight for major defects)

Challenge process

Control execution (CE) + 7 days

Compliance sends data regarding exceptions to the concerned RM

CE + 21 days

RM completes the Challenge form, attaches the necessary evidence, and submits the form to Compliance

CE + 30 days

Compliance reviews and challenge and communicates final decision

 

Examples of challenge cases

  • Uploaded data was wrong
  • Account inherited by RM with deficiencies: Evidence such as emails or system report to support data of inheritance
  • Long absence of RM
  • Disagreement of assessment as unauthorized advice
  • Any other
