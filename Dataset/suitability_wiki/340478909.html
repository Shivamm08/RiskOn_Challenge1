Where do I start my order?

https://wiki.juliusbaer.com/spaces/fkh/pages/340478909/Where+do+I+start+my+order

In Wealth Navigator or in another Order Entry Tool (depending on the financial instrument)


Special case: Private SPOS

Private SPOS can't be sold through Wealth Navigator nor OTMS. 
The issuer needs to place the order in Host or via Stp Execution team.
However, from a suitability perspective, the Wealth Navigator fallback guidance is applicable in such cases: PowerPoint Presentation. In a nutshell for sell orders this means:

  • Enter the order
  • Capture a contact note and archive any evidence (e.g. a mail if applicable)
  • If advice is provided to sell the instrument (what I would not assume): additionally a suitability statement must be provided and/or archived

Hence for pure execution (unsolicited), no additional checks are required from a suitability standpoint.
