SCOPE & APPLICABILITY

To ensure compliance with the current market access for RML CH to Germany (subject to so -called ‘Standard Freistellung’), clients with account holder domicile Germany need to be served applying a full MiFID approach (same as with the EEA locations). This also includes the recording of telephone conversations, about which we must inform the relevant clients for legal reasons.

This scope defines the “relevant users” to which this guidance is applicable, i.e. all RMs /ARMs (including deputies) who have contact with Germany-domiciled clients or any Investment Advisor. The term “relevant user” is used throughout the guidance to facilitate the reading.

In Booking Center Switzerland (RML CH), Phone recording of 'relevant users' is enabled for the following use cases:

  • All incoming/outgoing calls from and to Germany (area code +49)
  • All incoming calls to your landline phone where the counterparty calls with a masked number

Affected phone calls are recorded on an ‘always on’ basis.

Investment Advisors have a permanent recording not limited to Germany domiciled clients, i.e. all calls are recorded. 

Attention: The recording needs to be activated. Depending on your role as (A)RM or IA, the request in ServiceNow is a different one. Select what is applicable to your role.

  • (A)RMs (RML CH) must request landline recording via the ServiceNow request: Voicerec Listener RM CH
  • Investment Advisors must request the following: Voicerec - Listen BJB-XYZ (whereas XYZ corresponds to the location of the IA)


DISCLOSURE DUTY FOR RECORDED CALLS

To be in line with Swiss law (Swiss criminal code forbids any recording of phone calls without consent), the counterparty (clients, prospects, etc.) must be informed that the phone call is recorded. 

Outgoing Calls

Germany-domiciled clients (booked in Switzerland) must be informed that their telephone conversations are recorded. In order to ensure this for existing clients, a one-off information letter ‘Voice Recording’ was sent to all relevant partner roles domiciled in Germany who could potentially have a phone conversation with the Bank (e.g. AH, BO, PoA, holder of parental authority, etc.) in March 2024. These information letters are archived in OnDemand. For new clients from Q2 2024 on, the ‘Voice Recording’ letter has been integrated as a new form (VOICE-REC) in the account opening process (AOF). Consequently, an additional announcement at the beginning of recorded outbound calls is obsolete as it is assumed that the counterpart is aware of it.


In case of a recorded outbound call to another counterparty than a Germany-domiciled client (e.g., where permanent recording is enabled for all calls), it is the callers responsibility to ensure that the counterparty is aware of the call is being recorded. No explicit consent from the counterpart is needed. If the counterpart has been made aware of the fact that the call is being recorded and he/she does not end the call, this is sufficient as implicit consent.


Incoming Calls

For incoming calls, the following location-specific announcement message is played automatically:

  • Relevant user in DE-speaking CH: "Willkommen bei Julius Baer. Dieses Gespräch wird aufgezeichnet. Welcome to Julius Baer. This call will be recorded." (audio file DE/EN)
  • Relevant user in FR-speaking CH: "Bienvenue chez Julius Baer. Cet appel sera enregistré. Welcome to Julius Baer. This call will be recorded." (audio file FR/EN)
  • Relevant user in IT-speaking CH: "La ringraziamo per aver chiamato Julius Baer. La informiamo che questa chiamata verrà registrata. Welcome to Julius Baer. This call will be recorded." (audio file IT/EN)

Please note: If a phone call from a German number is forwarded from a non-recorded to a recorded line BJB-internally, the announcement message cannot be played due to technical constraints and the counterpart needs to be informed verbally, e.g., A client from Germany calls Julius Baer main number (058 888 11 11). The call agent forwards the client to an user whose phone is recorded. In this case, the client briefly needs to be informed by a relevant user that the phone call is recorded from now on.


SPECIAL CASES (EXCEPTIONS)

Business mobile phones

Unless specifically approved, calls via mobile phone number are not recorded. In case of business-related conversations, you must switch to a recorded line (e.g., your landline or “Webex” app[1]). You can also set an automatic call forwarding to your Webex landline phone number (make sure that your mobile phone number is suppressed) and pick up the calls via the recorded “Webex” app line (your landline phone number is configured on your phone and also on all Webex apps) to avoid having to interrupt conversations to switch to a recorded line.

[1] The Webex app technically uses your landline number and thus, these calls are recorded as well (outgoing calls to +49 numbers). Further information on how to activate and use the “Webex” app can be found here.

DO'S AND DON'TS

Do’s

  • For business-related conversations, always use recorded devices.
  • In case a client calls you on the mobile phone (or contacts you via an unauthorized communication channel), you need to switch to a recorded line.
  • Inform your counterparty at the beginning of each outgoing call that the call is recorded.
  • Make sure to timely request the Voice Recording rights for new relevant users[2]. The requests are listed under “scope & applicability” at the beginning of this article. Please note that (A)RMs and IAs have different requests in ServiceNow.

[2] New relevant users: RM /ARM (including deputy) who will have contact with Germany-domiciled clients or any Investment Advisor


Don’ts

  • It is not allowed to use non-recorded channels (Skype, WhatsApp, MS Teams, ZOOM, etc.) for business-related conversations.
  • Private calls on recorded lines should be limited to emergency cases and the caller needs to be made aware of the fact that the call is recorded.

 

GUIDANCE FOR DOCUMENTATION & FURTHER REQUIREMENTS

Whenever you have a phone conversation with your client, make sure to select “Phone” as communication channel in your CRM / DiAS contact note. Be careful: “Phone” is the default value when entering a contact note in DiAS, i.e. ensure at all times to select the correct channel to avoid deficiencies of contact types.

Furthermore, always document the content, the time of the call (including time zone) as well as the clients (counterparty) phone number to enable the link to the respective voice log. Specify accordingly if the call was performed via Webex.

Always make sure to use phone numbers registered in CRM and update CRM accordingly in case of new client contact data.

 

RECORD KEEPING & ACCESS

The recorded conversations may be used as evidence to clarify any differences that may have arisen during a call with a counterparty or client. Moreover, these recordings may also be used to clarify matters in the context of internal control activities, official investigations or for the purposes of fraud prevention, detection, and trainings as well as to be compliant with the industry’s good practice and applicable laws and regulations. 

Recorded calls will be kept for five years and are deleted automatically if not marked (e.g. for investigation purposes).

Every employee has access to his/her own calls that have been recorded. Recorded calls can be accessed in the application “POWERplay”. Information about how to search for and listen to calls can be found in the Listener Guide. As a relevant user, you can only listen but not download your own calls. Further information can be found here.


In addition to that, selected employees may have access to all recorded calls for control purposes (so-called “Privileged Listeners”).

In case of investigations/audits, CRO employees or auditors can request for an ad hoc access to selected calls (e.g. all incoming and outgoing calls from a certain number between date x and date y). Such a request must first be approved by Legal HR and afterwards forwarded to Information Security (cfc@juliusbaer.com) – by using the form "Request for Listening of recorded Telephone Conversations". If the request is approved, the Information Security team assigns the respective calls temporarily to the person who is doing the investigation. Precondition for accessing these calls is the investigator role which can be requested via ServiceNow request "Vrec Investigator". If a certain call needs to be available as audio file, this can be stated in the aforementioned form.

If a superior or colleague of a relevant user needs to have access to a certain phone conversation, the form “Request for Listening of recorded Telephone Conversations” must be completed and handed in with Legal HR for approval. If the request is approved, the Julius Baer Telecom team assigns the respective calls temporarily to the requestor. If a certain call needs to be available as audio file, this can be stated in the aforementioned form.
