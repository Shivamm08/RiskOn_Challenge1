How to amend the issuer concentration

https://wiki.juliusbaer.com/spaces/fkh/pages/509364399/How+to+amend+the+issuer+concentration

The default issuer concentration threshold is 20% per issuer and this practice is also in line with industry standards. Please note that certain jurisdictions even prohibit to amend a threshold (e.g. in BC HK / SG –). In case the client explicitly demands that a higher threshold shall be applied, document the request of the client and the decision accordingly in a contact note. As a principle always mention 1. the request from the client and the threshold, 2. the reason for an adjustment, 3. the risk disclosure towards the client including consequences of the change and 4. the final agreement with the client

 Example:        

The client wants to decrease the risk in her portfolio by rotating from equities and corporate bonds into US treasury bills, notes and bonds and therefore have a 30% concentration in one issuer. I informed her about the issuer concentration risk, and she is well aware. I informed her, that we would not recommend any further instruments in this issuer as our threshold is set to 20%. She asked if this can be changed, and I explained on specific requests this is possible. She requested that I change it to 30%. I informed her that this new threshold will apply to all issuers, which she acknowledged. She believes the US treasuries are low from default risks and would consider resetting the thresholds if she feels uncomfortable in the future.


How is a change done in WN:

1. Enter the Portfolio Configuration Screen from the respective portfolio view:

2. Open the “Portfolio configuration” and stay on the tab “Mandate” and adjust to the desired monitoring value for the issuer concentration. This amendment is not available for every location (e.g. for BC HK/SG no change of the issuer concentration is permitted) If you need to amend additional values in other tabs (e.g. SAA Leeway), continue to adjust all values and only then press “save”.

3. The distribution screen for the Portfolio configuration notification appears where you can configure, preview, and select the language of the client output.

4. The configuration dialogue allows to amend the language again or hide the portfolio number and previews the document. Press “save & close” to continue with the distribution.

5. Select the contact partner and distribution channel to share the document.  The notification must be shared via e-channel but can be simultaneously e-mailed to the RM (e.g. for a client meeting).  

6. In the contact note, the ‘additional notes’ field is already pre-filled with the applied changes. Complete the contact note by adding additional information on describing the client request, see points that need to be documented as well as the example above for guidance. Don’t forget to archive any additional evidence, e.g., full e-mail exchange with the client, accordingly.

Note that the changes are only done if you finish the process by pressing “submit & finish”.
