Background:

  • MAS issued Guidelines on Environmental Risk Management for Banks and Assets Managers.
    • Guidelines-on-Environmental-Risk-Management-for-Banks.pdf (mas.gov.sg)
    • Guidelines on Environmental Risk Management for Asset Managers (mas.gov.sg)


  • HKMA issued Supervisory Policy Manual for Climate Risk Management for Authorised Institutions (AIs).
    • GS-1: Climate Risk Management (hkma.gov.hk)

 

  • MAS and HKMA expect financial institutions to apply guidelines by Jun 30, 2022 and Dec 31, 2022 respectively.
  • The guidelines recommend banks to incorporate and consider environmental risk into their risk management, portfolio construction & group-wide disclosures.
