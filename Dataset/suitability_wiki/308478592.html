2. What constitutes a bond rating drop?

A drop in the rating of a bond by selected rating agencies (the firm will take the worst of bond rating from Moody’s, S&P, Fitch or ZKB) that results in a one or more notches drop in the consolidated bond rating triggers the “Bond Rating Drop Alert” in Wealth Navigator.

3. What actions are necessary by the RM upon receiving a Bond Drop Rating alert?

  1. If the new (lower) rating communicated to the RM remains above investment grade or the minimum bond rating defined by the client, no switch recommendation will be sent to the RM.
  2. In the event, however, the rating drops below (or is already below) the personal rating, an additional risk alert “Bonds with bond rating below client’s threshold” is sent out which contains a switch recommendations (see Alert Bond Rating below Client’s Threshold).
  3. Even if the alert does not trigger a switch, it is good business practice to inform the client about the rating drop and the rationale behind it.
