MiFID II for RML Switzerland, Monaco & Guernsey

https://wiki.juliusbaer.com/spaces/fkh/pages/303982058/MiFID+II+for+RML+Switzerland+Monaco+Guernsey

The above mentioned RM locations introduced a slightly different framework for their EEA clients than RM locations within the EEA did. This means, Switzerland, Monaco & Guernsey follows the so called 'MiFID add-on approach' whereas locations within the EEA introduced a full MiFID framework.  However, for clients domiciled in Germany, a full MiFID approach is also applied in RML CH:
