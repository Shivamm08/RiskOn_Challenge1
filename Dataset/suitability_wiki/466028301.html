2. Description of the alert

ESG stands for environmental (e.g. energy and water consumption), social (e.g. employer appeal and supply chain management) and governance (e.g. remuneration policy and company management).

JB’s sustainable investment rating methodology allows to recognize financial instruments that deem to have a material negative impact on the environment or society, or to bear severe governance-related risks (so-called ESG Risk Investments). The bank excludes such instruments from the offering, which means that no advice is permitted. As this alert combines critical and non-critical elements, the different scenarios are explicitly outlined in the following.


3. What triggers this alert?

An ESG Risk Alert is triggered when a financial instrument is defined as ESG Risk. This may happen either:

a) in the Advisory Session as a result of a newly proposed transaction, or

b) in the Client Book (non-critical only) as a result of overnight portfolio risk monitoring, e.g. due to a change of the financial instrument’s classification

B. Client Book (overnight portfolio monitoring alert)

ESG Risks are displayed as business opportunity (non-critical) and therefore not mandatory to be followed up. However, it is an opportunity to create an investment proposal in Wealth Navigator with a non-ESG risk instrument and to propose a switch.
