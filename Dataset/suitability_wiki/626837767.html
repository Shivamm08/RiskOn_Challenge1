1. Background and alerts in scope

As per the Bank Julius Baer Europe's ("BJBE" or "the Bank") current contractual framework with the client (i.e. see section 1.3 of the "Advice Advanced Agreement" (form 3-1105) and section 1.2 of the "Advice Premium Agreement" (form 3-1104)), portfolio monitoring is done at single mandate level. Nevertheless, BJBE can accommodate clients who wish to monitor their assets across multiple mandates, subject to adherence to the process outlined in the subsequent sections of this procedure. The checks that could be performed (and alerts whose existence or lack thereof would need to be determined) at a consolidated level will include Consolidated Product Risk ("CPR"), Strategic Asset Allocation Exposure ("SAAE"), Concentration Risk on Single Positions ("CR") and Issuer Concentration Risk ("ICR").

Consolidated portfolio monitoring would be applicable for:

a. advisory mandates with different service models and/or investment strategies; 

b. advisory mandates and discretionary portfolio mandate(s) within the same account.

  In case of various advisory mandates with the same service model and investment strategy, the RM should consolidate them in a -901 portfolio in Wealth Navigator. 

N.B.! In case the mandates/account for which the client wishes to have a consolidated view have different types of mandates/service models, the CIP investment strategy of the account should be applied for the consolidated view.

2. Main prerequisites and template letters to be used

The client must request a deviation from the contractually agreed portfolio monitoring at mandate level, stating their wish clearly and in an unambiguous manner to have multiple mandates or the whole account monitored collectively instead, explicitly mentioning which portfolios/account need to be taken into consideration for the consolidation. Then the next steps should be followed:

1. RM completes the “monitoring at consolidated portfolio/account level” template letter below with the related client details and gets it signed by two employees at BJBE.

2. RM sends the signed letter to the client via e-Docs*.

3. RM documents in a TAP contact note the client's initial request to have the portfolio monitoring at multiple mandate/account level.

4. RM notifies Business Risk Management (BRM) about the new client to be included in the Consolidated Portfolio Monitoring Register.

5. Thereafter, RM follows the process in section 3 whenever an overnight alert in the client book (i.e. see section 3A) or a pre-transaction alert (i.e. see section 3B) is triggered in Wealth Navigator.


*In case the client does not have e-Docs, the RM must send the letter via their preferred channel of communication.

3. Process

A. Overnight alert is triggered in the client book. 

The RM reviews whether the alert would be triggered if a consolidated mandate/account level view was applied. 

    1. If no alert* would be triggered in such case, the RM does not have to contact the client, but simply justifies the alert with "client accepts risk" and makes reference to: (1) how the calculation at consolidated level has been done**; and (2) the agreement with the client (i.e. see the example below on alert justification).
    2. If an alert* would be triggered in such case, the RM must resolve the alert as usual by following the guidance in the Suitability Wiki page - "Overnight Wealth Navigator alerts".

Example of alert* justification

"SAAE review at consolidated level has been done by reviewing the TAP report generated for portfolios 1, 2 and 4. No threshold breach according to consolidated view as confirmed to client in writing on 30.05.2025 and documented in FIO”

 
B. Pre-transaction alert triggered in a Wealth Navigator session. 

RM reviews whether the alert* would be triggered if a consolidated mandate/account level view was applied.

    1. If no alert* would be triggered in such case, the RM does not have to warn the client, but simply justifies the alert* with "data exception" and makes reference to: (1) how the calculation at consolidated level has been done**; and (2) the agreement with the client (i.e. see the example above on alert justification).

    2. If an alert* would be triggered in such case, the RM must resolve the alert as usual by following the guidance in the Suitability Wiki depending on the type of alert (i.e. see section 4 for SAAE alerts; see section 3 for the CR alerts; see section 4 for CPR alerts; and see section 4 for ICR alerts).

Example of alert* justification

"CPR calculation at consolidated level has been done by using the XLS CPR calculator. No threshold breach according to consolidated view as confirmed to client in writing on 30.05.2025 and documented in FIO”

*See alerts in scope under section 1 above.

**For the calculation of the CPR at multiple mandate/account level, a screenshot of the calculation of the CPR with the CPR XLS result must be archived in a TAP Contact Note.


N.B.! If a client choses to have the portfolio monitoring at multiple portfolio/account level, the RM must manually check the thresholds defined by Julius Baer on account level.

The details of the related thresholds to be followed can be found here:

  • Strategic Asset Allocation Exposure
  • Consolidated Product Risk
  • Issuer Concentration Risk
  • Concentration risk on single positions
