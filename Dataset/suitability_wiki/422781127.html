Regarding DiAS Hard blocks For Institutional Shares Classes Of Funds Domiciled In Luxembourg

Per Se Professionals / Eligible Counterparties vs Elected Professional Clients


Per Se Professional Clients must at least met one of the following criteria:

  • An entity requiring authorisation or being regulated to operate in the financial markets by virtue of being a credit institution, an investment firm, an
    insurance company, a collective investment scheme or the management company of such a scheme, a pension fund or the management company of a
    pension fund, a commodity derivatives dealer, a local, another authorized or regulated financial institution.
  • A larger undertaking meeting two of the following size requirements on an entity base:
    • A balance sheet total of EUR 20 000 000 or even more
    • Net turnover of EUR 40 000 000 or even more
    • Own funds of EUR 2 000 000 or even more
  • A national or regional government, a public body that manages public debt, a central bank, an international institution or another similar international
    organization.
  • Another institutional investor whose main activities is to invest in financial instruments, including entities dedicated to the securisation of assets or
    other financing transactions.

Elected Professional Clients:


A request for a re-classification as Elected Professional client must always be initiated by the client and must be done in writing. Relationship Managers
are forbidden from promoting opting up.
To classify a client as Elected Professional client, the following criteria must be met (Minimum 2 out of 3):

  • Over the course of a year the client has an average of 10 transactions per calendar quarter
  • Portfolio value exceeds EUR 500’000
  • Client has worked in or has experience in the financial sector for over one year

Eligible Fund Share Classes


Every fund setup in Luxembourg is subject to the taxe d 'abonnement of 5 bps. A share class of a fund may profit of a lower td'a (1bp instead of 5bps) if all clients in such
share class are institutional investors according to the ruling of the Luxembourg tax authorities. The definition of institutional investors is not strict but does not include
private individuals. BJB and its clients are obligated to adhere to this restriction at all times, in particular if a distribution agreement has been signed between the fund provider
an BJB.  

If the private investors are invested into a institutional share class the fund may be subject to the higher tax rates. This may cause compensation complaints by the fund company
to the fund distributor and lead to fines by the Luxembourg tax authority. 

Following industry / market practice (input from Investment Navigator with 12 client banks, incl. global banks like HSBC and local banks like Bank CIC).
For share classes restricted for LU_INST, only the following client types will be allowed:

  • Discretionary Mandate clients
  • MiFID eligible counterparties
  • MiFID & FIDLEG per se professional clients
