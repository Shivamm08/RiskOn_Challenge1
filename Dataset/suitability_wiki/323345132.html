BJBE CIP consistency and quality checklist

https://wiki.juliusbaer.com/spaces/fkh/pages/323345132/BJBE+CIP+consistency+and+quality+checklist

Under MiFID II, BJBE is required to comply with certain key requirements when providing investment services:

  • Collect information about client's K&E, investment objectives and financial situation,
  • Keep high-quality records of agreements with clients,
  • Test suitability and appropriateness ex ante, and
  • Monitor suitability and appropriateness.


To mitigate any potential risks of not complying with them (e.g. getting External Audit findings or CSSF/CNMV/CBI fines, not having reliable documentation of agreements with clients ready in case of litigation by the client, etc.), the following internal control framework is in place:​

  • Compliance as the second line of defense performs a sample-based, CIP quality Baer control on a monthly basis.
  • Internal Audit as the third line of defense performs periodic MiFID-II-related audits.

  • The BJBE Front (that is, RMs and RMAs in Luxembourg, Spain and Ireland) as well as CDS, are required to ensure, respectively, that all CIPs uploaded to FIO are consistent and of high quality as well as duly completed, to ensure compliance with articles 16 (6) and 25 (5) of Directive 2015/65/EU and articles 54 (7), 72 (2) and 73 of Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2017/565. In this regard BJBE Front is responsible for the review of CIP quality and consistency. CDS is responsible for the review of CIP completeness and proper upload to FIO.​


To facilitate the control to be performed by the BJBE Front and CDS, the IWMS, Regulatory Advisory team prepared a CIP consistency and quality checklist for the BJBE Front and the CIP completeness checklist prepared​ for CDS, which are also available in the Suitability Wiki.

We kindly ask the BJBE Front and CDS to go through the checklist with newly renewed CIPs (simply having it as a reference, no need to document every single review with it!) and:

  • ​ If all answers are detected as “YES":
    • By the BJBE Front: Please send the CIP to CDS.
    • By CDS: Please upload the CIP to FIO.


  • If any answer is detected as “NO":
    • By the BJBE Front: Please review the CIP with the client.
    • By CDS: Please send the CIP back to the RM/RMA for correction.
