Products & Services Test

https://wiki.juliusbaer.com/spaces/fkh/pages/774749853/Products+Services+Test

The Products & Services Test applies in addition to the Suitability Assessment (CPR Check) and combines several checks to ensure compliance with local regulatory standards, cross-border and tax rules, contractually agreed obligations and distribution rules.
