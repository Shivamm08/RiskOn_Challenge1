Contractual Obligation - what is the service promise ?

Accountholders with the following service models must be provided at least once a year with a portfolio analysis report (PAR) or an equivalent investment proposal. In addition, our advisory clients with below service models can also expect that their portfolio is regularly reviewed with them (at least on a yearly basis) and any deviations as well as new investment opportunities are discussed.

Service Models in scope:

  • Advice Premium
  • Advice Exclusive (Advanced) – direct / indirect
  • Investment Advisory – direct / indirect

Which points should be included in the review (focus: Advisory clients and client suitability)?

Below points are a best practice overview of points to include in a yearly review. Some points might not be applicable depending on the clients' situation. The yearly frequency is regarded as a minimum, clients can request reviews more often, additionally location specific guidelines and instructions precede. 

Client Investment Profile and Investment Strategy: 

    • Recap of the portfolio details (service model, investment strategy and respective asset allocation, ESG preferences and any individual portfolio configuration)
      • Is the CIP still in line with the clients' situation?
        → Was there any substantial change in the clients personal (financial) circumstances which impacts the clients risk ability or risk tolerance and therefore need an update of the CIP (e.g. Inheritance, Marriage/Divorce, real estate buy/sell, etc.)? CIP - what is it for? - Suitability Wiki - Confluence / How to define a suitable investment strategy - Suitability Wiki - Confluence / CIP & K&E FAQ - Suitability Wiki - Confluence 
        → Is the K&E (Knowledge & Experience) still up to date or has the client gained new new knowledge or experience which is not yet reflected? Please note: To update the K&E documentation steps must be considered (Details here: https://wiki.juliusbaer.com/pages/viewpage.action?pageId=305482340)
        → Are the ESG preferences of the client still accurate?
        → Are there any individual portfolio configuration including Lombard which needs to be considered (Portfolio Configuration - Suitability Wiki - Confluence)
        → Is the portfolio in line with the strategy?
        → Is a CIP renewal pending? CIP Renewal process - Suitability Wiki - Confluence
      • Is the chosen service model still matching to the clients need?
    • Tool support: 
      • CIP/K&E/ESG preferences → CLM
      • Portfolio overview → DIAS (PAR → Portfolio Analysis Report - Suitability Wiki - Confluence)

Advisory portfolio value and performance: 

    • Recap the advisory portfolio market value and discuss the performance incl. main influencing market events
    • Point out significant profit or losses on single instrument level
    • Tool support: 
      • Portfolio overview → DIAS (PAR → Portfolio Analysis Report - Suitability Wiki - Confluence) / or client specific reports
      • PSP → Pages - Home
      • Support material (CIO View, Insight, Market updates) → IWMS-Portal

Advisory portfolio composition incl. monitoring results: 

    • Discuss portfolio overview (asset allocation, currency exposure, consolidated product risk, ESG
    • Highlight concentration risks (single positions, issuer, sector/country allocation etc.) and suggest rebalancing if required
    • Highlight individual positions and alerts, e.g. non-recommended instruments, change of house view on products, and recommend alternatives → Discuss any investment proposal with the client
    • Tool support: 
      • Create proposal → DiAS and generate PIR (Create Proposal - Suitability Wiki - Confluence), please note that suitability calculation in the proposal tool needs done (step 8 in linked guidance). 
      • PSP → Pages - Home
      • Support material (CIO View, Insight, Market updates) → IWMS-Port

Which additional points should be considered for Advisory clients?

Leverage:

    • If applicable: Discuss leverage utilization, LTVs and available margins, currency mismatch of loans to assets
    • Tool support: 
      • Temos

Client opportunities: 

    • Check if top-down Sales initiative of Bank and Region can be positioned
    • Check if bottom-up Sales opportunities and opportunities can be positioned
    • Update of 360 financial plan or discussion of total A&L
    • Check pricing 
    • Tool support: 
      • CAP
      • CLiPP (SOKO CH/GUE)

Client relationship: 

    • Confirmation of personal data (incl. KYC aspects if triggered by periodic KYC review)
    • Document deficiencies
    • Confirmation of communication instructions/distribution preferences
    • Any pending tasks
    • Tool support: 
      • CRM/CLM
      • Action Manager

This best practice guidance focuses on the considerations for Advisory clients but of course also other mandates can be discussed in a similar structure where applicable.
