1. General information /internal regulation

DvP/RvP business is a very sensitive business and is therefore only permitted with exemption approval to eligible clients, which have been approved in accordance with the below described process description/ published policies.

As DvP/RvP will remain a very limited business, the overall objective should be to internalize the transactions, means if JB has the same capabilities to execute the transactions (even with the same counterparties), then the trade should be executed within the Markets trading floor at JB to ensure a transparent and controlled order execution chain.

RMs must ensure that approvals for DvP/RvP are obtained prior to the EAM/PB client executing transactions with the counterparty (this applies for permanent and for one-off transactions).

2. Permanent versus one-off transactions 

Permanent setup = series of DvP/RvP transactions for a definite or indefinite period and a limited or unlimited number of transactions.

One-off transaction = one single transaction. The RM has to ensure that one-off transactions remain exceptions for the respective EAM/PB client.

Please regard that for permanent and also for one-off transactions the same approval process has to be initiated and also for one-off transactions a client agreement has to be signed.

3. Pre-conditions and process description incl. required forms and agreements 

 a.) For all one-off and permanent transactions:

  • It must be checked if the trade can be internalized (please check with the trading desk in CH)
  • The transaction must not already be placed with the external counterparty by the client. Means the DvP/RvP approval must be given before the client initiates the trade with the external counterparty (e.g. broker)
  • The DvP/RvP transaction can only be settled if all approvers have given their approval and if the client has signed the equivalent client agreement (also for one-off transactions). Please regard that the approval process is very time intensive and that an equivalent lead time must be considered.
  • If additional brokers should be used, the approval process has to be re-initiated for these additional brokers again (same approval form to be used)

b.)  Restricted countries: 

Due to country specific procedures of retroactive withholding taxes, restrictions, sanctions or other market specific risks, we do not offer 
Delivery versus Payment transactions for the following countries:

  • Indonesia
  • Iceland
  • India
  • Russia
  • Argentina
  • Oman

See also restricted countries in the following CH form: Order Form CH-01595-EN 20240405.pdf (juliusbaer.com)

4. Automated settlement

Automated settlement (e.g. via SWIFT, data upload etc.) is not allowed and requires an exception to policy approval (EtP) beforehand.

After approval of automated settlement, Operations has to be involved, an appropriate lead time has to be considered for the implementation and testing of the automated setup.

Please revert in such cases to IWMS Eurohub Markets.

Existing EtP approvals must be reviewed on a regular basis.  

For automated settlement there is no approval of the used brokers required, see policy JBG-2080-00 Transaction banking, point 8 "automated settlement".
