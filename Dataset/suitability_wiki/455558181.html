CC logic1) Who should be re-classified?

As only Account Holders have an influence on the professional flag in Wealth Navigator, only Account Holders are re-classified. 

Any other roles (PoA, BO, etc.) have no influence and are generally not re-classified. Further, the client classification (including documents and tasks - see Q4) are always on partner level. 

Important: If the Account Holder is a minor, it is not possible to classify this account as professional.

CC logic, domicile, form2) What classification document is the correct one for my client?

First of all, the domicile determines the applicable regulation (see Client Classification Country List). In short, domiciles in the EEA (European Economic Area) including UK only require a MiFID classification. Example: If a Switzerland-domiciled client relocates to Italy, the client would require a MiFID classification - not FIDLEG anymore.

CC logic, joint accounts, collective accounts, FIDLEG, MiFID3) How can I re-classify joint- or collective accounts and what influence have different regulations?
  • The logic of highest protection is applicable here, meaning that if one Account Holder is a Private or Retail Client, the overall ZR has no professional flag (yet). To change this, all Account Holders of this ZR must be re-classified.
  • When re-classifying the Account Holders, each of them must individually fulfill the K&E and asset requirements of the given regulation. As an example, 1/3 of the assets of the ZR can be considered for each request if there are 3 Account Holders.

In case of a joint or collective account the following Wealth Navigator rules apply.

  1.  MiFID wins over FIDLEG: In case one account holder is domiciled in an EEA country, MiFID workflow is applied. 
  2.  Account is classified according to the weakest Account Holder ("Private/Retail wins over Professional"): In case one Account Holder is classified as Retail Client, Wealth Navigator applies the workflow for Private/Retail Clients.

CC workflow, document, CC task, confirmation task4) How does the CC technical workflow look like?
  1. By activating the document (via CDS/Account Modification, visible via Documents on partner level), a task is automatically generated and visible in CLM Task List or on Partner profile under Client Classification thereafter.
  2. After justifying the re-classification of the Account Holder, the task can be sent to the approval step.
  3. If approved, the front staff receive the confirmation task.
  4. Re-classification to be confirmed to the client. Do not send the confirmation letter to the client prior to the CLM approval.
  5. Once the re-classification is confirmed to the client (previous step) and the confirmation task is closed, the new classification is Active in CLM and visible in Wealth Navigator.

CC criterium, assets8) What is there to bear in mind regarding assets?
  • Ensure that the information on CLM Wealth Profile is up to date. This will ensure efficient processing. Future inflows cannot be considered.
  • Only bankable assets where the client is Account Holder can be considered (JB, third-party assets). Any company-related assets (or the like, where client is BO, PoA) cannot be considered. 
  • Only bankable assets can be considered, whereas pension and real estate assets, amongst others, cannot be considered.
  • The amount of assets of joint and collective accounts can only be partly considered (split by the number of Account Holders, see Q3).
  • Digital assets can be considered for the asset requirement.

9) How can I delete pending or active classifications?

One must differentiate between pending (i.e. Pending RM, Pending approval) and active (i.e. Active) classifications.

a) For pending requests, the RM (or the Reviewer) can select Reject (discard request) within the task. This will remove the item and expire the document.

b) For active classifications, a re-classification to FIDLEG Private Client (Form ID CH-01533) or MIFID Retail Client (Form ID CH-01690) is required via a separate document. Thereafter, the normal process in CLM is applicable.

Only for bank-initiated downgrading as a consequence of the regular client classification review: Please review FAQ14.

CC logic, asset class10) Can one re-classify a client just for one asset class (e.g. complex bonds)?No, asset class-specific re-classifications are technically not possible for RML CH. If an Account Holder is re-classified, the re-classification is valid for all asset classes. Consequently, K&E aspect must be adhered to holistically (vast majority of asset classes, i.e. 11 or more) and not just specific to one asset class (for MiFID and FIDLEG Elected Professionals).
client confirmation11) Where to find the re-classification letter?

In CLM, after the client classification approval has been given, the RM will be notified in the system about the duty to confirm the new status to the client. This can now be done directly from CLM once the client classification is approved and in status Pending notification (CLM Partner Profile). One can click on the three dots below Action, select Distribute letter and follow the guidance thereafter. Only once the confirmation task has been closed, the status of the client classification will be Active (CLM Partner Profile) and the new status will be reflected in Wealth Navigator.

inactivation, regular CC review14) What does the process of the bank-initiated downgrading to FIDLEG Private / MIFID Retail look like?

This part only covers the bank-initiated downgrading following the regular review of active client classifications. These cases were raised separately via CLM Task Manager. If you come to the conclusion that the client should be downgraded as the requirements are not fulfilled anymore, please follow the steps below.

  1. Contact GPS Risk Management via Investment.Suitability Mailbox to inactivate the active client classification and request the confirmation letter in the correspondence language.
  2. Once the client's classification is inactivated, you will receive a notification task related to the duty to inform the client about the change. Do not provide the system-generated letter. Instead, use the letter provided by GPS Risk Management and adjust it accordingly (i.e. add address, salutation, and select the correct option form the drop-down menus) and sign it before its distribution. Don't forget to archive it in CLM/OnDemand.
      For any locations other than Zurich, you will have to use your location-specific letter template and copy & paste the text from the letter provided by GPS Risk Management.
  3. Close the notification task.
tax impact15) Does tax authority treat the client as 'professional securities dealer' when he/she is professionalised under FIDLEG/FinSA?

Whether the requirements for commercial securities trading are met depends on the totality of the circumstances in each individual case. The tax authorities, in paragraph 3 of Circular No. 36, have established cumulative criteria which, if fulfilled, exclude the existence of commercial securities trading. If these criteria are not cumulatively satisfied, commercial securities trading cannot be ruled out.

As evident from the individual criteria, customer classification under FIDLEG has no direct bearing per se on this individual assessment.

That said, it can certainly be acknowledged that a client’s knowledge and experience (K&E) in relation to securities may influence the tax authorities’ case-by-case analysis. However, the Bank assesses K&E independently of the customer classification—meaning even private clients may possess the highest levels of knowledge and experience. Furthermore, the thresholds applied for FIDLEG purposes are relatively low (see K&E form in the attachment). From a quantitative standpoint, approximately five transactions over three years per financial instrument would likely still fall well short of constituting commercial activity.

Accordingly, the conclusion may more appropriately be reversed: A person engaging in commercial securities trading could very likely qualify as a professional client under FIDLEG; however, not every professional client under FIDLEG meets the criteria for commercial securities trading.
