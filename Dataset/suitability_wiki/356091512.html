Three ESG alerts have been introduced in 2022 to ensure suitable advice in accordance to client ESG Preferences. These alerts apply to RM Locations Switzerland, Luxembourg, Spain and Ireland. For Hong Kong / Singapore including CUR Locations with Singapore, Hong Kong, Bahrain, Dubai & Thailand only the ESG Risk alert is activated.

The alerts are:

1. ESG Risk

The alert is triggered when an order is entered in an ESG Risk investment. For advised orders, the alert is business critical (red) and for non-advised orders a business opportunity (blue).

Rationale: ESG Risks are excluded from the offering and may not be advised – even upon request from the client.

The alert is triggered in the advisory session.

In any other cases, when the ESG Risk Alert appears, it is not allowed to process in the DiAS advisory session (for solicited/reverse solicited).

2. ESG Category Mismatch

The alert is only triggered for active & reverse solicited orders for instruments with lower ESG category than the client’s ESG preference.

Actively Solicited

Under the condition that there is no alternative/equivalent product available within the client’s ESG preferences, RMs can provide active advice on a single financial instrument against the client’s ESG preferences provided that the client’s approval to receive investment advice outside of his ESG preferences is received prior to providing such advice. This means RMs can exceptionally justify the ESG alert in an actively solicited scenario. In this case the following must be documented:

  • No alternative/equivalent product is available within the client’s ESG preferences
  • The client’s approval for receiving such Investment Advice has been obtained prior to providing the advice (please note that the client’s explicit approval is required for each trade)
  • Client’s clear acknowledgement that this Investment Advice will not be in line with the defined ESG preferences but he/she agrees to receive such Investment Advice (i.e.  client agrees to a one-time adaption of the ESG preferences for this trade only, no change of CIP)
  • Accurate documentation why this advice has been provided and when (date) and how (channel) this approval by the client has been received

Contact note example: I have contacted the client due to a business critical alert. As there is no equivalent product for the switch on the shelf for the clients ESG preferences, I asked the client for his approval to receive investment advice outside of his ESG preferences. The client agreed and acknowledged that this investment advice will not be in line with the defined ESG preferences but he/she agrees to receive such Investment Advice. I consequently suggested a switch from A to B.  

Reverse Solicited

In case of a reverse solicitation from the client (according to Suitability Wiki, see screenshot below), you may advise the client outside his/her ESG preference universe. However, it’s your duty to inform the client about the ESG mismatch and get the client’s approval and acknowledgment before proceeding with the execution.

Contact note example: The client called me and asked for advice on instrument A (specific instrument). According to our systems, instrument A is a Traditional investment, however, the client has selected a Responsible ESG preference in his/her CIP. I informed the client about the ESG preference mismatch. The client acknowledged the mismatch but nevertheless wanted to proceed with the transaction.

3. ESG Data Missing

The instrument is not assigned an ESG category (and the asset class is covered by our Methodology).

Rationale: The instrument may have a lower ESG category than client’s ESG preference.

The client must be warned before order placement.

The alert is triggered only in the advisory session. If an asset class is not covered by the Methodology, no alert will be triggered.

4. Overnight Discretionary Mandate Check

The alert is triggered overnight when a discretionary mandate is selected by the client that has an ESG category below the ESG preference of the client (e.g. DM is a Traditional Investment but client has selected Responsible Investment).

Rationale: Discretionary mandates need to be in line with ESG preferences. There is a point of sale check in MSD. If a discretionary mandate is set-up via AOF rather than MSD, which is not mandatory, or a client changes their ESG preferences to a higher category, this alert notifies the RM of this mismatch.

The alert is triggered overnight and is business-critical.

The alert is as follows:


This alert can only be resolved by contacting the client to inform that this discretionary mandate does not match the chosen ESG preferences and determining the appropriate follow-up action:

a) You may discuss with the client to change the Discretionary Mandate (no resolution note required, if change is initiated immediately, but you should add a comment on the change of the mandate in a contact note, explaining the reason for the mandate change [i.e. alignment with the ESG preferences in the CIP]). If a proposal is sent to change the Discretionary Mandate, use resolution note “investment proposal sent” (alert will be snoozed for 30 days).

b) Change the ESG preferences of the client (no resolution note required, but RMs should add a comment on the change of ESG preferences in a contact note, explaining that the ESG preferences have been changed so that the mandate is now in line with the adapted ESG preferences)

c) Accept the mismatch via justification "Client Accepts Risk" (resolution note is required, ensure to mention in the resolution note that you clearly communicated the ESG mismatch present and what the client's decision was (including a rationale for decision). A one-way communication is not sufficient (e.g. having notified the client by e-mail but not having received a response). By using the justification "Client Accepts Risk" the RM (indirectly) confirms that he has notified the client and that the client has stated that he wants to keep the mandate

In case the client was not reached please mention in the resolution note for "Client not reached" when and how you tried to reach the client (e.g. notified the client by email, but did not receive their response yet) → once this has been selected, the alert will greyed out for month and then pop-up again.


5. Portfolio Check - Sustainable Investments according to SFDR / EU Taxonomy

As of mid-September 2023, clients booked in Germany or Luxembourg can selected additional ESG preferences (see here for details: Client ESG Preferences - Suitability Wiki - Confluence (juliusbaer.com))

If a client selects such additional ESG preferences the client's advisory portfolio alignment to Sustainable Investments according to SFDR / EU Taxonomy will be compared against the corresponding threshold defined by the client's ESG preference.

If the proposed trade(s) worsen the portfolios proportion of sustainable investments according to SFDR / EU Taxonomy below the specified min. proportion threshold a business critical alert gets generated.

Actively Solicited
