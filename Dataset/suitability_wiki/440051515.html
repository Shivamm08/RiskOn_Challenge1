PRIIP KID*

If a PRIIP KID exists, then all clients, regardless of their category and country of domicile, can purchase it, but the Front must provide the PRIIP KID to them ex ante (because the PRIIP KID contains costs and charges information that must be given to the client pre-purchase to complement the pre-purchase cost disclosure that will get generated and sent to them out of DiAS).

*If a PRIIP KID does not exist, only the following clients can purchase it:

  1. Non-EU domiciled retail clients
  2. Professional (elective or per se) clients, regardless of their country of domicile
  3. Eligible counterparty clients, regardless of their country of domicile
