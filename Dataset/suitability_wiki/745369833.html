2. Description of the alert

Every fund domiciled in Luxembourg is subject to tax rates of 5 bps. The fund may profit from lower tax rates (only 1 bps.) if all investors are institutional investors.  The definition of institutional investors is not strict but does not include
private individuals. Therefore, certain funds have limited the investors to institutional investors and have set up respective agreements with their distributors (in this case JB). A non-adherence may cause compensation complaints towards JB and may lead to fines by the Luxembourg tax authority. Hence, JB limits investing in such funds to the following clients:

  • Discretionary Mandate clients
  • MiFID eligible counterparties
  • MiFID & FIDLEG per se professional clients
  • FIDLEG institutional clients

Private individuals and elected professional clients are blocked for those investments, without any exception. See additional information here.

Further information and definitions of client classifications can be found under →Client Classification


3. What triggers this alert?

The alert is triggered in the Advisory Session for buy-like transactions of clients that are not classified as Discretionary Mandate client, MiFID eligible counterparty or MiFID / FIDLEG per se professional when investing in a Luxembourg domiciled fund limited to institutional investors.
