Investment Updates

https://wiki.juliusbaer.com/spaces/fkh/pages/382719878/Investment+Updates

In order to help the Relationship Manager to invest larger cash amounts into the portfolio they can add Investment Updates (containing Opportunity, Corporate Action, Investment Update, Life Cycle Event).
A full diagram what content is available and shareable can be found under Sharing investment content & ideas.


1) From the Portfolio page select "Proposals" this will bring you to the overview page where you can find all your proposals made for this specific client or start a new one.


2) Select "Create new" unless you have already an saved proposal from the list.


3) On the right hand side, select "Investment Updates" to see the available Updates.


4) with the little arrow on the left you can see the full detail of this specific "Market Opportunity". If this opportunity fits to your client, you can select the littleicon on the right (sometimes you need to scroll to the right) to implement it into your proposal, this will open then the "Add Instrument Position" page.


5) Enter the amount you would like to invest (Unites or amount), chose "Solicitation type" and give a "Reason for advice" (scroll down) before you add the instrument to your proposal.

6) The positions will be added to the proposal.
