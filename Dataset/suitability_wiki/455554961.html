How to classify your client under FIDLEG

https://wiki.juliusbaer.com/spaces/fkh/pages/455554961/How+to+classify+your+client+under+FIDLEG

RML Switzerland (and RML Guernsey)

1. WHAT ARE THE CLIENT CLASSIFICATIONS UNDER FIDLEG?

Under FIDLEG, banks are obliged to classify their clients according to precisely defined legal criteria into the following three categories:

  • Private Clients
  • Professional Clients
  • Institutional Clients

All new clients will be informed about their classification. Existing clients will be informed only if their existing classification changes. 

PRIVATE CLIENTS

Private Clients are afforded the most regulatory protection regarding their investment universe and information provided to them. The Bank decided to consider all PB clients as FIDLEG Private Clients unless they choose to opt-out to become an Elected Professional or the RM would like to classify the client as Per Se Professional or Institutional Clients.

PROFESSIONAL CLIENTS

The level of protection for Professional Clients is lower than for Private Clients. They are considered to have sufficient knowledge and experience to make their own investment decisions and to appropriately assess the associated risks. FIDLEG distinguishes between two types of Professional clients: (i) Elected Professionals and (ii) Per Se Professionals.

Key differences to the afforded regulatory protection for Professional vs. Private Clients:

  • Less information to receive
  • Broader investment universe
i) ELECTED PROFESSIONALii) PER SE PROFESSIONAL

A request for re-classification as Elected Professional can be initiated by the client or by the Bank. In any case, a signed application form (from the client) is needed.

To classify a client as Elected Professional, the client must meet one of the following criteria:

  1. Own bankable assets of at least CHF 500’000, plus have sufficient knowledge based on training, education and professional experience
  2. Own bankable assets of at least CHF 2’000’000

Note: For joint accounts/collective accounts, each Account Holder needs to have bankable assets of CHF 500’000 or CHF 2’000’000 respectively.

Contrary to the Elected Professional, Per Se Professionals can be classified by the Relationship Manager. The client has the option to apply for a re-classification at any time.

The following are defined as Per Se Professional clients:

  • Financial intermediaries as defined in the Banking Act (BA) or the Financial Institutions Act (FinIA) (including portfolio managers and trustees)1
  • Insurance companies as defined in the Insurance Supervision Act (ISA)1
  • Foreign financial intermediaries or insurance institutions subject to prudential supervision
  • Central banks
  • Public entities with professional treasury operations (such as municipality)2
  • Occupational pension schemes with professional treasury operation2
  • Companies with professional treasury operations2
  • Large companies, which fulfil at least two of the following criteria:
    - A balance sheet total of minimum CHF 20’000’000
    - A turnover of minimum CHF 40’000’000
    - Equity of minimum CHF 2’000’000
  • Private investment structures with professional treasury operations, created for high-net-worth Private Clients.2

1 Only financial intermediaries and insurance companies domiciled in Switzerland
2 A professional treasury is deemed to exist if the company entrusts at least one professionally qualified person experienced in the financial sector with the permanent management of the company's financial resources. (such as industry and commercial enterprises)

INSTITUTIONAL CLIENTS

Contrary to the Elected Professional, Institutional Clients can be classified by the Relationship Manager. The client has the option to apply for a re-classification at any time.

The following are defined as Institutional Clients:

  • Financial intermediaries as defined in the Banking Act (BA) or the Financial Institutions Act (FinIA) (including portfolio managers and trustees)1
  • Insurance companies as defined in the Insurance Supervision Act (ISA)1
  • Foreign financial intermediaries or insurance institutions subject to prudential supervision
  • Central banks
  • National or supranational public entities with professional treasury operation2

1 Only financial intermediaries and insurance companies domiciled in Switzerland
2 A professional treasury is deemed to exist if the company entrusts at least one professionally qualified person experienced in the financial sector with the permanent management of the company's financial resources. (such as industry and commercial enterprises)

2. WHAT ARE THE CRITERIA FOR RE-CLASSIFICATIONS?

Please bear in mind that the approver can only review the requests on the basis of current documentation. Therefore, please ensure up-to-date information in CRM, CLM, KYC (amongst others). This will contribute to an efficient processing.

a) FIDLEG Elected Professional

One of the following two criteria must be fulfilled:

CriteriaRequired information

1. Necessary knowledge (based on training, education, professional experience) and bankable assets of CHF 500'000

  • The K&E profile must reflect the good knowledge in the vast majority of asset classes (i.e. 11 or more) in CLM. If the client does not have a sufficient K&E profile, please follow this guidance.
  • If the asset requirement is not fulfilled with in-house bankable assets, ensure that the asset breakdown is reflected in CLM Wealth Profile.

2. Bankable assets of CHF 2'000'000

  • If the asset requirement is not fulfilled with in-house bankable assets, ensure that the asset breakdown is reflected in CLM Wealth Profile.

Please bear in mind that i) only assets where the client is Account Holder can be considered, ii) for joint accounts, each Account Holder must individually fulfill the requirement, iii) amongst others, pension and real estate assets cannot be considered.

3. HOW CAN A CLIENT BE RE-CLASSIFIED?

If the requirements are met, a re-classification can be initiated at any time by using the official forms from AOF or CLM. Use the respective form from the list below. The CLM workflow (including the 4-eye verification process) is explained here in more detail.

FIDLEG Elected Professional

FIDLEG Per Se Professional

FIDLEG Institutional Client

Form ID CH-01400

Form ID CH-01472

Form ID CH-01694

Elected Professionals, Per Se Professionals, Institutional Clients have the right to be re-classified to higher levels of protection available. If any of these clients wish to be re-classified as FIDLEG Private Client or if the client does not meet the requirements anymore ("Change in Circumstance" such as divorce), the client may request a reclassification by signing the form (Form ID CH-01533). Further information can be found here: CH-5031-00 Client Classification.

For bank-initiated downgradings as a consequence of the regular client classification review, please consult FAQ14.
