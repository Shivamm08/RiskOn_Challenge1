JB ESG Scores

The JB ESG Scores are proprietary scores which enable to evaluate and summarize the performance along a certain ESG topic in an easy and understandable way.

The ESG Scores are constructed using raw data 3rd parties (from MSCI and Sustainalytics) and summarize dozens of data points into one score, which is more understandable for our clients.

The scale used for the JB scores ranges from -3 (worst performance) to +3 (best performance) except for the Global Norms Score, which ranges from -3 to 0 and the Natural Capital Score that ranges from -3 to 2 . This scale allows for sufficient granularity, while remaining simple and intuitive. The methodology utilizes these scores to generate thresholds which define the classification of an instrument. The scoring process starts with a status quo score: by combining data from external data providers and internal research, an absolute score is obtained based on the company’s performance. To reward companies that show significant improvement in an ESG area we determine a transition score by leveraging our Next Generation research or transition related data. This is a key element of our methodology, as it adds an important forward-looking element based on our internal expertise.
