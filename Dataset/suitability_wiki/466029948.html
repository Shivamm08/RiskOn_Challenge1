Question 1 ("Q"): My client is domiciled in a country other than Spain. Therefore I cannot distribute PEGO I FCR to him, correct?

Answer 1 ("A"): Yes, correct. Distribution of PEGO I FCR is only allowed to Spain-domiciled clients, regardless of the solicitation scenario. 


Q2: My client is a legal entity domiciled in Spain. However, its UBO and authorized representatives are domiciled in another country. Therefore I cannot advise PEGO I FCR to it, correct?

A2: It depends: if your client is a legal entity with legal personality, you will be able to advise to subscribe PEGO I FCR to its representatives; however, if it is a legal entity without legal personality, first of all the account participant who under MiFID2 is to be considered as the client will need to be determined: please contact IWMS Regulatory Change for this.


Q3: Are there really AIFs that do not have an ISIN?

A3: Yes. Any collective investment scheme that does not qualify as a UCITS qualifies as an AIF. Examples of AIFs without an ISIN are most of the AIFs launched by the Private Markets team in BJB CH, which are structured as limited partnerships domiciled in the British Virgin Islands or Cayman Islands. They do not have an ISIN.


Q4: I am in the Intermediaries desk. Which share class can I make available to the EFA: the retro-loaded ones or the retro-free ones?

A4: That depends on the type of investment advice that the EFA provides to the end client. If independent, then the retro-free ones (because the EFA will not be able to retain inducements); if non-independent, then the retro-loaded ones and the retro-free ones (because the EFA will be able to retain inducements).


Q5: Can I share the PEGO I FCR marketing material with all clients?

A6: No, only with clients domiciled in Spain. This is because (1) sharing an AIF’s marketing material = marketing that AIF, (2) PEGO I FCR has for the time being (on 15.04.2024) not been registered for marketing in any jurisdiction other than Spain, (3) BJBE ES branch has a distribution agreement with Alaluz Capital (the AIFM of PEGO I FCR), and (4) considering all this, only sharing the marketing material with clients domiciled in Spain is possible.


Q6: Can I share the PEGO I FCR marketing material with intermediaries (EAMs and EFAs)?

A7: Yes. However, the intermediaries will have to comply themselves with their own distribution obligations (i.e. they will need to adhere to marketing rules (e.g. not market the PEGO I FCR to clients domiciled in jurisdictions where it has not been registered for marketing), inducement-related rules (i.e. they won't be able to retain inducements of retro-loaded share classes if they provide DPM or independent investment advice), etc.). 


Q8: In the case of legal entity clients, are client-based distribution rules applicable to the legal entity clients or to the UBOs?

A8: To the legal entity clients as long as the legal entities have legal personality. If they don't, then please contact IWMS Regulatory Change to determine on which account participant their application shall be based.
