2. Description of the alert

For certain service models the service model contract states that the client will receive a Portfolio Analysis Report (PAR) at least once a year. This duty can also be fulfilled by providing an Investment Proposal Report (PIR or proposal summary). Further details about the content and structure of PAR can be found under →Portfolio Analysis Report

The PAR-alert reminds RM/IA that the report must be provided. The PAR is not automatically distributed, but must be generated and distributed by the RM/IA  in the Wealth Navigator (->“Portfolio view” ->“more” ->“Portfolio Analysis Report”).

To fulfil the contractual obligation, the PAR must always be sent to the account holder (for natural persons accounts) or authorized signatory (for legal entity accounts), even if the account holds zero balance or remains unfunded. The PAR requirement applies regardless of funding status, as long as the account is open and reportable. Once the documents are sent via e-channel (e-banking or e-mail), the alert will reset. It will not reset when documents are sent to a third party (e.g., PoA) or if an ad-hoc report is downloaded without sending it to the e-channel of the AH/AS. After every reset, the PAR alert will be triggered again 12 months later.

In cases with multiple account holders, sending the document to at least one is sufficient to reset the alert. For collective accounts or signatory rights an additional report should be sent to the collective signatory holder. In case of a client visit, it is possible to send the reports to the client via e-channel and also download and example (e-mail to RM) to discuss the physical copy in a meeting.

3. What triggers this alert?

The PAR-alert is triggered in the Client Book as a result of overnight portfolio risk monitoring as a reminder (non-critical alert) 300 days after the last PAR was sent to the client. The alert will become critical 30 days later.

5. Special Case: Deceased clients

Until the rightful heir(s) or legally appointed executor(s) have been formally identified and verified, the PAR must not be distributed via Wealth Navigator. This is because the deceased Account Holder’s (including Power of Attorney arrangements) is suspended in CLM and e-banking systems upon notification of death.

Recommended Procedure:

  • Do not distribute the PAR alert until the heirs or appointed executors have officially be confirmed
  • During this interim period, it is acceptable for the PAR alert to remain overdue in the system.
  • Once the legal representative is onboarded and fully verified, distribute the outstanding PAR to the authorized person.
  • After distribution, submit a standard Advisory Scorecard challenge for all previously overdue alerts linked to the deceased client, providing a clear explanation referencing the client’s passing and subsequent verification of representation.

⚠️ Important: Always coordinate with Legal Inheritance. Remove or deactivate the email address of the deceased client in CLM to prevent unintended communications.
