On top of the above logic, the following exception applies (for RML CH/GG only):

  • If a partner is classified as professional client (MIFID/FIDLEG), the existing knowledge level is also migrated to all newly split categories and “good” to the new category Real Estate.


For RML CH only – Digital Assets:

For single and collective account holders having signed the new Special Terms & Conditions for Digital Assets (STC-DIGITAL2), the knowledge is also set to “good” for Digital Assets, as the education is part of this process.

For all other account holders including those who signed the old Special Terms & Conditions for Digital Assets (STC-DIGITAL), the migrated value is “none”.

Exception: If you have educated account holders or order givers on Digital Assets, have provided them the Digital Assets education material, and documented the education in a contact note in CRM, you can update the knowledge for Digital Assets to "good" for these account holders or order givers in CLM by referring to the original contact note.

Additional information on K&E Digital Assets process: As a general requirement the Special Terms & Conditions for Digital Assets (STC-DIGITAL2) must be signed by the account holders. The account holders must be educated, and the respective education material must be handed out. The education of the account holders must be documented in a contact note. Afterwards the knowledge of the respective account holders can be updated directly in CLM. This also applies for Trade Basic clients. Additional order givers have to be educated according to the standard K&E process within the DiAS session. Please refer to the dedicated offering page for further details – here.
