4. Education of a client in and outside a DiAS session

https://wiki.juliusbaer.com/spaces/fkh/pages/424918494/4.+Education+of+a+client+in+and+outside+a+DiAS+session

When an order giver has no or only limited Knowledge in an asset class, the order giver needs to be warned that the knowledge is insufficient (unsolicited orders) or educated on the impacted asset classes (solicited orders).

Education involves explaining the risks and characteristics of the affected category to the order giver and additionally sending the respective information material (client education material – link). Sending of the client education material only for self-study is not sufficient. There are two possible processes:

EDUCATION DURING A DIAS SESSION:

DiAS highlights any missing Knowledge in the Session. If you are educating a client during a DiAS Session, the education also consists of explaining the risks and characteristics of the affected category to the client and additionally sending the respective information material. DiAS also provides an opportunity to confirm the education.

In the first step, the RM receives a warning that no recommendation must be made before ensuring that the client is educated. Please be informed that after this education the Knowledge will be updated permanently to “good”:


As second step , the education material must be sent together with the further mandatory documents to the client. 

Afterwards, the successful education can be confirmed in DiAS:

DOCUMENTATION

Change compared to today: 

If the successful education is confirmed in DiAS, no new K&E form has to be obtained from the client but the education that took place during the session must be duly documented in the contact note at the end of the session mentioning also the affected category. Please note: You must confirm the education and the update to “good” to the client either via e-mail to the client or via taped conversation by phone. In case of email confirmation, please attach the email to the contact note.

EDUCATION OUTSIDE DIAS

If the RM is discussing new instrument categories with an order giver that has not yet good Knowledge, the education must be part of this conversation. After the successful education, a new K&E form needs to be obtained and signed. The conversation including the outcome and upgrade needs to be documented in a contact note (RM u-No. and date of this contact note must be noted on the new K&E form) – also see part 2 with contact note examples. Please use the contact type “CIP and KE assessment/renewal” to document the conversation and afterwards, send the K&E form to CDS (for avoidance of doubt, the contact note itself must not be handed in together with the form).

Best practice: If an order giver repeatedly places orders for instruments, belonging to a category in which he/she has insufficient knowledge stated in our files, it is beneficial to assess, if the order giver has obtained knowledge in the meantime and if an increase to “good” (following beforementioned process) should be considered.
