1. What is this template about?

It is a template with which a "risk indicator, liquidity notice and complexity notice" is given pre-contractually to the order giver and client as required by Orden ECC/2315/2015, de 4 de noviembre, relativa a las obligaciones de información y clasificación de productos financieros.

2. Who must customize the template?

The Front (i.e. RMs, RMAs and IAs) in RML ES.

3. When must the template be customized?

Wealth Navigator will automatically generate the required "risk indicator, liquidity notice and complexity notice" and then attach it to the Client Package, but only in relation to products part of the Monitored Universe.

However, when Wealth Navigator cannot generate it, it will trigger a "Mandatory Document Disclosure Availability" ("MDDA") alert: it is then that the Front in RML ES must customize the template.

4. Once customized, what must be done?

The Front in RML must upload it to the Client Package. 

5. How to customize the template

Introduction

  1. Select the right salutation for the order giver and enter his/her name.
  2. Enter the name of the financial product as well as its ISIN if available.

Financial product

  1. Click on the [...] and, from the dropdown menu, select the class corresponding to the financial product relating to the financial service that the client will receive.

Risk indicator

  1. Click on the [...] and, from the dropdown menu, select the risk indicator corresponding to the financial product (which must coincide with the class just selected).

Liquidity notice

  1. For all the traits that the financial product has, click on the [...] and, from the dropdown menu, select an X; otherwise, select a blank.

Complexity notice

  1. Click on the [...] and, from the dropdown menu, select the complexity level corresponding to the financial product.
