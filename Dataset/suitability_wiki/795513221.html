FinSA
(Standard Workflow)

RML all (excl. SG/HK)

  • Client Classification according to FinSA standard (RML CH only)
  • Pre-Trade PRIIPS-KID for solicited transactions
  • Pre-Service Cost Disclosure (Rate Cards /Special Conditions)
  • PIR/Suitability Report and Cost Disclosure on client's request
RML CH / FIDLEG
MiFID add-on

RML CH, when clients are domiciled in EEA or UK

Reason: Switzerland has ratified the Lugano Convention, according to which local judgments (based on local laws and regulations) can be directly enforced in Switzerland.


RML MC, GG for EEA domiciled clients

MiFID add-on Standard is stricter than the FinSA Standard and is applied on top of the FinSA Standard.

  • Client Classification according to MiFID Standard
  • Pre-Trade PRIIPS-KID distribution for unsolicited transactions
  • Pre-Trade Cost Disclosure
  • 10% loss notification for leveraged products (e.g. options)
  • Post-Trade PIR/Suitability Report
RML CH / MiFID add-on
Full MiFID

RML LUX, IT, ES, PT UK and

RML CH, when clients are domiciled in Germany*


* Reason: Julius Baer has obtained a cross-border license in the form of a standard exemption "Freistellungsverfügung" from the German regulator "BaFin". Therefore JB is under the supervision of BaFin and must comply with the Full MiFID Standard, similar to a domestic bank. 

Full MiFID Standard is stricter than the MiFID add-on Standard and is applied on top of the FinSA and the MiFID add-on Standard. 

  • Pre-trade PIR / Suitability Report
  • Yearly distribution of Annual Cost&Charges Summary
  • Portfolio Cost Simulation
  • Phone recording
  • Cost-Benefit Analysis for Switch Recommendation
  • MiFID Target Market Check
  • Pre-trade PIB ("Produktinformationsblatt")
RML CH / Full MIFID
