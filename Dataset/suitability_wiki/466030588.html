1. What is the “Pre-Trade Warning Generator” XLS?

Under CNMV Circulars 3/2013 and 1/2018, the Front in the BJBE Spain branch must, prior to the execution of the BUY trades for retail clients:

  • Generate and send to the order giver certain pre-trade warnings,
  • Collect them (in most cases duly signed, and in some cases with a handwritten risk acknowledgement), and
  • Archive evidence of their dispatch.

Old process. Until 09.02.2025, the pre-trade warnings were generated with the “annex 5a, 5b, 5c and 5d” templates which had to be manually customized.

New process. On 10.02.2025, IWMS Regulatory Change (now GPS Advisory Framework & Client Suitability) rolled out the “Pre-Trade Warning Generator” XLS (training video here), a tool with which such pre-trade warnings get generated automatically based on the answers manually given by the Front in the BJBE Spain branch. The purpose of this tool is to ease the pre-trade warning generation step.

2. Where can I find the "Pre-Trade Warning Generator" XLS?

You can find it here.

PLEASE DO NOT DOWNLOAD THE XLS FILE.

Instead, open it from the SharePoint that the link above (i.e. behind the "here") takes you to, as follows:

1. Click on the "Edit in Excel" icon at the top right-hand corner of the page: the "Pre-Trade Warning Generator" XLS will open in Microsoft Excel on your computer.

2. Once open, click on "Enable Content" that you will see at the top bar.

3. Now the XLS will be ready to use - simply click on "START" on the initial window.

4. If you have it use it for multiple BUY orders in a row, please close and open it every 3 trades in order to avoid it from crash it. 

5. To close it for this reason or simply because you are done using it, please click on the "X" at the top right-hand corner of the initial window (see step #3 above).

B. When does it have to be used?

When the following conditions are met:

  • The Front Officer is located in the BJBE Spain branch.
  • The client (i.e. the account holder) is retail.
  • The client is going to buy / subscribe a financial instrument.

C. How does it work?

Considering the exceptions in section 3A and the conditions in section 3B, the Front in the BJBE Spain branch must run the “Pre-Trade Warning Generator” XLS during the Wealth Navigator session for each transaction in scope.

It will ask the user a series of questions:

  • Some will be multiple choice: the right option has to be chosen.
  • Others will request manual input: the requested data must be inputted.

If:

  • No “Pre-Trade Warning” PDF must be generated and sent, it will notify the user: the Wealth Navigator session must simply be completed as usual.
  • A “Pre-Trade Warning” PDF must be sent, it will:
    • Automatically generate it, and
    • Notify the user, asking him/her to send it before proceeding with the completion of the Wealth Navigator session: such PDF must be sent (please refer to the following section).
