1. If my client has 50% of his portfolio, say, invested in Amazon shares while the other 50% remains in cash, and I get a strategic asset allocation exposure (SAAE) alert on DiAS, can I advise my client to invest their cash into a diversified set of financial instruments?


No. Advice cannot be provided with issuer concentration risk (ICR), consolidated product risk (CPR) or SAAE without there being a valid data exception.

2. ICR, CPR and SAAE are calculated automatically at portfolio level by DiAS. Can DiAS automatically re-calculate ICR, CPR and SAAE at account level.2


No. For the time being, this calculation must be done automatically.

3. ICR, CPR and SAAE are calculated automatically at portfolio level by DiAS. If, when manually re-calculated at account level, ICR, CPR and SAAE can be proved to no longer exist at account level, can that be used as a way to justify the ICR, CPR and SAAE alert triggered by DiAS?3


Yes. In such a case, ICR, CPR and SAAE can be justified with “data exception”. However, that manual re-calculation must be described and documented in the corresponding justification “comments/information” box.

4. My client and BJBE have agreed to all-in fees, so the cost disclosures DiAS generates for each trade contain costs and charges valued at zero. Do I still have to provide my client with these cost disclosures before executing the trade?


Yes. Even if the costs and charges reported within the cost disclosure are valued at zero, you must still provide your client with the cost disclosure before executing each and every trade, as required by article 24 (4) of Directive 2014/65/EU and article 50 of Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2017/565.

10. The CIP and K&E form renewal process are too manual and cumbersome – could they be automated? For example, can I let my client confirm or update their CIP and K&E form via email?


The CIP and the K&E form are two of the most important contractual documents between BJBE and your client. They contain the information with which appropriateness and appropriateness can be tested, as required by MiFID II, before providing your client, respectively, with the investment services of, on the one hand, reception and transmission/execution of orders (RTO) and, on the other, discretionary portfolio management (DPM) and investment advice.


Both the CIP and the K&E form must be kept up-to-date to ensure that appropriateness and suitability can be properly tested ex ante as well as monitored during the course of the provision of the aforementioned investment services to your client.


As key contractual documents between BJBE and your client, to renew the CIP and K&E forms, your client:


  • May confirm them via email.
  • However, even if it is too manual and cumbersome, may only update them by filling out a new CIP or K&E form.11

12. In insurance accounts, which participant is “the client” mentioned by the BMO alerts that DiAS sometimes triggers – the account holder (i.e. the insurance company) or the policy holder?


The account holder (i.e. the insurance company), because the account holder is, under MiFID II, the client (i.e. the individual or legal entity to whom BJBE provides investment and ancillary services).

13. Can I advise my client to subscribe UCITS like the Global Quant Equity Fund?


If the Global Quant Equity Fund (and actually any other UCITS) and the account holder (i.e. the client) are domiciled:


  • In the same EU country, yes – you can advise your client to subscribe it.


  • In different EU countries and:


> If the UCITS is registered for distribution in the country of domicile of the account holder, yes – you can advise your client to subscribe it.

> If the UCITS is not registered for distribution in the country of domicile of the account holder, no – only a non-advised order from the client to subscribe the UCITS is permitted.14

15. What MiFID II online trainings and tests do I have to complete before 31.12.2021?

  • (1) The external, annual, (2021) PwC Luxembourg 2021 MiFID II refresher online test

Mandatory and KRI scorecard relevant only for LU RMs, RMAs, IWMS EuroHub staff and Portfolio Management staff (not ES and IE!).

Deadline: 31.12.2021


  • (2) The external, annual, (2021) PwC Luxembourg 2021 MiFID II refresher online training

Mandatory and KRI scorecard relevant only for LU RMs, RMAs, IWMS EuroHub staff and Portfolio Management staff (not ES and IE!).

Deadline: 31.12.2021

As Human Resources will have informed you, you will receive your credentials to the PwC Luxembourg Academy platform via email.


  • (3) The internal, half-year MiFID II refresher training online test

Mandatory and KRI scorecard relevant for LU, ES and IE RMs, RMAs and Investment Advisory Solutions staff.

Deadline: 31.12.2021.16
