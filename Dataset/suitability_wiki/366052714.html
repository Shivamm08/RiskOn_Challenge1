All financial instruments booked with BJB are assigned to an investment universe type, based on assignment criteria (e.g. conduct of a distinct due diligence process; life-cycle management activities to be performed, etc.) defined by the global instrument class owner (global ICO) of the respective instrument class. The financial instrument solicitation rules (FISR) are depending on the assigned investment universe type of a financial instrument as indicated below and set the basis for clear recommendation of a specific financial instrument in an advisory context, as regulatory required e.g. under MiFID II. A potential shift of a specific financial instrument from one investment universe type to another lies within the responsibility of the ICO, e.g. if the financial instrument no longer meets the assignment criteria as outlined in the respective Financial Instrument Global User Manual.

Why is it important:

We want to select the best instruments for our clients, and we have to consistently apply group-wide standards on financial instrument level. This is why the Global Investment Universes has been established to consistently and well-founded advising our clients to buy or sell financial instruments.

What does it mean:

Before advising a client to buy | sell or hold a financial instrument, you need to check its universe type to ensure you are in line with the respective FISR.

Investment Universe Types

Universe Type

Definition

Description

OFFERED UNIVERSE


Financial instruments for which the Bank’s view is comfortable to advice clients to buy instruments of this category. 


→ The bank expresses a 'house view' (product quality is monitored)

→ Can be actively solicited - taking into consideration the portfolio context, applicable country manual restrictions and respective service model1.

1 Note: For Advice Light mandates active solicitation is not permitted, even for financial instruments assigned to the Offered Universe.

OK TO KEEP

Financial instruments for which the Bank only can advise to keep them in the portfolio, but not to buy or to increase exposure.


→ The bank expresses a 'house view' (product quality is monitored)

→ Can advise to 'hold' but not to 'buy' / 'increase'

→ Advise to 'reduce' or 'sell' is allowed

→ e.g. illiquid bonds or stocks

NEGATIVE VIEW

Financial instruments for which the Bank expects a weak performance or (partial) capital loss and hence has expressed a negative view. 


→ The bank expresses a 'house view' (product quality is monitored)

→ Can advise to 'reduce' or 'sell'

NO BANK VIEW

Financial instruments for which the Bank does not intend to provide a view (e.g. sanctioned financial instruments, ESG risk instruments, instruments for which the Bank has a conflict of interest).   

→ The bank does not express a 'house view' (product quality is not monitored)

→ Only factual information can be provided

→ Advise to 'reduce' or 'sell' is allowed

→ e.g. JB bonds and shares

OUT OF MONITORED UNIVERSE


Financial instruments, which are technically set up in the systems in order to be processed, traded, and booked, without checking of any legal, tax, compliance and/or regulatory aspects, or being monitored. As per default, all financial instruments integrated within Julius Baer systems are per se considered as Out-of-Monitored Universe. 

→ The bank does not express a 'house view' (product quality is not monitored)

→ Only factual information can be provided.

→ Advise to 'reduce' or 'sell' is allowed

What can you do, if investment universe status of a financial instrument is not correct or if you have a question regarding the underlying calculation methodology?

You can contact the INSTRUMENT CLASS OWNER 'ICO':

  • Funds: funds@juliusbaer.com
  • Equity: equity.specialists.switzerland@juliusbaer.com
  • Fixed Income: fixedincome.specialists.switzerland@juliusbaer.com
  • Markets Products: stp.advisory@juliusbaer.com

If the ICO confirms that the investment universe status is not correctly reflected in the system, you can justify the alert and upload the mail from the ICO as evidence.
