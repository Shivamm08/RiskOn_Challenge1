2. Description of the alert

ESG stands for environmental (e.g. energy and water consumption), social (e.g. employer appeal and supply chain management) and governance (e.g. remuneration policy and company management). It is a set of standards and criteria used to measure a company's impact and performance, which are increasingly being framed by various laws and regulations globally.

JB classifies financial instruments  into the following categories: 

  • ESG Risk
  • Traditional investment
  • Responsible investment
  • Sustainable investment

Further information can be found under →Client ESG Preferences

The ESG Data Missing alert verifies if an instrument is assigned to an ESG category as this is the precondition to perform the ESG suitability check. As this alert combines critical and non-critical elements, the different scenarios are explicitly outlined in the following.


3. What triggers this alert?

“ESG Data Missing” is a session alert that is triggered if a financial instrument is not assigned to an ESG category or respective data has not been delivered to the Wealth Navigator, although the asset class is covered by the JB Sustainable Investment Rating Methodology. The alert is triggered in the Advisory Session as a result of a newly proposed transaction.

Please note: If an asset class is not covered by the methodology, no alert will be triggered. Currently the following asset classes are covered: equities, corporate and sovereign bonds, funds, structured products and unsecured derivatives. For further information please see below links.

Critical alert: For clients with ESG preference sustainable und responsible investments, the RM/IA must inform the client prior to execution of the transaction that this financial instrument does not have a JB ESG category. An additional risk disclosure is in such cases printed in the PIR.  

Non-critical alert: For clients with ESG preference traditional investments the RM/IA may inform the client.

If the client acknowledges the information and wants to proceed:

RM/IA to select "client informed" in the alert justification (for critical alerts) and document the warning accordingly in the alert justification/ contact note.

For non-critical alerts RM/IA to note the conversation with the client in the CCN at the end of the session.

Non-critical alert: RM/IA may inform the client prior to execution of the transaction that this financial instrument does not have a JB ESG category

Any conversation with the client must be duly noted in the CCN at the end of the Session.
