Marketing Material

What makes Marketing Material become "active solicitation" (Investment Advice)?

If Marketing Material is forwarded to a client (even if tailored by the Bank), it is not considered investment advice under the Swiss Regulation (FinSA). 

However, if made individual by adding an explicit or even implicit comment (call for action), it becomes investment advice.

Example explicit recommendation: "Dear Mr. Meier, please find attached the newest Investment Views. I would recommend an investment as it would perfectly fit to your portfolio."

Example implicit recommendation: "Dear Mr. Meier, please find attached the newest Investment View as this could be interesting for you."

Example unsolicited: "Dear Mr. Meier, please find attached the newest Investment Views"

falseImportant

Under MiFID forwarding tailored Marketing Material is considered Investment Advice!!

Applicability of MiFID interpretation: RML LU, UK, ES, IE, IT, PT and CH, if client is domiciled in GER


Is the distribution of an Equity Update considered investment advice?

No, an equity portrait is a marketing material and distribution of marketing material is not considered advice.

→ If the client comes back and instructs the RM to invest → flag the transaction as unsolicited "UNSOL"

→ If a client comes back with questions regarding the mentioned Equities and the RM provides a recommendation to either invest or refrain from an investment → flag transaction as reverse solicited "RSOL"


Is the information on an expiring bond considered investment advice?

No, pure information on an expiring instrument is not considered advice (factual information). However solicitation type might change depending on on how client is approached or how the client responds

→ If the client comes back and instructs the RM to invest → flag the transaction as unsolicited "UNSOL"

→ If the client comes back and asks for a recommendation and RM provides a list of bonds → flag the transaction as active solicited "ASOL".

→ If the client comes back with another bond in mind and asks the RM for his/her opinion → flag the transaction as reverse solicited "RSOL". 


Is the distribution of a list with financial instruments considered investment advice?

It depends.

→ RM sends a list with a selection of financial instruments (shortlist) to one or multiple designated clients → flag the transaction as active solicited "ASOL"

→ Client asks the RM for potential investments in European health care companies and RM sends a list with potential investments → flag the transaction as reverse solicited "RSOL"

→ Client asks the RM for a general list of financial instruments (e.g. all US-Treasury-Bonds maturing in 3 years or all new issued bonds in Japan) → flag the transaction as unsolicited "UNSOL"

Important

Unasked distribution of a list with financial instruments is considered investment advice. Only if the client ask for a list and RM sends out an unfiltered list, then a succeeding transaction in one of the instruments can be considered unsolicited.

  •  Where can I see the investment universe status of a new bond issue?

The universe status information is available in OTMS. For technical reasons, new issues cannot be displayed on PSP during the subscription period, until a valid ISIN number is available on an instrument level. 


  • How to proceed, if a new bond (IPO) is in the offered universe according to PSP, but the universe status is not correctly reflected in DiAS?
    In case an instrument is not accessible in DiAS proceed as follows:
    - By selecting "new instrument" and entering the ISIN a new instrument can be opened in DiAS as well as in the trading tools. This should take ~ 1 minute. If this does not work
    - Create a "custom instrument". The DiAS session can be run by using the dummy valor. The order is forwarded to the trading desk, which executes it manually. 

    For further information go to: How to add a new/custom instrument?


  • How to proceed, if the investment universe is wrong?
    There are different possibilities: 
    - In case the instrument is wrongly flagged e.g. as "out of the monitored": Use "Data Exemption" and document how data was identified and proven to be incorrect within the alert justification/contact.
    - In case the financial instrument is flagged "without Universe Status": Use "Checked manually" in case you have validated the instrument as offered and document how you have validated it. 

    For further information go to: Solicitation Alerts

Handling of Solicitation Alerts

  • Am I allowed to actively advice on an instrument outside of the offered universe?

No, it is not allowed to actively advise / solicit an instrument, which is not in the offered investment universe.

  • What if the client ask for advice (reverse solicitation) and a 'Negative View' Alert pops up?

The RM advices that client against the transaction (provide Negative Advice) and if client wants to proceed anyway, select justification option 'client accepts risk'. With this you document that you have advised against the transaction. This is stored in DiAS, but also documented as such in the PIR/Suitability Report.  

  • What if a 'Negative View' Alert pops up in an unsolicited case? 
    The RM informs the client about the 'Negative View'. Pure neutral passing on of information is not considered advice and hence, solicitation scenario 'unsolicited' can remain.  

  • How to act, if the solicitation alert informs you that the instrument is 'out of monitored? or 'no bank view'?

You are not allowed to provide any advice. Inform the client that the instrument is not monitored resp. JB does not have any view on the instrument. If the client still wants to proceed, change solicitation type to unsolicited. 

  • Until now, active solicitation was strictly forbidden for JB Advice Basic. Is this still the case?
    Yes, this is still the case. 

  • So far contact initiation by the bank was automatically active solicitation. Is this still valid?
    No, the contact initiation does not automatically define the solicitation type. It is relevant who triggered the advice. The decisive factor is whether investment advice has been provided and if so, who generated the concrete investment idea (bank or client). In case the RM calls the client to congratulate to his/her birthday and the client askes for advice it is reverse solicited. 
     


  • In order to classify as reverse solicitation, does the client request need to match three of required parameters or is it sufficient, if the client mentions the asset class?
    The client has to mention three attributes, whereas one of the attributes must be the asset class.

Alert Handling - Client accepts risk

  • In the justification drop-down, what does the option ("no further action") mean?
    This justification rationale can be chosen in case the client acknowledges the respective risk but, decided not to take any action to resolve it and still wants to proceed or keep the situation.

    See further info: Suitability Wiki – Alert Handling

  • How do we document the dropdown values that do not require an additional mandatory comment field is required (such as "no further action", "personal interest holding", "portfolio in closure")? In the contact note at the end?
    It is not necessary to duplicate the information in a contact note. However, you might want to provide additional information or document other topics you discussed with the client.  

  • Are these justifications aligned with FRM/IWMS Risk Management so that we don't get deficiencies on this later on, especially regarding missing details in a contact note?
    Yes, they are. The dropdown values guarantee a standardized and complete documentation for "client accepts risk". If additional mandatory information is required, please add this in the comment field, as this is subject to control. Please be aware that unspecific comments such as "client wish" are not sufficient. Remember as a general guiding principle that your selection must be plausible and clear so that a third party understands. 

  • Which justification comments appear on the client documents (PIR)?
    The Justification rationale and the mandatory comment are internal documentation. On the PIR the following standardized warning message will be printed:
    "Advice was provided on your request: The transaction leads to a deviation from the investment guidelines which we do not recommend (please carefully read the next pages): By proceeding with the trade we understand you accept these deviations and the risks involved."

  • How we document advice against, if no critical alert is triggered (no "client accepts risk" appears as an justification option)?
    In the client feedback step you can document your advice against either with "proceed against advice", if the client still wants to continue, or with "advised not to proceed", if the client cancels the order. Furthermore, there are free text options in the PIR to manually comment transactions and insert warnings. In any case, document the rationale for your advice against in the contact note.
