What is UK SDR?

  • UK SDR stands for UK Sustainability Disclosure Requirements, a regulation that introduces a set of sustainability-related product labels for the UK
  • The current UK SDR is only applicable to UK-domiciled funds
  • The main elements of UK SDR are:
    • Sustainability labels: Funds that meet the requirements can opt into one of four new sustainability labels (Sustainability Focus, Sustainability Improvers, Sustainability Impact, Sustainability Mixed Goals)
    • Product- and entity-level disclosures: Detailed rules on specific product-level disclosures (consumer facing, pre-contractual and ongoing) and entity-level sustainability reports

    • Requirements for distributors: Rules to ensure that product-level information (including the sustainable investment) is made available to consumers.

What clients are in scope?

  • As Julius Baer does not have any UK-domiciled funds applying for a UK SDR label (or with ESG-related naming), no UK SDR product disclosures for any funds need to be produced (this may change if and when discretionary mandates become part of the UK SDR scope)
  • Julius Baer's clients are affected by the "Requirements for distributors" meaning that Julius Baer needs to be be able to provide its clients with UK SDR disclosures documents in its role as a distributor
  • RM Locations in scope: UK
  • Clients in-scope: Retail clients 

What documents need to be provided?

  • There are three reasons why a document needs to be provided to an in-scope client (as defined) above, based on the UK SDR distributor rules:
    • Fund client wants to trade is a UK-domiciled fund with a UK SDR label
      → UK SDR Consumer Facing Disclosure (CFD) document needs to be provided. This is a maximum 2 page report outlining how the fund is meeting its sustainability claims. The CFD document is created by the fund manufacturer, and should be automatically sourced into supporting documents in Wealth Navigator, as a mandatory disclosure to be sent out to the client. This applies to all purchases (actively solicited, reverse solicited and unsolicited) and a retail client cannot proceed without the CFD document being provided in this scenario.
    • Fund client wants to trade is a UK-domiciled fund with ESG-related naming
      → UK SDR Consumer Facing Disclosure (CFD) document needs to be provided. This is a maximum 2 page report outlining how the fund is meeting its sustainability claims. The CFD document is created by the fund manufacturer, and should be automatically sourced into supporting documents in Wealth Navigator, as a mandatory disclosure to be sent out to the client. This applies to all purchases (actively solicited, reverse solicited and unsolicited) and a retail client cannot proceed without the CFD document being provided in this scenario.
    • Fund client want to trade is a non-UK domiciled fund, with ESG-related naming or wording in marketing material or prospectus
      → UK SDR Notice for Overseas Fund needs to be provided. This is a one page document which serves as a standardised disclosure notice to highlight to the client that this fund is not subject to UK sustainable investment labelling and disclosure requirements, and as a result may be subject to less stringent disclosure obligations regarding its sustainability claims. This document is written by Julius Baer, is non-Fund specific and will be auto-loaded into supporting documents in Wealth Navigator, as a mandatory disclosure to be sent out to the client. This applies to all purchases (actively solicited, reverse solicited and unsolicited).
      (in practice it is not possible to identify all non-UK funds with any type of ESG-related wording, therefore this notice is provided for all non-UK domiciled funds)
  • The above documents are sourced from PSP into Wealth Navigator and attached to the client package automatically, whenever available

How to proceed if the UK SDR documents is not automatically available in Wealth Navigator?

  • If Wealth Navigator cannot automatically source the document from PSP, an MDDA alert will be generated
  • Where the Consumer Facing Disclosure (CFD) is required (i.e. the first 2 instances above), the document must be retrieved manually from the fund manager's website and attached to resolve the alert. If no such document can be found, the trade cannot proceed for retail clients. Please reach out to your local UK Business Risk Management if you are unsure.
  • Where the UK SDR Notice for Overseas Fund document is required, please manually attach this standardised disclosure document to resolve the alert. Such cases should be raised to bfs retrospectively. → Link to document
  • It is important to note that the UK SDR Notice for Overseas Fund document must not be used for a UK-domiciled fund.
