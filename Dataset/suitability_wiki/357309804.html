This article provides a step-by-step explanation on how to access the ESG reports in OnDemand.


1. Log in OnDemand:


2. Choose the folder «CA-Kundenabrechnung» or «CA-Clientdossier-PB» :

3. Type in «ESG» within the Description field or «Client Letter»:


4. Double klick on one of the results at the bottom left to get the letter and all reports corresponding the each ZrNr on the right side:


5. Scroll down to see details of the letter and reports attached:
