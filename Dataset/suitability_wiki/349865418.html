• In the first step, a risk-based screening is applied to identify those investment funds that are deemed ESG risk investments and are therefore to be avoided within Julius Baer’s investment universe.
  • In the second step, we draw on the self-attested classification of the investment fund management companies according to the EU Sustainable Finance Disclosure Regulation (SFDR). This fund documentation undergoes a due diligence process by our Fund Research Team.
      • SFDR Art. 6 products are categorised as traditional investments,
      • SFDR Art. 8 products are categorised as responsible investments,
      • SFDR Art. 9 products are categorised as sustainable investments (a lower category may apply if further internal requirements or risk-based screening criteria are not met)
  • The last step requires the Responsible Investment Committee (RIC) to validate any exceptions to the automated fund classification: the validation may include both upgrades and downgrades.
