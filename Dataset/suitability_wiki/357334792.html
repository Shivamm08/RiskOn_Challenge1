In order to ensure the correct functionality and development of the methodology, as well as to avoid arbitrary changes to the classification of instruments a clear governance structure has been set up. As such, two different committees have been set up which are tasked with the oversight of the methodology on the strategic and operational level, namely the strategic and operational responsible investment committees (RIC) respectively.

As can be seen from the image above, certain groups within Julius Baer have the possibility to challenge the classification of instruments according to the methodology if they have clear reasons.


Challenges of the ESG classification of instruments

The ESG classification of an instrument can be challenged in certain cases in which the information provided by third parties is deemed to be outdated or incorrect, thus leading to a misleading classification. Only specific members of Julius Baer can raise such challenges, which are then handled by the Operational Responsible Investment Committee.

The operational RIC follows a materiality approach for the review of such challenges. This means that the higher the banks holding are, the more priority is given to a challenge. In general, however, a minimum overall bank holding of CHF 2 million is required for a challenge to be taken up by the operational RIC.
