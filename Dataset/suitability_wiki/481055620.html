SUITABILITY IN MOC - INTEGRATED VALIDATIONS, WARNING MESSAGES & GUIDANCE

From a suitability perspective below mentioned checks & validations have been built into the MOC journey to support you in generating a suitable proposal. 

But, there are a few warning messages which shall raise your attention for client-specific clarification and manual validation, especially if you tailor your proposal to specific client investment needs.


  1. Integrated validations
    1. all BCs/RMLs (excl. BC HK/SG): 
      1. CIP-related information: The MOC journey displays the relevant information along the workflow and on ZR level for:
        1. Investment objectives
        2. Overall risk profile
        3. Overall investment strategy
        4. ESG preference category
      2. Offering suitability: Based on the client's CIP and ESG preferences, the MOC journey displays by default the suitable offerings from the applicable offering shelf (advisory & discretionary). Other investment strategies and ESG categories can be selected in the tool. However, this is ONLY allowed upon client's request. If deviating strategies or ESG preferences are selected upon client's explicit request, please ensure to warn the client about the according risks and document this accordingly as stated in the warning message. 
      3. Client output & disclaimer: The final proposal document incl. the relevant disclaimer information. Please consider the guidance provided in the e-mail you'll receive.

    2. BC HK/SG / all RML:
      1. CIP-related information & offering suitability: The MOC does not show any information on a consolidated level, because investment strategy, etc. are only applicable on portfolio-level. This has an impact on the workflow, as you are required to manually validate the offering, according to local regulation.
      2. Client output & disclaimer: The final proposal document incl. the relevant disclaimer information. Please consider the guidance provided in the e-mail you'll receive.

  2. Warning messages & guidance
    Where applicable, the workflow includes warning messages and information to guide you, aiming to ensure that you check suitability aspects of the individual client case. 

    ENSURE TO DOCUMENT THE CLIENT CONVERSATION ACCORDINGLY! See here: Pre-contract suitability checks in MOC - Suitability Wiki - Confluence
    1. Higher investment strategy: If you're selecting new offering with an investment strategy exceeding the CIP thresholds, you receive a warning message. 
    2. Overall Investment Strategy Check: After you have allocated asset to a mandate with an investment strategy exceeding CIP thresholds, and based on the OIS-calculations the breach materializes, you receive a warning message. 
    3. ESG preference: If you change the pre-defined setting and search for mandates which do not match the ESG preferences, you receive a warning message. 
    4. BC HK / SG specific warning, "Product selection" step: Because CIP-information relates to the portfolio-level, a manual validation concerning Investment Objective, Investment Strategy, Risk Profile and Investment Horizon applies. A specific information is displayed in the workflow.
    5. MOC e-mail: After you've finalized your proposal, you'll receive an e-mail with the attached PPT and PDF documents. Please note the guidance provided in this e-mail about the communication to clients as well as the limitations concerning the distribution of documents:
      1. Client-specific considerations: Make sure that for PB Clients and End-clients of EFAs client suitability (incl. investment strategy and ESG preferences), overall investment strategy (OIS), risk disclosures and distribution requirements are fulfilled and adhered to when sharing any documentation with clients.
      2. Cross-border restrictions (e.g. reverse solicitation) might apply: 
        • For PB Clients and End-clients of EFAs:  Please consult the applicable Country Guideline. This document cannot be distributed to all countries. The document disclaimer lists the maximum distribution scope (excluding the US). Any cross-border distribution must comply with the applicable Country Guideline.
        • For EAMs with Delegation of PM Activity: This document can only be distributed if a positive EAM Legal Opinion Memorandum concerning Delegation of PM Activity to BJB is available (see LO Repository). Please be reminded that for this service the TPMA & Addendum to the TPMA shall be signed by the End-client of the EAM. The DM Agreement incl. Investment Strategy selected by the EAM shall be signed by the EAM. 
      3. Consent on receiving documents in English: Make sure that every party involved who receives documentation in English, has agreed to receive it in English and understands the documentation’s content in all relevant aspects, especially if English is not their native language. 
      4. Document-specific considerations: Make sure that when changing the PowerPoint presentation, your amendments do not violate the principle of fair, clear and not misleading communication.
      5. Portfolio Cost Simulation: Where applicable, a Portfolio Cost Simulation must be compiled and sent to the client (or the EAM, if applicable) to fulfil regulatory obligations. More information and a web-based tool for the simulation can be found here.
