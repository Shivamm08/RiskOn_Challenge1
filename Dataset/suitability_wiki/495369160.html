Overview

The BMO Distribution and Tax rules provide automated checks, such as onshore and cross-border financial product distribution rules and tax rules, for about 60 countries.

The results of these checks are shown by different applications such as Products and Services Portal (PSP), Digital Advisory Suite (DiAS) and the BMO WEB Front End.

The relevant parameters required to perform the checks include RM Location, Booking Centre, Client Domicile, Service Model, Solicitation Type, and Client Classification, and of course the financial instruments attributes.

The BMO Rule Engine is able to check the offered and the booked universe of Julius Baer.
