How to define a suitable investment strategy

https://wiki.juliusbaer.com/spaces/fkh/pages/531883124/How+to+define+a+suitable+investment+strategy



According to point 3.2. of the global Suitability Policy, local policies set out on the client information necessary to allow for a suitability- and appropriateness assessment. The collected information must allow determining the following aspects: Investment Objectives, Knowledge and Experience in particular financial instrument categories, ability to bear financial risk as well as financial risk tolerance and Investment Strategy (...). Therefore, local policies regulate on further details about the suitability aspects. One of this topics is the investment strategy of the Client Investment Profile (CIP) with regards to the investment strategy of discretionary- and advisory mandate. At some JB-locations*, it is not allowed at all, having another investment strategy in the contract in comparison to the one in the CIP. At some JB-locations (e.g. RML CH), the local suitability policy allows having deviating investment strategies (CIP vs. contract), if explicitly wished by the client and if certain elements are fulfilled pre-contract. This means, the RM always needs to advice against such constellations. However, if explicitly wished by the client, the following elements must be considered and discussed with the client, and also documented in locally available systems before the contract is signed by the client:

  • Risk warning: Please explain to the client the risks of not having the same strategy in the mandate as in the CIP (e.g. possible higher gains come along with possible higher losses; explanation of the concrete differences between the 5 strategies)
  • Client's decision to keep the current strategy: Please ask the client for decision after the risk warning took place (e.g. client followed the explanation from the RM about the risks and characteristics of the deviating strategy and wants to keep the divergent strategy even though)
  • Rationale: Please ask the client why deviating strategy/ies in comparison to the CIP are wanted (e.g. client follows different strategies with other banks and explicitly wants to focus on a certain strategy at JB; core-satellite portfolio; portfolio with 'Liebhaberaktie', etc.)

These 3 elements must in all cases be documented in a client contact note (CCN) of the according locally available system. As such deviations can arise while using different tools or systems, please follow the according instructions:

  • If MSD (mandate solution designer) is used for creating contracts/mandates: Please follow the according instructions for documentation here: Pre-contract suitability checks in MSD - Suitability Wiki - Confluence
  • If MOC (modular offering configurator) is used for creating contracts/mandates: Please follow the according instructions for documentation here: Pre-contract suitability checks in MOC - Suitability Wiki - Confluence
  • If contract templates are directly taken from local JB-form inventories (e.g. CRM AOF, local JB-form repository), please document the above described 3 elements directly in a CCN of your local CRM-system and use the OIS-calculator for manual evaluation (e.g. CRM-CH contact note, T24, myCRM, etc.)


After the activation of discretionary- and advisory contracts/mandates (post-contract) in local systems, DiAS/WN calculates the so called 'Overall Investment Strategie' (OIS) - at locations where available and applicable. If the OIS of the portfolio/s under one ZR/BR is above the CIP-strategy, a OIS-Alert appears overnight in DiAS/WN, which needs to be justified within 5 business days. If such an alert appears, please follow the guidance here: Overall Investment Strategy Check - Suitability Wiki - Confluence


Important: It is not allowed to recommend deviating strategies to the client at all locations. The Bank needs to advice against any deviations in this regard and shall always offer strategies which are in line with the CIP (client investment profile) or – in case of multiple advisory / discretionary mandates under one ZR/BR – which result in an Overall Investment Strategy which is aligned with the CIP strategy.


In case of any doubts, please reach out to bfs or your local FRM-colleague for proper documentation handling.
