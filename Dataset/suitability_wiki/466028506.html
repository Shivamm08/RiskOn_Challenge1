1. Rationale for Alerts

There are three different reasons for alerts

  1. Regulatory Requirement: Basis is a regulatory requirement (MiFID, FIDLEG, PRIIPs), which requires the bank to perform a respective check or to provide a certain document to the client. 
  2. Contractual Agreement: Basis for these alerts is the contractual agreement with the client (Service Model Promise), in which JB promises the client certain services in return of a fee (advisory mandate fee).
  3. Business Opportunities: Basis for these alerts are potential business opportunities (recommendation of a certain instrument instead of another). It is optional, if these alerts want to be resolved. 

2. Criticality of Alerts

We distinguish between two different alert types.

Critical Alert 

  • Advisory session: Alert must be justified to execute the transaction 
  • Client book overnight monitoring: Alert must be resolved with a respective resolution note & the client must be informed within five business days.

Non-Critical Alert

  • Alerts do not need to be justified / resolved
  • No time frame given by Wealth Navigator

3. Responsibility

The responsibility to contact the client depends on the Service Model:

  • Investment Advisor: Advice Exclusive Direct (former Advice Advanced) 
  • Relationship Manager:
    • Advice Light
    • Advice Premium
    • Advice Exclusive Indirect (former Advice Advanced) 
    • Strategic Fund Access
    • Advice Dynamic
    • Portfolio Monitoring Mandate
    • Advice SCS
