Create Model Proposals for Prospects

https://wiki.juliusbaer.com/spaces/fkh/pages/382716138/Create+Model+Proposals+for+Prospects

We have realized a feature to enable mind blowing Prospect Proposals by making dedicated Investment Management Model Portfolios available in DiAS, so that our users can efficiently create Prospect Proposals in a simple manner.

How to use the "Model Proposal" functionality

Open DiAS an click on top on "PROPOSALS" afterwards on the right side click on the arrow and proceed by selecting "CREATE MODEL PROPOSAL"


A dialog opens to configure your Portfolio in 4 steps: 
- Select the applicable Investment Strategy for your client
- Select the applicable Reference Currency
- Select a Client Domicile (if a dedicated client domicile is not available select world as the Proposal Module will in a second step apply all checks required to remove instrument not suitable in your dedicated client context)
- Include an investment amount and hit “Create Proposal” 


If you want to have a first glimpse of the instruments that will be used to create your prospect proposal you can click on the info button on the right side and browse through the instruments.



This will lead you to the proposal module. The selected values are pre-filled and the allocations are calculated automatically. You can further tailor investment parameters, client information, distribution information etc.
All the selections are applied to the Model. Once completed, please move to the positions screen. 

On the positions screen you will now see an overview of all the positions of your model proposal with predefined weights. You can further tailor the proposal through removing and adding positions or changing the respective weights to create a tailored advisory prospect proposal for your dedicated client needs. 


Once you are satisfied with your proposal, please hit the “Recalculate Notifications” button to re-run the checks and confirm that the distribution of your proposal and sending of the client package to the prospect customer is permitted.


Check out the Model Prospect Proposal you have created and tailor it further using the configurator before sending it out to your prospect. 


Please note:

• This is the first version of this new feature. More functionalities will follow in due course depending on prioritization.
• The feature is available for prospect proposals only.
• Checks/Notifications: please be aware that some notifications (e.g. Sell recommendation, Share Class Selection) could be triggered and need to be individually handled
