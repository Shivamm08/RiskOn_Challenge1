The development of our ESG Investment Rating Methodology stems partly from regulatory requirements to ask clients in the EU what their ESG preferences are. However, the way the methodology was developed is the approach that Julius Baer has decided to take. Therefore, other banks have and will probably develop other ways of fulfilling these regulatory requirements. Regardless of the approach a bank decides to take, the integration of ESG factors into the corporate and investment processes can be done in many different ways.
