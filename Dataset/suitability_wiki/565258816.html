FAQ - Advisory KRI Scorecard

https://wiki.juliusbaer.com/spaces/fkh/pages/565258816/FAQ+-+Advisory+KRI+Scorecard

Q1: Would it indeed become the Relationship Manager's (RM) responsibility if an Investment Advisors (IA) does not handle overnight alerts correctly for an Advice Exclusive / Advanced or IA mandate?

  • The answer depends on the alert and mandate type.

For the OIS (Overall Investment Strategy) alert, the RM is always responsible. Similarly, the RM is responsible for all other alerts in an Advice Exclusive / Advanced / IA mandate indirect,  

If the IA fails to handle alerts (except OIS alert) correctly in Advice Exclusive / Advanced / IA mandate direct, the RM is not responsible. In such cases, the overdue alerts are excluded from the RMs Advisory KRI Scorecard.

Note that the Advisory KRI Scorecard concept is not applicable to IAs, who have their own risk framework. This framework ensures close monitoring of control findings and considers risk behavior during year-end evaluations..


Q2: In case an (A)RM would enter a session / order for Advice Exclusive / Advanced mandate and does a mistake, does that impact the RM?

  • Yes, it does as control reports are generated based on the individual who performs the DIAS session or transaction, rather than the service model used. 

As a result, any sessions or transactions entered by a (A)RM are reviewed by the relevant FRM team. It's worth noting that errors made by an assistant will ultimately be attributed to the accountable RM.
