Providing advice on Digital Asset

https://wiki.juliusbaer.com/spaces/fkh/pages/377953321/Providing+advice+on+Digital+Asset

Scope (Booking centre and RM location Switzerland)

Providing advice (active and reverse solicitation) on Digital Assets is only allowed for Relationship Managers (RMs) and Investment Advisors (IAs), when the client is booked and served out of Switzerland.


Mandatory Pre-Requisites for RMs / IAs

Before providing any advice to clients RMs/IAs need

  • a line management approval (only for RMs)
  • to have completed DA training modules 1-3. Module 4 and 5 are not mandatory. Module 5, however, is strongly recommended for RMs serving crypto-savvy clients.
    • Digital Assets Module 1 – Fundamentals
    • Digital Assets Module 2 – Trends, Investment View & Products
    • Digital Assets Module 3 – Advisory Journey

    • Digital Assets Module 4 – Wealth Planning

    • Digital Assets Module 5- Digital Assets Onboarding
  • to have received Certification by Digital Assets Development team

Advisory Process Steps

The Crypto Handbook (or type cryptohandbook in your browser) contains more in-depth information on each of the steps displayed in below process overview.


1 – Client Eligibility

RM/IA verifies client eligibility criteria for Digital Asset advice, thereby, clarifying the possibilities of active and reverse solicitation scenarios.


Steps to be completed – see slides 4-11 of Crypto Handbook

1

Verify that the client is not domiciled in a country with a DA ban (banned Country list)

2

For UK/EEA-domiciled clients: Verify that she/he is a professional client under MiFID II
3Verify eligibility of active solicitation for EEA-domiciled clients (i.e. EEA appendix)
4

Ensure that the client meets minimum criteria of target investor profile, reference is set to  JB DTM Matrix

Financial Risk AbilityInvestment Strategy
at least "moderate"at least "balanced"

2 – Client Readiness

RM/IA ensures the account holder signature of the Digital Asset Waiver and that respective order givers are educated on Digital Assets and the corresponding risks.


Steps to be completed (before first advice on DA) – see slides 12-15 of Crypto Handbook

1

Send DA Waiver (Special Terms & Conditions & Risk Disclosure Appendix1) &
obtain account holder signature before offering advice or any trade in crypto tokens

1also required for unsolicited transactions in crypto tokens

2

Send education material to client and educate client/order giver on DA; update the K&E directly in CLM; document client education in CRM contact note

3

Send DA pricing sheet to client (only before trading crypto tokens for the first time)2

2also required as substitute for pre-trade cost disclosure in case of trades via trading desk

3 – Offering Fit

RM/IA selects products (crypto tokens and financial instruments with DA underlying) considering universe status and potential restrictions.


Steps to be completed – see slides 16-20 of Crypto Handbook

1

Access products (crypto tokens and financial instruments with DA underlying) in PSP/DiAS Advanced Search (for relevant information and) to observe universe status as a base for solicitation

2

For StP/ETP, check for additional selling restrictions in product documentation

(KID, factsheet, prospectus, etc.)

4 – Advisory Session

RM/IA advises client, performs suitability checks and places trades

Start order entry either in Toolbox for crypto tokens or in DiAS for financial instruments with DA underlying and begin an advisory session
