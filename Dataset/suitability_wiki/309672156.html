4. Contact Note Documentation - Best Practice

Please find below the best practice example to document the steps performed when following the WN fall-back scenario guidelines:

TAP Contact Note documentation best practice:

Contact Type: Transaction
Topic: MiFID

Description: Suitability outside WN:

1.Pre Checks:

    • Country of domicile of the account holder: Italy
    • Client Classification: Retail
    • Investment Strategy: Capital Gain
    • Client’s mandate and mandate strategy in the portfolio: Premium - Capital Gain
    • K&E (of order giver): GOOD - > 5 Trades
    • ESG preferences: Traditional Investment
    • Financial Risk Ability: High
    • Time Horizon: More than 10 years
    • Solicitation: Actively solicited

ISIN: LU1437018598

2.Distribution Check

No alert appeared during the BMO check; distribution is possible.
(Any alert should be described here, along with the justification.)

Product universe Status: Offered

3. Target Market Test:
The client is in the instrument’s positive target market
(If documentation indicates that PSP target market data is incorrect, the inconsistency must be documented.)

4. Suitability & Appropriateness Test:

  • Knowledge & Experience: The client has good knowledge and experience with the traded product.
    (If the client needs to be educated or warned, this should be clearly documented, including the communication provided.)
  • The client’s ESG preference is aligned with the traded product.
  • The product risk rating (PRR) is 3, which is consistent with the client’s investment strategy

If PRR above investment strategy: The PRR is 7, which exceeds the client’s investment strategy; however, the overall portfolio CPR will remain within the accepted investment strategy threshold.

(In the case of an unsolicited scenario, the client may accept the risk, but this must be documented.)

5. Pre-Trade Documents:
The KID and suitability report were provided to the client prior to the trade (see attachment).

→ LU1437018598 is suitable for the client.
