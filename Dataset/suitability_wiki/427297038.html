1. Is there an opt out / delete possibility?

No, there is no opt-out or delete possibility. All calls within the defined scope are recorded on an ‘always on’ basis.

2. Can I access my recordings?

Yes, you can access your own recordings: A detailed listener guide can be found here:

    • VREC Listener

3. How long are recorded calls stored?

Recorded calls will be kept for five (5) years (or longer, depending on the individual local country laws). If not marked (e.g. for investigation purposes), the calls are going to be deleted automatically after the retention time.

4. Do I still need to capture a CRM Contact Note?

Yes, this is still required. And please be careful to mention the right date and time stamp when documentation the contact so that the respective recording can be found later on.

5. Are Webex Meetings with clients from Germany recorded as well?

No, because the phone recording is limited to numbers of the counter party with the area code +49. When you dial in to a Webex Session, you do this by calling a Swiss phone number.
