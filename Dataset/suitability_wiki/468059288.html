2. Description of the alert

Certain regulations or internal standards require the delivery of mandatory disclosure documents (“MDD”) prior to the placement of the order.

Not exhaustive list:

  • Key Information Document (“KID”) for financial instruments that are classified as Packaged Retail Investment and Insurance Products ("PRIIPS")( →KIDs for PRIIPs)
  • German Produktinformationsblatt for equities and bonds
  • Prospectus for Non-Traditional-Funds


3. What triggers this alert?

The MDD-alert is triggered in the Advisory session as a result of newly proposed transactions when the required MDD is not available. It is a session alert that applies for each solicitation type: active, reverse and unsolicited depending on the regulatory requirement (→)


4. How can this alert be resolved?

You can resolve the alert with one of the following actions:

RM/IA must upload the correct MDD in the session. The MDD can be sourced from external repositories or created in the toolboxes; further information can be found under →Where can KIDs be retrieved or created?

If the latest document has already been provided to the client (e.g. with an investment proposal) the RM/IA may select "document already provided" in the alert justification and document "when" and through which "channel" the document has been provided.
