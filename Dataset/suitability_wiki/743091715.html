2. Description of the alert

With the enforcement of the UK regulation Consumer Duty in 2023, the English regulator (Financial Conduct Authority - FCA) introduced fair value assessments, as a requirement linked to the price and value outcome. The requirements apply to both product manufacturers and distributors, aiming to ensure that investment funds represent fair value to customers. Fair value means the costs are proportionate to the benefits that the fund provides.

Manufacturers of funds registered in the UK must assess whether the fund provides fair value to its target customers and share information about their fair value assessment with distributors, so that distributors can meet their obligations under this requirement.

UK distributors, such as Julius Baer International, must not distribute a product unless its distribution arrangements are consistent with the product providing fair value to retail customers. In most cases, this means using the manufacturers fair value assessment, which is pulled through from our systems where available.

However, in cases where no such fair value assessment is available from the manufacturer, the obligation lies with the distributor. In such cases, for clients advised out of the advisory location UK (JB International), RM/IA must perform a manual check as outlined below to ensure the fund provides fair value.


Manual check:

The Total Expense Ratio (TER) / Ongoing Charges Figure (OCF) of the fund (typically found on the Key Information Document (KID) or Factsheet) is compared to the JB Investment Charging Threshold (ICT) table below, for the relevant Fund Type:

If the fund’s TER ‘is greater’ than the ICT (i.e. the Fund’s costs are more expensive than our Fair Value threshold) = a failed test (fund does not represent fair value)

If the fund’s TER ‘is equal to or below’ the ICT (i.e. the Fund’s costs are cheaper than our Fair Value threshold = a passed test (fund represents fair value)


3. What triggers this alert?

The fair value (UK Consumer Duty) alert can be triggered for UK advised clients either:

a) in the Advisory Session as a result of a newly proposed buy-like transaction if

  • the selected fund does not represent fair value or
  • the fair value data/assessment is not available

b) in the Client Book as a result of an overnight portfolio risk monitoring when a portfolio has fund positions that do not meet the fair value requirements (e.g. the outcome of the manufacturer fair value assessment has changed or fair value data on this fund is no longer available)

You can resolve the alert with one of the following actions:
