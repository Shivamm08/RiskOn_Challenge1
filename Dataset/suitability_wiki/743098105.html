E-Channel Enforcement for Client Package, PAR & PCN

https://wiki.juliusbaer.com/spaces/fkh/pages/743098105/E-Channel+Enforcement+for+Client+Package+PAR+PCN

The delivery of mandatory pre-trade documents (e.g. KID for PRIIPS) will be enforced in the Advisory Session through automatic pre-selection of an e-channel (e-banking or email), if available for that order giver.
Selecting an additional channel will be possible on top of the enforced channel, e.g. e-Banking plus send to myself.
Please also note that the e-channel enforcement logic also applies to the Portfolio Configuration Notification (PCN) as well as the PAR (which needs to be sent to the Account Holder / Authorised Signatory at least once per year, for applicable advisory service models).


E-Channel enforcement at client package/PCN/PAR distribution:

Removal of "e-mail to me/ send to myself" as standalone distribution option for Client Package & PCN

Client packages:

For orders requiring pre-trade documents, trade processing will be blocked if no e-channel exists to distribute them. (A)RMs/IAs will receive a warning during order giver selection if no valid e-channel is available. The block can be lifted by either adding the order giver’s email or enabling e-Banking.
Exception: If the order giver has an approved exception (EBAN-ETP), the “email to me /send to myself” option remains active, and no block applies. Provision of the client package (e.g. via physical mail or during client meeting) must be document in the CCN. These cases are subject to controls.

Portfolio Configuration Notifications (PCNs):

Amendments to investment guidelines (e.g., SAA thresholds) require an e-channel for PCN delivery. Processing is blocked without one. A warning appears at contact partner selection. Resolution requires adding the partner’s email or setting up e-Banking.
Exception: If the contact partner has an approved exception (EBAN-ETP), the “email to me /send to myself” option remains active, and no block applies. Provision of the PCN (e.g. via physical mail or during client meeting) must be document in the CCN. These cases are subject to controls.
