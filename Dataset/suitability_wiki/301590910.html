Please find below the frequently asked questions around the CIP:

Key word

Question

Answer

investment objective, cash flow

Why is it possible to define a supplementary investment objective (i.e. cash flow generation)?


This provides the RM further information how to choose the asset allocation


CDS/scanning

What do I have to do with the completed and signed form?

Please send the completed document to CDS for further processing (scanning). The document will be filed in CLM. The locations using myCRM or CRM(Asia) have to manually enter the CIP information in their local systems.

review

A client has already completed a CIP. Some time later the client decides to change the investment objectives or to revise the financial risk ability / risk tolerance. Is a new CIP required?


Yes, in such a case the client needs to complete a new CIP (incl. client signature) since basic parameters have changed.

printing

​Do I have to print the CIP one-sided or duplex?

​The CIP can be printed and handed in to CDS one-sided or duplex.

client acceptance

What about if a client refuses either to complete or to sign the CIP?

The CIP helps building the foundation for tailored advice and individual investment solutions. Therefore, the profile needs to be assessed in close and personal collaboration together with the client. In addition, the information gathered in the client profile is necessary for the suitability- & appropriateness checks. These checks are required by the regulator. In the light of the above, the client must sign the forms.

purpose

For which purposes will the client investment profile be used?

The collected data assessed will be used to check suitability and/or appropriateness of any advice given to a client in order to meet the regulatory requirements (e.g. MiFID II, FinSA, etc.).

form

Is the CIP only available as paper-based version or is there also an e-form available?

Following versions of the CIP are available:

> CLM guided version

> CRM AOF

> local systems/intranet for local-specific forms

new account, existing client

What happens if an existing account holder opens a new account?

For each new account - even if it is for an existing account holder - a new CIP has to be completed. However, it is possible to apply the same CIP on all accounts with the same account holder constellation. Please refer to the CIP page ‘General data’.

languages

In which languages is the CIP form available?

German, English, French, Italian and Spanish

myCRM, CRM Asia

How is the CIP form implemented in myCRM and CRM (Asia)?

Values are entered manually into the local CRM systems - CIP and K&E. The forms need to be sent to the relevant booking center.

signature

Who has to sign the form?

Private individuals: All account holders
Legal entities: Authorized signatories according to the signatory requirements of the bank

client profiling, exception

Who has to be profiled?

As a matter of principle the account holder has to be profiled. In cases where the CIPLIC applies, the necessary information regarding the beneficial owner is relevant. Further information regarding the CIPLIC can be found here.


The CIP is not required in case of service models/type of relationships, which do not comprise advisory/portfolio monitoring services by JB and where JB does not invest at the account of the client:

> Trade Basic (formerly ‘Execution-only’); for this type of relationship, it is necessary to collect the information regarding the knowledge and experience level of the account holder(s). For this, the dedicated form ‘Knowledge and Experience’ has to be completed and signed by the order giver.

> Non-Trading-Account

> Global Custody

> EAM

> EFA - K&E-only form is required

business relation, ZR

How many CIP do I need for a business relation (ZR)?

The CIP applies on ZR level and not on custody account level (except Asia). In case of ZR’s with identical account holder constellation, the same CIP can be used for all ZRs.

authorized signatory, signature, PoA

Do all authorized signatories (AS) of a legal entity need to sign the CIP?

For legal entities, the same signatory rules as indicated in the signature forms of the account opening package apply. E.g. if the signature rules imply a joint signature by three, then three signatures with that authorization are required.

joint account, signature

In case of a joint account - who has to sign the CIP form?

All account holders.

partner, ZR

Is the client investment profile (CIP) linked to the partner or the ZR?

The CIP is linked to the ZR. In case a client has several ZR’s, the CIP is applied to all ZR’s unless there is a dedicated CIP for a specific ZR available.
The K&E profile is maintained on partner level.

CLM

Where do I see the CIP and the K&E data in the CLM system?

The CIP is shown in the tab 'Investment Profile' of the BR-Level in CLM.

The K&E data of both types of account holder individuals and legal entities are displayed in CLM on partner level in the tab 'Investment Profile'

Remark: The K&E is registered on partner level. All authorized signatories who act as order givers must complete separate K&E-only forms. 

education

Where do I get information on how to complete the client investment profile?

You may find a step-by-step guide here: CIP - what is it for? - Suitability Wiki - Confluence (juliusbaer.com)

K&E

What is the Knowledge & Experience form for?

The K&E information builds part of the client profiling. It is kept in a separate form. Each account holder(s) and each authorized person who acts as order giver need to fill in and sign such a form.

leverage

What is the leverage section for?

The leverage section is not part anymore of the CIPs in the following booking centers:

> Since September 2023: BC Luxembourg and BC Monaco

> Since November 2023: BC Switzerland and BC Guernsey

The remaining BC's still have the leverage section (including RML UK) in their current CIP. For some locations/BC it is mandatory to gather and for others not.

risk profile, investment objective, investment strategy

What are the interdependencies between the overall risk profile, the investment strategy and the mandate type (contract)? 

The CIP documents the investment-related situation of the client. Amongst others, these are the investment objectives and the overall risk profile consisting of client’s risk ability and risk tolerance. The investment strategy of the client is derived from the indications about the investment objectives and the overall risk profile and represents a recommendation of JB for a personalized and suitable investment strategy to the client. Therefore, it is recommended to choose a strategy in the mandate which is in line with the one of the CIP.

If the client choses a strategy in the mandate contract which does not match with the one in the CIP, the client must be informed regarding the inherent risks of this deviation. This individual risk disclosure has to be documented in a client contact note (CRM, myCRM, etc.). Please consult your local suitability policies for proper procedure and regulatory requirement. 

PoA

A person has a Power of Attorney (PoA) for several accounts of different clients. How many K&E forms does the PoA need to complete?

Only one K&E form is required. The K&E profile is applied on partner level.

product risk

An existing client has an investment objective 'capital preservation' but invested the assets in 'blue chip equities'. The determined investment strategy e.g. is (fixed) income which would allow only a very limited quote of equities. The client perception is that the blue chips are 'conservative'. Is he forced to re-sell his blue chip equities and to re-invest into government bonds?

For each investment strategy, the quotes for the different asset categories (e.g. cash products, bonds, equities) are defined and there are bandwidths for each category. This means, there is some flexibility in allocating the assets.

The following options apply to the portfolios with local suitability requirements & the requirements of a specific mandate:

> Review of client investment profile

> Re-allocate

> Review mandate

validity

Is the assessed client investment profile still valid when the RM changes job within the bank or leaves the bank?

Yes, the CIP data remains valid even if the RM changes the job within the bank or leaves the bank.

joint account, K&E

If two K&E forms have been completed (for a joint account), what is the impact during the account opening process and for the investment process (ordering party)?

In case of joint accounts, for every account holder who gives orders a dedicated K&E profile must be completed. 

The situation is different if for the same order giver two different K&E profiles have been obtained. In this case, once the K&E profiles are scanned in CLM, a work item is generated requesting to indicate which one of the K&E profiles shall apply.

legal entity account, K&E

If the authorized signatories do have collective signature rights: Which K&E needs to be considered?

The K&E of the dedicated order giver is considered while entering the trade in DiAS. For the collective signatory check, the K&E of the partner who needs to provide consent is not relevant.

appropriateness, MiFID

Can a client with an investment objective 'capital preservation' still give an order to invest in a very risky product? (applicable for MiFID II advisory locations only)

As long as the resulting portfolio risk is less high or equal to the overall risk profile and the client has sufficient knowledge, the trade can be executed.

beneficial owner, PoA

What form do I need for a partner who has different roles such as BO (Beneficial Owner), PoA (Power of Attorney) or AS (Authorized Signatory) at the same time?

The form K&E is mapped on partner level. Therefore, the same K&E profile of a person applies on all ZRs where this person has a role as a partner (PoA, authorized signature, account holder, etc.).
A beneficial owner must have power of disposition (e.g. PoA, authorized signature, management power, account holder) on the account, otherwise she/he cannot act as an order giver.

advice location, booking centre

Which CIP applies to a client with advice location Hong Kong or Singapore but booking center Switzerland?

For RM located out of Hongkong but booked either in Switzerland or Singapore, specific CIP forms have to be used. These CIP forms are available under the respective booking centers. For advice out of Singapore, the CIP forms of the booking centers have to be used.

insurance wrapper

The account holder of a ZR is an insurance company but the beneficial owner is a private individual. What form is used and who needs to sign?

The CIP for ‘Life Insurance companies, pension solution providers, fiduciary companies (CIPLIC) needs to be used. For insurance wrapper the insurance company, as our contractual relationship, needs to sign the CIP and the RM.
Further information can be found here.

advice location, booking center

An RM unfortunately used the CIP form for the wrong booking center. Is it possible just to replace the last page (containing the disclaimer and booking center address) or does he need to complete the whole form again?

No, this is not possible. A new form is required.

portfolio management

Why must clients having a managed mandate complete 1) the CIP resp. 2) the K&E section? Does limited or no knowledge for certain asset categories prevent to invest in such categories (for clients having a managed mandate)?

The information gathered in the client investment profile allows to select an appropriate mandate strategy. For clients having a managed mandate, the K&E needs to be obtained as well even though portfolio management is applied (regulatory duty).

ZR, mortgage, cash

A client has a ZR which is only used for holding of e.g. strategic cash / security positions or loans.

If no trading activities (including fiduciaries) are foreseen on a ZR, it can be managed as a so called ‘Non-Trading-Account’ (NTA) for which a CIP is not required. For this, the document ‘Non-Trading-Accounts’ has to be signed by the client and the ZR to be coded as a ‘non-trading account’ in the systems CRM/HOST. 

Please be aware that trading activities are not allowed on NTA – accounts and therefore will be blocked, whereas cash transactions are allowed.

trust, foundation

If a Trust or a Foundation acts as account holder: Is there the need for a CIP?

Yes, a CIP for legal entities has to be completed.

client contact, mailing

Can I just simply mail the client investment profile to the client?

Sole mailing of the form for completion by the client itself is not deemed as appropriate. The CIP helps building the foundation for tailored advice and individual investment solutions. Therefore, the profile must be assessed in close and personal collaboration between the RM and the client.

documents

Where do I find the CIP forms and related documents?

For advisory locations using CLM, the CIP shall be completed via the tab 'Investment Profile'.

The CIP forms for the different Booking Centers can be found here. The CIP is also available in Account Opening Forms 'Appway'.

risk profile, investment strategy

A (new) account holder requests an investment strategy/a mandate type that is more risky than the resulting one based on the CIP. Does the RM/IA need to convince the client to choose a more conservative strategy or can the bank offer the more aggressive strategy since the client signs the CIP?

It is not possible to select in the CIP itself a riskier mandate strategy than the resulting investment strategy would allow.

However, the client has the possibility to decide* on a different investment strategy in a specific service model (e.g. JB Advice Premium) than the one recommended in the CIP. In this case the relationship manager has to warn the client on the related risks. The client’s acknowledgment of the risk disclosure as well as its decision to keep the deviating investment strategy in the service model contract is to be internally documented.

*It has to be noted that the RM/IA must not recommend actively a deviating investment strategy in the service model contract. Please consult your local suitability policies for proper processes as in some locations, deviations might not be allowed due to regulatory requirements.

investment strategy, mandate strategy

Is there a technical enforcement that the investment strategy must fit to the mandate strategy?

No, there is no technical enforcement but the OIS Check in DiAS informs you about any deviation between the Investment Strategy in the CIP form compared to the strategy of the advisory- and/discretionary mandate: Overall Investment Strategy Check - Suitability Wiki - Confluence (juliusbaer.com)

form 

How do I know which CIP-form/version is the newest? Do I need to use the ESG- or the non-ESG-version?

For locations using CLM, please always fill in and print the form out of CLM as CLM shows you the newest and the correct version for your dedicated location. If the ESG or the non-ESG-version is applied, derives from the fact, if your RM-location had the ESG rollout or not (application of Sustainable Finance Rules). If you select the CIP from Appway, a message within a red frame informs you about the applicability of ESG in the dedicated locations.
