FCR's are Private Equity Funds in which Retail clients are able to invest in, if they are registered with the Spanish regulator as "is Retail Eligible". 

RM Location Luxembourg and Spain are registered with the Spanish regulator and are therefore able to actively distribute FCR's to Retail clients, domiciled in Spain. Other RM Locations do not have this registration and are therefore not able to advise Spanish clients on FCR's. Based on a Risk decision, the Bank does not allow any unsolicited sales either. 

This distribution setup is enforced via a respective BMO alert., which can only be oversteered with Data Exception. 


Distributor Target Market (DTM) basically blocks any investment of a Retail client into Private Equity funds. For specific FCR Valor's this block is removed.
