Advice Premium/Exclusive 

What is the regulatory background?

As BJB Switzerland is not licensed, it may only provide new services to EEA clients if such activity is carried out at the client’s own exclusive initiative (“reverse solicitation”), without prior solicitation. Because of this, BJB Switzerland must be able to provide records showing how the client relationship was established and whether the client has taken the initiative to open the account and to receive new investment services or products. Furthermore, without case-specific documentation that the client relationship and service provision have been established on the client's own and exclusive initiative, BJB cannot rely on the “reverse solicitation” exception and is exposed to civil and regulatory liability risk.

With this solution we transition from the tactical K&E approach to the strategic set-up, leveraging the Strategic Asset allocation of the clients investment strategy. 

What does this mean?

EEA clients served out of Switzerland with an Advice Premium or Advice Exclusive (former Advice Advanced) mandate can be actively advised on instruments that are in line with their chosen investment strategy (What instruments are in which asset class (see Appendix)? / What are the asset classes per investment strategy?).

Important remark: you are required to duly document the client request for opening the advisory mandate / for a new product.

  • For new clients (>19.09.22):
    Every client signing a new Advice Premium / Advice Exclusive (former Advice Advanced) mandate will receive such an Appendix (this is triggered automatically). Furthermore, it is key that the origin of the request (“reverse solicitation”) is documented in a comprehensive and detailed manner. Without such documentation, the contract will not be activated by CDS.
    See section: What are the documentation requirements for new Advice Premium / Advice Advance mandates, a new Investment strategy or new products?
  • For existing clients (mandate signature before 19.09.22) in case they change their investment strategy or want to invest into new instruments (e.g. Digital Assets):
    Only once your client wants to change the Investment Strategy or invest into new product categories (e.g. Digital Assets), a new Appendix is required.
    Note: if your client requests a new product category (e.g. Digital Assets) document the reverse solicitation of the new product category, send a copy of the Appendix to the client and archive the duplicate via CDS. There is no need to send the Appendix to your existing clients unless there is any change (the current asset classes applied to the advisory mandates and the included financial instruments remained the same over the past several years and this is in line with how we have been serving our clients in the past.)
    See section: What are the documentation requirements for new Advice Premium / Advice Exclusive (former Advice Advanced) mandates, a new Investment strategy or new products?

In the Contact Note in CRM: What are the documentation requirements for new Advice Premium / Advice Exclusive mandates, a new Investment strategy or new products?

An Advice Premium / Advice Exclusive mandate for EEA clients out of Switzerland can only be opened under reverse solicitation principles (i.e., the client’s own exclusive initiative without prior solicitation). The same is true for a change in the investment strategy. This means a Client Contact Note (CCN) is required to document this in a comprehensive and detailed manner, including the following mandatory information:

  • Who initiated the contact with the Bank? And (ii) under what circumstances?
  • Who initiated the account opening | service provision | change of investment strategy? And (ii) under what circumstances?
    (The Bank | RM may not initiate any account openings or provision of new services for clients domiciled in the EEA)
  • Was there any previous solicitation by RM
    (The Bank/RM may not do any (also not previous) solicitation)
  • The initiation for the account opening | service provision | change of investment strategy took place:
    Where (location?)
    How (phone, meeting, email etc.?)
    When (date):
  • What was discussed
    (specific services | products requested and or offered, if not already documented under the above questions)
  • What documents | information was sent | exchanged?
    (Please list or attach document (like Appendix) or refer to CCN-ID of communication, email exchange)

The following examples can serve as guidance for account openings:

Example 1 for account opening:

“I met Ms. XY at a book launch (location/date). She asked me to give her my business card. On (date) she has contacted me via email (Ref. to email received) and wanted to have concrete information about our product offering (please document as much detail as possible). On her request I sent her our brochure (Ref. to email sent) and answered her following questions. She then decided to open an account with us. For this purpose, she came to our premises in Bahnhofstrasse on (date).”

Example 2 for account opening

“One of my clients is very convinced of our investment expertise and has therefore given my contact details to his colleague DFG. DFG contacted me (date) by phone and inquired about the account opening formalities and investment possibilities (see my CNN dated xx). Upon his request (“Reverse Solicitation”), I agreed to travel to his domicile to answer all his questions in detail. He then decided to open an account relationship with us and asked me to send him the account opening documentation to his email xxxxx (Ref. to email sent). PS: I have checked the applicable Country Guidelines to make sure that to travel and send account opening documentation is allowed”

Example 3 for account opening

“A. has read (Details of where?) about Julius Baer’s ESG offering and contacted me (when? How?) to get further information. Upon his request I send him (what? Ref. to email sent) and answered his follow-up question on a call (What and under what circumstances? When?). After intensive email exchange (I tried to answer all his question to the best of my knowledge), A. finally decided to open a Sustainability Mandate and came to Basel to sign the documents on (date).”

The following examples can serve as guidance for new products / change of the investment strategy

Example 1 for new products e.g. digital assets:

"Ariane (documented as MiFID professional client) has called me on (date) and told me she heard through a colleague about an investment opportunity in digital assets. She asked me for an investment into Bitcoin. I educated her on digital assets during our phone call and have emailed her additional education material (ref. to email sent) together with the digital risk waiver and the Annex to her Advice Premium mandate."

Example 2 for change of the investment strategy:

"Ron asked me to meet him (where?) to discuss his new financial situation as he has recently inherited a large amount. During our meeting he expressed the wish to invest in a more "aggressive" way. This lead us to reassess his entire financial situation and to revise his investment strategy. He has signed the adapted CIP and the Advisory contract with the amended strategy (new: capital gain, before: balanced). With the new mandate contract I also handed out the Appendix."

Important remarks to consider with regards to account opening and in a cross-border context:

  • Always check applicable country guidelines for country specific restrictions (NAVEX)
  • The Client request must be documented as detailed as possible
    i.e., conversation was with whom, when, where, contact method, which topics initiated by whom, detailed circumstances & content (services/products/products categories discussed)
  • Documentation must be in a granularity to allow a third party to retrieve all interactions
  • You can always react to the Client's statements and answer his/her questions. If the Client asks for alternatives or options, you can respond, and provide a high-level answer of our service offering. -
  • Stay in line with what the Client told you about what he/she wants ( i.e. “you told me that you want more interaction with me on …, Do I understand correctly that you are interested in …, Do you want me to further explain?”).
  • The information provided must be in line with the Client’s request, without soliciting other investment services or financial products that are not covered, or where the mention is not triggered by the Client's request.
  • After the Client conversation please follow up with an email providing the specific information discussed referring to the Client’s request(s) (i.e., “as requested by you during our phone conversation this morning …”).
  • Ideally, the Client comes to Switzerland to discuss any account opening or new mandates and to sign new forms. If the Clients requests it, the contractual documents can be sent cross-border for signature if permitted by the country guidelines (e.g., for France this is not allowed, Italy only upon exceptional prior authorization provided by the MH).
