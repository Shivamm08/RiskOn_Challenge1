The ESG Report

https://wiki.juliusbaer.com/spaces/fkh/pages/357306483/The+ESG+Report

1.- Why was an ESG report sent?

This is a management decision and furthermore, by August 2nd 2022 we had a regulatory obligation to ask clients for their ESG preferences. Due to these reasons, the report was sent out to clients.

2.- Will the ESG report be sent out on a regular basis?

No, the ESG report distribution was a one-off.

3.- Why don't all clients receive a report?

The decision of which clients receive a report follows some strict criteria. The reason that some clients that did not receive a report is that their portfolios are too small, contain mainly asset classes that are not covered by the methodology or have too many instruments where we don't have ESG information. Furthermore, the ESG report is currently available for clients in booked in CH/GG or LU with RM Locations Switzerland, Luxembourg, Spain and Ireland only.

4.- Can a report be ordered for a client that requested it?

Yes, for clients that are in in-scope locations, a report can be ordered in OnDemand (see details here: ESG Report in OnDemand - Suitability Wiki - Confluence (juliusbaer.com))
