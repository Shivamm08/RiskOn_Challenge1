2. Description of the alert

The K&E alert checks, if the order giver (who may differ from the account holder) has the respective knowledge and experience (K&E) in a certain asset class. Therefore each order giver needs to complete an own K&E-form with the according knowledge- & experience-levels for each asset class. 

The alert differentiates between the solicitation scenarios, applicable regulations (i.e. MiFID II and FinSA/FIDLEG) and the assessment for direct and indirect investments (indirect investments are only considered for K&E forms that contain 19 asset categories: The advisory locations UK and BC SG & HK are therefore excluded as they still operate with 10 K&E asset classes). For direct investments (e.g. investments in bonds, stocks etc.) Wealth Navigator checks the K&E in the respective instrument. For indirect investments (e.g. investment in funds, structured products etc.) it checks the K&E in the respective instrument and the knowledge of the underlying(s).


Examples for different investments:

Direct investment: Microsoft Shares → K&E Category “Equity instruments” is checked

Indirect investment: 

  • Barrier-Reverse Convertible on Microsoft shares → K&E Category “Structured Products – non-principal protected products” and K Category “Equity instruments” are checked
  • Fund that contains Microsoft shares and bonds → K&E Category "Traditional funds", K Category "Equity instruments" and K Category "Investment grade bonds" are checked

Special Case: Multi-Asset Class Fund:

  • Sufficient data available: K&E Category “Traditional funds” or “Non-traditional funds”, K Category “Equity Instruments” and K Category “Investment grade bonds” are checked (no further drilldown)
  • Not sufficient data available on the underlying’s: only the K&E Category “Traditional funds” or “Non-traditional funds” is checked


3. What triggers this alert?

The K&E alert is triggered in the Advisory Session for a buy-like transaction, if

  1. an order giver lacks a completed K&E form or
  2. has insufficient (see table below) knowledge and experience levels for the asset category of the instrument in the transaction

and it depends on the applicable regulation of the Advisory Location:

  1. MiFID:
    • Luxembourg, Ireland, Spain, UK
    • Switzerland, Monaco and Guernsey for EEA- & UK domiciled clients
  2. Standard Suitability (FinSA/FIDLEG):
    • Switzerland, Monaco and Guernsey for all non-EEA-non-UK client domiciles
    • South Africa, Brazil, Uruguay and Chile for all client domiciles
    • Dubai, Bahrain and Abu Dhabi for all client domiciles and booked in BC CH or BC GG

5. Justification example(s)#justificationexample

 Use Case

Example

Session Alert in an unsolicited scenario: 'I have warned'

The client wanted to invest into xy shares on an unsolicited basis but does not have any knowledge nor experience in trading in this instrument class. I warned the client about the lack of knowledge/experience in this instrument class but the client still decided to proceed with the trade.

Session Alert in a solicited scenario: 'I have considered'

I have considered the client’s lack of experience while advising him/her to invest in this instrument class class by providing additional information about the risk behaviour of this instrument by phone.

Session Alert in a solicited scenario: 'I will educate'

I educated the client on the instrument class 'equity instruments’ by phone based on the education material I had sent beforehand via e-mail/e-banking. He/she confirmed to me to have understood the contents and that he/she is fine to proceed investing into xy shares. He/she understands that the level of knowledge for 'equity instruments' will be permanently changed to 'good'.
