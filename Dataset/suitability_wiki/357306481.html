1.- What is the relationship between the MSCI ESG Rating and the JB ESG categorization of an instrument?

There is no clear correlation between the MSCI rating and our own methodology. The reason is that the creation of our own methodology allows us to include elements of other third party (MSCI and Sustainalytics) data and own research into the classification of an instrument. 

2.- Can one ZR have multiple ESG preferences if that ZR has multiple portfolios?

The ESG  preferences are collected in the CIP, which applies to all portfolios under one ZR number. If a client wants to have several portfolios with different mandates / strategies under one ZR, then it is most straightforward to apply the least restrictive ESG preference on the CIP (i.e. according to the portfolio which has the lowest ESG preference). An other option is to change the ESG preferences of a dedicated portfolio in the portfolio configuration tab in DiAS (changes are only possible upwards, e.g. from Traditional Investor to Responsible Investor & if a client has more than one portfolio under a given ZR).

3.- Do the CIPs of clients have to be repapered according to their ESG preference?

No, as clients are automatically defaulted as "Traditional" investors. For RML CH/EU & BC CH, the ESG preferences category of the client got added to the "CIP Renewal Summary" (page) that gets sent to the client as part of ordinary renewal process of the CIP.

4.- How do the new CIPs have to be signed?

The process of signing or renewing CIPs stays unchanged. 

5.- Do clients have to sell ESG risks that remain in their portfolio?

No. Clients can keep  ESG risk instruments in their portfolio. The only difference is that we cannot advise them on ESG risks anymore. We can only process their orders on an unsolicited basis.

6.- What happens if an instrument is classified as an ESG risk after it has been advised to a client?

As ESG Risks have universe status "No Bank View", no advice can be provided. RMs can provide factual information to the extent of the client's request, including the reasons that have lead to this re-classification (e.g. MSCI has downgraded their ESG rating for this instrument to CCC).

7.- My advisory clients who choose to express Sustainable ESG preferences in the CIP -  do I need to repaper the mandate?

No,  repapering the advisory mandate is not mandatory. ESG preferences define which financial instruments can be proactively advised, but they do not have a direct impact on the advisory mandate / investment strategy itself (unlike with discretionary mandates, where the ESG preference determines which mandates can be offered to the client).

However, we highly recommend to propose Advice Exclusive for these clients. Our Investment Advisors have received extensive training on Sustainable Finance and are best equipped to navigate the topic with a client.
