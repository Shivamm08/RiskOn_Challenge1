Valid for BC CH, GUE & LUX

1) When the financial instrument is found in PSP/ DIAS (i.e. offered or booked instruments)

  • “Add transaction” with specific ISIN/Valor (consider share class)
  • Perform suitability checks as per DiAS guidance (i.e. handle alerts) and upload documents if required by DiAS
  • Perform the following checks manually (pre-trade)
    • AI Waiver, including Appendix 2 regarding unrestricted or a restricted person status
    • US Person status (see Fund Prospectus/Subscription Information Sheet on PSP)
    • PEP status (see Fund Prospectus/Subscription Information Sheet on PSP)
    • Any further subscription conditions summarized in the Subscription Information Sheet

→ Document the manual checks accordingly in the Client Contact Note (DiAS Session)

Sample of contact note

    • Active Solicitation

I have made a proposal to the client to invest in [specific instrument]. Client decided to subscribe [amount] of instrument [specific instrument]. The client has signed the AI Waiver and he/she is eligible according to Fund Prospect (US and PEP status as well as further subscription conditions) to subscribe for this instrument. 

    • Reverse Solicitation (please consider the "reverse solicitation criteria")

Client specifically requested advice on [specific instrument]. Client decided to subscribe [amount] of instrument [specific instrument]. The client has signed the AI Waiver and he/she is eligible according to Fund Prospect (US and PEP status as well as further subscription conditions) to buy this instrument. 

    • Unsolicited

The client instructed to subscribe [amount] of instrument [specific instrument] without asking for any advice. The client has signed the AI Waiver and he/she is eligible according to Fund Prospect (US and PEP status as well as further subscription conditions) to buy this instrument. 

  • For BC CH/GUE submit the order via DiAS. 
  • For BC LUX please open the respective order entry mask in TAP (direct rooting to be implemented soon).
  • Fund Processing team validates order and sends comply e-mail and SIS to RM. The RM validates the conditions of the SIS and confirms comply e-mail. The Fund Processing Team processes the order. 

2) When the financial instrument is NOT found in PSP / DiAS (i.e. non-offered or non-booked instruments)

  • No solicitation is allowed (neither active nor reverse solicitation) 
  • For BC CH / GUE / LU please open a custom instrument in DiAS. Select Product Type "non-Traditional Fund" and Product Subtype "Hedge Fund" and solicitation type "unsolicited". Please note that you are not allowed to provide advice on non-offered funds.
  • Perform checks as per DiAS guidance and upload the fund prospectus (instead of the KID), when MDDA alert is triggered
  • Perform the following checks manually (pre-trade)
    • BMO Checks - product distribution and tax rules need to be checked manually by the RM/IA (->Link to BMO Front end). Fund Selling restrictions need to be checked against the fund documents (prospectus, offering memorandum etc.)
    • AI Waiver, including Appendix 2 regarding unrestricted or a restricted person status

→ Document the manual checks accordingly in the Client Contact Note (DiAS Session)

Sample of contact note - Unsolicited:

The client instructed to subscribe [amount] of instrument [specific instrument] without asking for any advice. The client has signed the AI Waiver and eligibility according to Fund Prospect (US and PEP status as well as further subscription conditions) has been checked and will be confirmed by comply email. 


  • For BC CH / GUE submit the order via DiAS and add missing instrument information in Portfolio Analysis.
  • For BC LUX go to respective order entry mask in TAP.
  • After order placement in the tool and once Subscription Information Sheet is provided to you, please re-check the following and confirm with comply e-mail: 
    • US Person status (see Fund Prospectus/Subscription Information Sheet)
    • PEP status (see Fund Prospectus/Subscription Information Sheet)
    • Any further subscription conditions summarized in the Subscription Information Sheet
  • The Fund Processing Team processes the order.
