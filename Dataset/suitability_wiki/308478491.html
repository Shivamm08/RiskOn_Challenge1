2. Description of the alert

Each investment strategy has a specific bandwidth for asset class & reference currency exposure that is also contractually agreed in the client's Service Model contract. 

Below bandwidths apply to the following Service Models:

  • Advice Light (former Advice Basic)*
  • Advice Premium*
  • Advice Exclusive (former Advice Advanced)*
  • IA Mandates**


Asset ClassFixed IncomeIncomeBalancedGrowthCapital Gain
Cash & short term0-100%0-100%0-100%0-100%0-75%
Bonds & similar0-100%0-90%0-70%0-60%0%
Equities & similar0%0-35%0-60%0-85%**25-100%
Altern. Investments & others0%0-25%0-25%0-25%0-25%
Ref. CurrencyMin. 60%Min. 55%Min. 50%Mind. 45%Min. 40%

* An individualized investment strategy can be assigned for Advice Exclusive. In this case all bandwidths can be changed individually. For Advice Light and Premium this is only possible for specific use cases under defined circumstances. Further guidance on how to amend the SAA of a portfolio configuration can be found under →How to amend the SAA of a portfolio.

** The 'Equity' bandwidth for the investment strategy Growth is set from 0-100% because for IA Mandates only strategies Income, Balanced and Growth are available. 


3. What triggers this alert?

An SAA alert can be triggered either:

a) in the Advisory session as a result of newly proposed transactions that causes the SAA to deviate beyond the risk asset class-specific bandwidth, or

b) in the Client Book as a result of overnight portfolio risk monitoring either when

    • there is a breach of the official threshold for 5 consecutive days or
    • immediately if the breach is significant: Deviation of > 5% per asset class (-/+) vs. strategy SAA

=> This means that the alert is calibrated to allow for small excesses, as long as the excess is not persistent.


4. How can this alert be resolved?

Resolving the alert depends on the type of the alert: 


A. Advisory Session (session alert)

Solicitation Type

Duty

Documentation (Justification / Contact Note)

Actively solicited

RM/IA must reduce the investment amount in the transaction or in other positions of the same asset class until the alert is not triggered anymore to make the investment advice suitable

or

RM/IA must not proceed with the transaction as long as the alert is shown, because the investment advice is unsuitable


Reverse solicited

RM/IA must advise against the transaction before its execution if it results in a SAA breach

or

RM/IA must advice to reduce the investment amount in the transaction or other positions of the same asset class

If client accepts the risk and wants to proceed:

RM/IA to document the client’s decision and rationale in the alert justification/ contact note as it is described here: →Justification rationale (dropdown values) for "client accepts risk"

Unsolicited

RM/IA must warn the order giver of the SAA breach before executing the transaction

If client accepts the risk and wants to proceed:

RM/IA to document the warning in the alert justification/contact note as it is described here: →Justification rationale (dropdown values) for "client accepts risk"

Important: In the event of any data issues in the system or in dedicated permitted use cases (e.g. workarounds) it is possible to overrule the alert and document it according to the defined process →Hard Block Override Procedure


B. Client Book (overnight portfolio monitoring alert)

DutyResolving the alert & Documentation (Resolution / Contact Note)

RM/IA must inform the client within 5 business days



RM/IA to create an investment proposal to rebalance the portfolio and align the positions with the defined SAA thresholds (either by selling or by buying). If the client requires time to consider the proposal, the RM/IA can snooze the alert for 30 days. RM/IA to select “investment proposal sent” in the alert justification and document 1) the date when the proposal was sent and 2) the channel in the contact note. 

RM/IA to reassess the mandate’s investment strategy if the client’s situation allows it and update the Service Model contract (and CIP, if necessary)

If the client does not want to make changes to the SAA and accepts the risk:

RM/IA to create a resolution note in the client book (“client accepts risk” – also see examples below).

Please note: In case of a single resolution note (use the check Action symbol [ ] in the line of the respective alert), the justification rationale are available to support documentation (→Justification rationale (dropdown values) for "client accepts risk"). However, if you are using “justify all” to close one or several alerts with “client accepts risk”, please note 1) the risks that have been explained, 2) the client’s decision after the explanation and 3) the client's rationale in the contact note.

If the client’s portfolio is enabled for One-way Notification (OWN):

RM/IA to create a resolution note in the client book/portfolio alert overview. Select justification option “client informed” and follow the process in Wealth Navigator to dispatch the portfolio monitoring notification to the client. Further details can be found under →One-way notification for monitoring alerts

If only fund positions are affected:

RM/IA can discuss switching on Fund Splitting with the client. This could solve issues if the portfolio contains JB Strategy Funds, which are classified as Alternative Investments as long as fund splitting is disabled (→Fund Splitting). Please note that enabling fund splitting could affect the allocation in other asset classes due to the additional transparency. Clients should also be made aware that the fund splitting can only be done if JB has the relevant data.

For Advice Exclusive Mandates, the leeways can be changed in Wealth Navigator under Portfolio Configuration. For Advice Light & Premium Mandates specific adjustments for defined strategies and asset classes are permitted. Please note that changing the leeways requires a discussion with your client and must be duly documented as defined under →Portfolio Configuration

If the client could not be reached, RM/IA to select “client not reached” in the alert justification and document 1) the date when the client was approached and 2) the channel in the contact note.


Note: In certain cases the SAA values can become negative based on the current calculation logic. This is the case, when fund-splitting (unbundling) is applied and the fund contains a negative overlay which is then also considered in the asset classes or if the client has an FX/PM forward, Accu/Decu etc. that has a negative P&L (=market value). If the SAA alert gets triggered due to an asset allocation smaller than a minimum of “0%”, the alert can exceptionally be closed without contacting the client. If you close the alert without client contact, capture the respective documentation and mention, that the client has not been contacted and add what caused the negative value (e.g. fund splitting - state which fund visible in PAR, negative development of a Forward etc.). Please bear in mind in case your client wants to be informed about the development of such a product (FX/PM Forward, TARF, Accu/Decu), please still contact the client and document the conversation accordingly.


Validity of Resolution Notes (RN)

A Resolution Note for SAA is valid until 

  • the snooze period ends or
  • the situation “worsens” or “improves” during the lifetime of the RN (→)
  • a new transaction increases the deviation beyond the SAA defined by the contractual investment strategy

If the alert is retriggered during the snooze period, the client must be contacted and informed again about the new situation. Reference to the first contact is not sufficient.


5. Justification examples

 Use Case

Examples

Session alert

The PoA holder called to ask for JB’se quity recommendation in the consumer staples sector. I told him we recommend buying Nestlé, but his portfolio is already overexposed to equities (Balanced strategy with 65% equity allocation). I advised him to sell Lockheed Martin (that he bought earlier, against advice) to bring the asset allocation back in line.

He decided to keep Lockheed and bought Nestlé with 5%, accepting the equity overexposure. We agreed to review the Asset Allocation again in six months.

Overnight portfolio monitoring alert

I informed the client that the portfolio is not in line with the SAA of the mandate strategy (CHF Balanced). I therefore suggested to reduce equities by 10 % and increase the reference currency weight by 10 %.

She told me that she is happy to hold this allocation for the time being , as she believes the US equity market will keep trending higher. She agreed to review the situation again in three months.

Changes in mandate configuration 

Example for Advice Light Mandates

The client wishes to increase the share of NTF xyz. I informed him that based on his strategy the recommended SAA is 25% in alternative investments, and I cannot recommend going above. He argued that he is having other investments outside JB and does not have any AI investments there, hence he would like to increase the threshold to 35%. I confirm to him, that I would amend in such a case the SAA threshold for AI to 35% and reminded him that our monitoring will then also consider the new threshold.
