Every strategy of an advisory mandate has a defined Standard SAA Leeway:

Advice Basic/Light and Premium 

These advisory contracts do not foresee an individualized investment strategy. Nevertheless, for certain frequent client needs adaptation of the SAA is permitted upon clients’ specific request. Examples are High Yield investors, or investors wanting to focus on single asset classes (e.g., core-satellite). The following adjustments are permitted:

  • For clients with strategy Capital Gain:
    • Bonds 0-100% (today default 0%)
    • Equities 0-100% (today default min. 25%)
    • Alternative Investments: 0-100% (today default max. 25%)
  • For clients with strategies Balanced or Growth:
    • Alternative Investments: 0-35% (today default max. 25%)

Ensure that you only extend any bandwidths to the extent requested by the client and refrain from setting all thresholds from 0-100%. Document the request of the client and the decision accordingly in a contact note. As a principle always mention 1. the request from the client and the threshold, 2. the reason for an adjustment, 3. the risk disclosure towards the client including consequences of the change and 4. the final agreement with the client.

Examples:        

The client wishes to increase the share of NTF XYZ, I informed him that based on his strategy the recommended SAA is 25% in alternative investments, and I cannot recommend going above. He argued that he is having other investments outside JB and does not have any AI investments there, hence he would like to increase the threshold to 35%. I confirm to him, that in such a case I can amend, as requested, the SAA threshold for AI to 35% and reminded him that our monitoring will then also consider the new threshold.


The client sought advice on USD High Yield bonds as they seem a good opportunity. I informed him that a Capital Gain strategy does in general not foresee any bond investments and therefore advice on bonds was not agreed contractually, that’s why I cannot recommend any specific instrument. The client asked if we could change this. I asked the client what kind of allocation he would prefer. He wished to include a share of up to 20% in bonds, hence we agreed to amend the SAA for his portfolio as requested and set the threshold for bonds from 0 to 20%.I explained that we also monitor this new threshold if it is exceeded which is fine for him. Following that change I recommended bond XYZ…


My client has bought bonds via e-banking various times in the past into his Premium Mandate with a Capital Gain strategy. Yesterday, he bought again a bond, therefore I reach out to him to inform him about the monitoring alert (SAA breached). He mentioned that he is aware and that he intends to continue buying bonds. I inform again that a Capital Gain strategy does not foresee any bonds and therefore no recommendations for bonds in general except upon specific client request. This means that a client can request the inclusion of bonds in his Capital Gain investment strategy and therefore also receive recommendations if wished and we would monitor the allocation going forward. My client then stated that that is exactly what he wants. I informed him that his current allocation is approx. 20% and ask him what allocation he would prefer. He thinks of a max. 25% allocation. I confirmed to him that I would alter the portfolio settings as requested and confirmed the change also via e-mail later on. 


During the portfolio review of the premium mandate with the client she confirmed her risk appetite and tolerance in the CIP leading to an overall strategy of Capital Gain. However, from an investment perspective she likes to continue with investments into High Yield bonds rather than just Equities and Alternative Investments. I informed her that capital gain does in general not foresee any allocation in bonds except upon specific client request. She then asked for the inclusion of bonds in her investment strategy and to also receive recommendations. I asked her how much of the portfolio should be held in bonds, in order to monitor the SAA going forward. She replied that she would consider an allocation up to 75%. I confirmed to her that I will alter the portfolio settings and confirmed the change also via e-mail later on. 


How is a change done in WN:

1. Enter the Portfolio Configuration Screen from the respective portfolio view:

2. Open the “Portfolio configuration” and move to the tab “SAA Leeway”. The left column shows the default SAA bandwidth as per investment strategy, middle column shows which values are permitted to be changed including the maximum permitted change and in the right column adjustments can be made. need. If you need to amend additional values in other tabs (e.g. bond rating threshold), continue to adjust all values and only then press “save”. 
Note for RMs in RML CH, for EEA-domiciled clients with Advice Premium: When you add a new asset class on request of the client, which is not yet part of the default SAA (e.g. Bonds into investment strategy Capital gain) an additional cross border disclaimer will be printed on the client output. Please be reminded again regarding the reverse solicitation requirements.

3. The distribution screen for the Portfolio configuration notification appears where you can configure, preview, and select the language of the client output.

4. The configuration dialogue allows to amend the language again or hide the portfolio number and previews the document. Press “save & close” to continue with the distribution.

5. Select the contact partner and distribution channel to share the document. The notification must be shared via e-channel but can be simultaneously e-mailed to the RM (e.g. for a client meeting).   

6. In the contact note, the ‘additional notes’ field is already pre-filled with the applied changes. Complete the contact note by adding additional information on describing the client request, see points that need to be documented as well as the example above for guidance. Don’t forget to archive any additional evidence, e.g., full e-mail exchange with the client, accordingly.

Note that the changes are only done if you finish the process by pressing “submit & finish”.

Please note: In case of a change of the investment strategy, any individualized SAA configuration is reset to the default values.
