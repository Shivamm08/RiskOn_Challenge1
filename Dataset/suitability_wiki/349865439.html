Please note that this article is applicable to RM Locations where client ESG preferences are gathered. As of May 2022, those locations are: Switzerland, Germany, Luxembourg, Spain and Ireland.

The ESG preference category selected by the client determines what is the lowest ESG instrument category which can be offered. For discretionary and advisory, the impact is different:

1) For discretionary mandates, the impact is on the level of the mandate itself.

A client with traditional ESG preference can choose any discretionary mandate. A client who is responsible can only choose mandates that are responsible or sustainable. And finally, sustainable clients can only choose a mandate that is sustainable.

If a client wishes to express higher ESG preferences (responsible or sustainable) and has a discretionary mandate that is classified as traditional, the outcome is that the client would need to change their mandate.

For clients that select additional ESG preferences for sustainable investment according to SFDR / EU Taxonomy, selected mandates should also be in line with selected preferences (i.e. min. proportion of DM same or higher than selected preference).

2) For advisory mandates, the ESG preference defines which financial instruments can be proactively advised.

A client with traditional ESG preference can has access to our whole Offered universe (subject to BMO rules and other restrictions). A client who is responsible can be offered instruments that are responsible or sustainable. And finally, sustainable clients can only be offered sustainable instruments.

For details on ESG checks and active vs. reverse solicited scenarios, please visit: ESG Suitability Checks in DiAS - Suitability Wiki - Confluence (juliusbaer.com).

Finally, unsolicited orders can be placed for instruments in all ESG categories. However, it is important to note that ESG Risk instruments cannot be advised, even upon request of the client. Only factual information may be provided.

3) What is our current offering for clients who want to express Sustainable ESG preferences?

Clients who want to have a discretionary mandate can choose the Sustainability Multi-Asset Class mandate, or the Sustainability Equity mandate (subject to their overall risk profile).

Clients who want to have an advisory mandate can choose any advisory mandate and any investment strategy that corresponds to their overall risk profile. However, it is highly recommended to propose Advice Advanced to these clients, due to the additional complexity of ESG and a limited offered universe.
