The most important things to keep in mind when handling business-critical alerts:

  1. When an alert is triggered, consider what it implies and what different options do you have in order to resolve it. The alerts are there to inform you - and your client, that the portfolio is deviating from the contractually agreed strategy, or that the proposed instruments are not suitable / in accordance to our offering.
  2. Use the right justification type. You will often have more than one justification type available and the default option might not be the appropriate one.
  3. Session alerts in the Advisory Session need to be communicated to the client before you proceed with placing the orders.
  4. HOP - Hardblock Override Procedure can only be used in the event of any data issues in the system or in dedicated permitted use cases (e.g. workarounds) → Hard Block Override Procedure  

For documentation examples, please refer to the Overnight Alerts Wiki article and the pages on the individual alerts.
