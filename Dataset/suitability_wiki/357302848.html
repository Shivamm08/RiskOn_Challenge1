Queries relating to regulatory, tax, mandatory documents alerts

All employees are required to adhere to the BMO rules implemented in the internal systems. 

If you have a question related to cross-border financial product distribution, please consult the Product Offering Guidelines in the appendix to the respective Country Guidelines available in the policy database.

If your question regards a Fund Selling Restriction, please check the fund prospectus to the extent reasonable (for example, by searching for key words like “selling restrictions”, “restrictions”, “Thailand” if your client is a Thailand domiciled client).


While addressing an inquiry regarding a BMO related alert, please always include in your  request:

  • Context information:
    • RM location
    • Booking Centre
    • Client Domicile and Classification
    • Solicitation type
  • ISIN


If your question may not be answered via the above procedure, please use the following contacts:

  • Cross-Border Financial Product Distribution: legal-iwms@juliusbaer.com
  • Service Model Compliance:  legal-iwms@juliusbaer.com
  • Fund Selling Restrictions: legal-iwms@juliusbaer.com
  • Tax Rules: taxservices@juliusbaer.com → there is also a Investment Product Taxation repository with valuable information e.g. to UK RND: Intranet - Investment Product Taxation
