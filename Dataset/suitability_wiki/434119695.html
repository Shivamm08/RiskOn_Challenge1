MIDAS:

Real-time physical precious metals pricing portal with indicative commissions. You can use it as a first price indication.

You can open the portal when going in the intranet under APPLICATIONS or type MIDAS in your browser.

Trading has to be done via the Precious Metals Desk in CH, contact see under process description. 


Process description: 

  • Price indications can be found in MIDAS (Real-time physical precious metals pricing portal with indicative commissions)
  • IA/RM/RMA directly contacts Precious Metals Desk in CH; contact details: Contacts (juliusbaer.com)
  • PM Desk CH provides Treasury Rate and Murex ID which is required for TAP order entry
  • if applicable RM has to open a sub-account for the precious metal before entering the order in TAP
  • after placing the order via Precious Metals Desk in CH, RM immediately enters the order in TAP, NO input in DiAS is required:

all required data have to be entered by RM/RMA/IA, including Murex number and in case of allocated bars (segregated custody) the bar number (provided by Precious Metals Desk CH)
