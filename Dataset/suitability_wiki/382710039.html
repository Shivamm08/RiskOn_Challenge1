Itemized Breakdown Report - FIDLEG vs MiFID version

There are two main differences between FIDLEG and MiFID version of the Itemized Breakdown Report, namely:

(1) Structured products issuer fees are disclosed only in MiFID version and not in FIDLEG version and are thus considered for the total investment product related costs only in the MiFID report.

(2) The split between distribution fee for JB and third-party structured products is visible only in the FIDLEG version of the Costs & charges overview (total costs being the same in both versions).


Since January 2025, FIDLEG version of the Itemized Breakdown Report include disclosure of placement fees for non-traditional funds (if applicable).

Quarterly reporting for discretionary mandates

This quarterly reporting serves to provide the client with the fair and balanced assessment of the activities undertaken by investment firm on behalf of the investor over the reporting period. It is automatically delivered (within the first half of the first month after each end of quarter) to discretionary mandate clients (Full MiFID Clients only).

It must be noted that the report's focus is not on the cost disclosure (although it contains certain cost & charges elements) but rather on the general overview of the mandate's developments in the preceding three months. This is the reason why the report is not mentioned in the main table of this section.
