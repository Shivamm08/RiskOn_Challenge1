HOST TA10 Monitoring

https://wiki.juliusbaer.com/spaces/fkh/pages/308479318/HOST+TA10+Monitoring

DiAS encompasses a systematic guided process supporting the Relationship Manager (RM) in delivering suitable and compliant advisory and/or trade execution services to their clients. Among other features, DiAS ensures the routing to and linkage with the appropriate order entry system (e.g. ‘Triple A’, ‘FX Toolbox’, ‘Derivative Toolbox’ or ‘OTMS’) depending on the selected financial instrument. This guided process serves to adhere to the suitability standards at the Bank.

Subject to the exemptions described in the Policy CH-5033-00 Host trading restrictions at Booking Centre Switzerland - TA10 (see section Exceptions below), direct order entry is not allowed in connection with any advisory and/or trade execution services. All advised orders and trade execution activities processed through DiAS have to be placed in the order entry systems connected to DiAS, in order to be compliant with the suitability standards at BJB Switzerland.

The user must follow the guided process of DiAS. The adherence to the mentioned policy will be monitored by the IWMS Risk Management and Regional Front Risk Management.

Purpose

The daily monitoring of the TA10 restrictions at Booking Centers Switzerland is to regulate the usage of order entry systems and trading functionalities not connected to DiAS.

Scope

The daily monitoring process applies to all client relationships that meet the following criteria:

Booking Centre:      CH
Service models:       All service models for which the DiAS workflow is rolled out (excluded are Discretionary mandates, intermediary end-clients (EAM), Global Custody clients, ‘Direct-access-clients’ and employee accounts)
Tool:                          DiAS session activated


Users must use the following order entry systems for any trades that are processed via DiAS:

  • DiAS GUI referring to ‘Triple A’, ‘FX Toolbox’, ‘Derivative Toolbox’ or ‘OTMS’.

Exceptions

RM’s may still trade the following (limited) instruments / asset classes via TA10:

  • Negotiable instruments (→ instrument, where no valor exists or new instruments for which a valor must first be created, e.g. OTC instruments)
  • Non-securitized / non-tradeable instruments with a 9er valor;
  • Alternative Instruments – Limited Partnerships with a 9er valor.

In case DiAS and ‘Triple A’ are technically not available, TA10 will be the fall-back order entry system. IT will inform the users accordingly (see fall-back scenarios and procedures under Support).

Manual pre-trade checks and documentation duties for TA10 usage

In any case of TA10 usage, including the exceptions mentioned above, the application of pre-trade checks and the documentation duties are required. This includes the following duties:

  1. If DiAS is available, perform the pre-trade checks via the DiAS Custom In strument Solutions (in session); 
  2. RM’s proceed along the fall-back scenario “DiAS Advisory Session is not available”
    1.  establish a manual client contact note in Client Relationship Management System (CRM) where the results of the performed checks are documented;
    2.  all communication and documents (e.g. suitability report, Education Material, KID) sent to the client need to be archived in OnDemand or via internal mail to the respective department

Reporting & Escalation

IWMS Risk Mgmt. provides a regular reporting to regional Front Risk Management for Relationship Managers / Relationship Manager Assistants and to IWMS Risk Mgmt. for Investment Advisory. Those risk units are responsible to control the exceptions to the rules respectively the manual performance of the pre-trade checks and documentation duties of the impacted front staff. Based on the control results, repeated violations of the rules of this Policy are escalated by the responsible risk unit to the responsible risk body, where appropriate measures are determined.

Non-compliance with the policy

Employees who are not compliant with the Policy CH-5033-00 Host trading restrictions at Booking Centre Switzerland - TA10 will be subjects to appropriate disciplinary measures.
