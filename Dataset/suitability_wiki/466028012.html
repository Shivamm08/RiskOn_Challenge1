2. What triggers an Investment Universe alert?

The investment Universe check verifies , if the financial instrument has the universe "out of monitored" or "none". In this case a non-critical alert is triggered.

3. What actions are necessary by the RM upon receiving an Investment Universe alert?

You might want to inform the client to switch into another instrument (business opportunity), as the financial instrument is not monitored (anymore) by JB.
