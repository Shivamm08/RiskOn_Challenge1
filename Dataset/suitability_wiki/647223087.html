Basically all instruments as defined by MiFID II (Directive 2014/65/EU - Annex I - Section C) and FinSA (art. 3 lit. a) are considered financial instruments. 


-> The following instruments are out of scope and not considered a financial instrument:

  • Fiduciaries 1)
  • Payments
  • Precious Metals (Physicals)
  • Lombard Loans
  • FX Spot (T+0, T+1, T+2) 2)

1) incl. Time and Call Deposits

2) Unless the FX Spot it is in connection with an advisory service or a transaction. Then it is considered an ancillary service under MiFID II.
