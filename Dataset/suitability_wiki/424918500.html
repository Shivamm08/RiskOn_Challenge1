STEP 1: BASIC ASSESSMENT 

Please ensure that there is no conflicting information on the form (e.g., a client stating ample experience in a product category but no corresponding knowledge) or conflicting information that you already have from the client (e.g., a client previously stating that she/he has traded many Hedge Funds but states to have no knowledge). Additionally, indicate the main source(s) of knowledge and experience on the 2nd page of the K&E-Form by crossing 1 out of the 4 boxes. If you are unsure about the client’s knowledge, you find sample questions for more complex asset classes that you can ask to increase the level of confidence that the client is familiar with the risks and characteristics of a product category. 

STEP 2: DOCUMENTATION OF THE ASSESSMENT

Once the K&E form is complete and the client has signed the form, you must document the assessment in a contact note. This contact note reference must also be captured on the form before sending it to CDS. Note: Capture the u-Number of the RM on the form who has performed the assessment even if the contact note is captured by the RMA. This contact note reference replaces the RM signature going forward. Forms without filled in contact note reference will be returned by CDS (for avoidance of doubt, the contact note itself must not be handed in together with the form). 

For new account openings: Since the account is not yet opened, you can not yet capture the contact note. The following process is to be applied: Note the date of the contact where the RM has obtained and assessed the K&E on the form with the respective u-number of the RM. Once the account is opened, please fill in the contact note with the required content of the assessment. Bear in mind that this is an important documentation requirement and also subject to the control framework.



Example of sufficient documentation in contact note (choose contact type “CIP and KE assessment/renewal”): I have discussed and assessed the K&E declared by the client xy and confirm the stated K&E levels per category.


Additional note to the documentation on the CIP. On the CIP the RM signature is also replaced by a contact note reference. Purpose of this contact note is to confirm that the CIP has been properly (i.e. correctly and completely) filled in and reviewed by the RM. As CIP and K&E are mainly discussed in the same meeting, the confirmation can also be inserted in the same contact note. To verify the CIP please consult the respective checklist: here. 

Example of contact note for the CIP: 

I have discussed the Client Investor Profile with Mrs. X and confirm the consistency, correctness and completeness and validity of the stated values.
