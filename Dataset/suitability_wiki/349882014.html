In short:

The JB Human Capital Score considers issues on labour management like health & safety, labour practices, human capital development, diversity & inclusion and community relations. The score averages several relevant data points from Ssutainalytics and classifies issuers in comparison to all issuers in our booked universe.

Short explanation of the calculation:

The JB Human Capital scores is based on the Sustainalytics risk score relevant to human capital performance. The scale is inversed (e.g. to ensure a score of 10 is the most positive score), corrections for principal adverse impact indicators are made, and accordingly the score is mapped to the JB scale using a logical scale, where a score of 5 results in an average score (JB score of 0).
