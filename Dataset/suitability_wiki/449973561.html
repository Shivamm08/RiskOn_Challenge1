PROCESS FOR JB "OWN" PRIVATE MARKETS OFFERINGS (Drawdown Funds)

Overview - Process Flow

Detailed Process Flow

1.        The RM discusses with the client a potential investment.

Pre-Trade Checks

2.a      The RM downloads the respective fund documents from the Private Markets intranet page. 

The RM checks the suitability of the instrument in the session by entering the internal valor of the fund as disclosed on the Private Markets intranet page. Do not use the dummy valor 9992888.

Depending on the RM Location and client domicile different mandatory disclosure documents need to be provided to the client. 

    • Advisory Session in Wealth Navigator will trigger a "Mandatory Disclosure Document Alert - (MDDA)" in case a KID (EEA PRIIPS KID, FinSA KID resp. UK PRIIPS KID) is required. You need to upload the KID manually. The KIDs can be found on the Private Markets intranet page. Please ensure that the KID is selected in the client package in order to be sent out to the client. 
    • The "RM Checklist" lists all the other documents, which need to be sent to the client (if any). Please note that this documents need to be sent to the client either separately or the documents can also be uploaded in the session and added to the client package. Please note that session will not trigger an alert and they are not automatically included in the client package. 

Important: the order cannot be entered in the Advisory Session. Please do not process via OTMS either, as these orders will be ignored. Instead close the session (don't forget to enter a Client Contact Note) and copy the Session-ID.


Subscription Process

2.b       RM to complete the "RM Checklist" (don't forget to add the Advisory Session-ID in the checklist) and submit it electronically via the submit form button in the PDF.

3.         Private Markets Desks collects the subscriptions and reviews the checklist for completeness incl. if Advisory Session has been performed. No Session → no trade. 

4.         Private Markets Desk enters subscriptions into the database to reserve the requested capacity on a first come first serve basis. The RM will get an e-mail confirmation e-mail (Ticket Status, Fund, Commitment, Account/ZR, Reference). The confirmation e-mail includes also the next steps.

5.a/b    RM sends all "Subscription Documents" to the clients and once signed forwards the documents to Private Markets Desk. 

→ see also Private Markets Subscription Process

Order Entry

7.          BC CH/GG: At the closing-date Private Markets Desk forwards the subscriptions to Private Market Execution team, who will enter the trades in HOST. No further action from RM required.

  Other BC's: At the closing-date Private Market Desk provides the RM with booking instructions via e-mail. RM enters the order in the system (Host or T24)


PROCESS FOR 3rd PARTY FUNDS (Evergreen Funds - non offered)

Onboarding Process for new instrument via LP Desk


  • Each new instrument needs to be checked individually, i.e. needs to be approved by LB Desk before any subscription.
  • For that, the RM forwards the fund documents (Offering/Private Placement Memorandum (OM)/(PPM), Limited Partnership Agreement (LPA) and (blank) subscription documents) to the LP Desk. 
  • After review, LP Desk sends the approval incl. side letter to the front.

Wealth Navigator Session

  • Before sending the side letter to the client, the RM performs the suitability check in Wealth Navigator. 
  • Use the "custom instrument" function in WN session with "Product type" → "Non-Traditional Fund" and "Product subtype" → "Private Equity" to ensure relevant checks are performed.


  • No BMO Checks are performed on instrument level by WN Session - product selling restrictions need to be checked manually by the RM/IA by consulting the prospectus.
  • Mandatory Disclosure Documents need to be uploaded manually. Remember PRIIPS-KID is required for a retail investor. For professional client, you can upload the prospectus, if no PRIIPS-KID is available. 
  • Save and document the Advisory Session in a Contact Note. 

Order Placement

  • The RM forwards the approval and side letter to the client.
  • Client sends back subscription documents.
  • LP Desk opens the internal instrument.
  • The RM places the order in HOST as follows: 
    1. Z: "Z"eichnung (subscribe)
    2. 10000000: Commitment/Unfunded amount
    3. 9991358: internal Valor (provided by LP Desk)
    4. 3214567: ZR (account number) (subscribe)

  • required fields are being filled out by the system automatically
  • any missing information will be indicated in red and must be manually entered
  • confirm entry with "ENTER"
  • activation of order with "F10"


Order entry system may differ for other locations (other than Zurich & Guernsey). Please refer to the local contact: 

Asia: PrivateEquityOpsAsia@juliusbaer.com

Europe: bfx_lux@juliusbaer.com

SECONDARY TRANSACTIONS

Private Markets offers a platform for JB clients for secondary transactions. Info on selected funds to be purchased is published via e-mail. Please be informed that a WN session needs to be performed, before you offer a fund to a client. 

Private Market Funds in the secondary market process are allocated to the "out of monitored" universe, as they are not offered anymore (closed end fund). Therefore a solicitation alert will be triggered telling that the fund is "out of monitored" and hence cannot actively solicited to the client. Please justify the alert with "data exception" and upload the "Private Markets - Secondary Action" Email containing this specific fund as evidence for proper documentation.   

FONDO DE CAPITAL A RIESGO (FCR)

FCR's are Private Equity Funds in which Retail clients domiciled in Spain are able to invest in, under the following conditions:

  1. the fund is registered with the Spanish regulator as "is Retail Eligible"
  2. the legal entity is registered with the Spanish regulator
  3. the retail client has completed and returned the "Retail Investor Declaration"

Currently only RML Spain and RML Luxembourg have the required registration.
