INTRODUCTION

With the Mandate Solution Designer you benefit from

  • Generating a suitable offering proposal to a prospect / client, using the pre-validated list of available mandates (discretionary and advisory)
  • Compare discretionary mandates concerning their asset allocation, historic performance, return forecasts and fees to find the best matching new offering
  • Receiving the marketing & supporting documentation (price lists, contracts) linked to your proposal

For more detailed information and education material (detailed user manual, video, example of a client output,...), please check here: IWMS Tools and Applications


SUITABILITY IN MSD - CLIENT PROFILE, INTEGRATED VALIDATIONS, WARNING MESSAGES & GUIDANCE

From a suitability perspective below mentioned checks & validations have been built into MSD to support you in generating a suitable proposal. 

A) Prospect or existing client?

  1. Prospects: Define the client profile
    If you access MSD and start with a prospect case (aka "blind profile"), you're asked to define the relevant parameter concerning the client's profile, i.e.
    1. Language (relevant for the client output)
    2. Client investment profile (Fixed Income, Income, Balanced, Growth, Capital Gain)
    3. QI block (Blocked, Not blocked)
    4. Client domicile (select the applicable country from the drop down)
    5. Booking center (select the applicable BC from the drop down)
    6. PM location (note, this is directly linked to the above selections, i.e. client domicile and booking center)
    7. Client segment (EAM, EFA End-Client, Private Banking)
    8. Client type (Standard, Pension, Life Insurance)
    9. ESG preference category (Traditional, Responsible, Sustainable)
    10. Currency (CHF, EUR, GBP, USD; note, this is the currency later shown on the selected mandate's contract)
    11. Investment amount

  2. Existing clients: CRM data as a basis
    If you access MSD and start with an existing client's number, the subsequently shown offering shelf (i.e. discretionary and advisory mandates) is selected based on the CRM data available for the client. This particularly incl. the clients investment strategy (based on the CIP) as well as the ESG preference defined. For reference, see the parameter for "prospects" mentioned above. 


B) Generate a suitable proposal and document the client contact

When entering the MSD workflow after selecting the client / prospect, you will find a pre-filtered, suitable list of offerings in correspondence to the parameters mentioned above. 

Please be aware that your amendments might trigger the following warning messages & guidance built into MSD workflows:


STEP 1 - LIST OF AVAILABLE MANDATES

You can change the default list of available mandates by using the "filter options" on the right side of the screen.
Warning messages which might be triggered:

  • Higher investment strategy: If you're selecting a new offering with an investment strategy deviating from the CIP's thresholds.
  • Sustainable Investment: If you change the pre-defined setting and search for mandates which do not match the clients ESG preferences.

    IMPORTANT: For the warnings on "Overall Investment Strategy" and "Sustainable Investment", and in case these settings are subject to the actual client interaction and part of the proposal, you must warn the client about the impact concerning these deviations to the client's CIP / investment strategy and/or ESG preferences. On top, you'll be asked to document the client contact in CRM, accordingly. However, as long as you're simply exploring the options within MSD, no further action required at this point.


STEP 2 - COMPARE HISTORIC PERFORMANCES

Once you've defined your selection, use the "Compare" functionality to explore and compare the selected mandates in more detail. Please note the following warning:

  • Minimal timeline = 12 months: When comparing the historic performance of mandates, it is not allowed to generate a client output with a timeline shorter than 12 months of the mandates' performance track record. The application allows to explore performances of shorter timelines (e.g. for your own meeting preparation or interest), but client outputs are blocked according the limitations.  


STEP 3 - ORDER DOCUMENTS

The "Proceed to order" button takes you to the "Order documents" screen and gives you an overview on the selected mandates, incl. the supporting documentation available. In reference to above, if

  • OIS the investment strategy applied deviates from the clients CIP, and/or
  • (the sustainable investment characteristics of the offering does not match the recorded ESG preferences, and/or)
  • the chosen timeline for comparing an existing advisory with new mandates is shorter than 12 months, you'll receive a warning on screen.

D) Mandate opening & implementation

After a successful client discussion about the new mandate (or the switch of an existing), it takes a few more steps before its implemented:

STEP 1 - OPEN NEW DEPOSIT ACCOUNT IN CRM

Outside of MSD, but relevant for the implementation of the new mandate, you need to open a new deposit account. Make sure the investment currency matches the signed contract and remember the selected "sequence number" (i.e. ZR + portfolio number --> "xxxx.xxxx / 02xx").

STEP 2 - ARCHIVE THE CONTRACT 
After you've submitted the signed client contract to CDS for scanning, it's mapped to the newly opened deposit account.

STEP 3 - ADD PORTFOLIO & IMPLEMENT MANDATE

Back in MSD, you'll see that the updated status of your proposal changed. 

  • If you're adding a new portfolio, clicking on "Add portfolio" allows you to link the new mandate to the deposit account you've opened previously in CRM. 
  • If you're switching an existing mandate to a new one, clicking on the "Implement" button takes you to the process to complete the process.

For both use cases, you'll see a warning about the need for a Client Contact Note (CCN).
