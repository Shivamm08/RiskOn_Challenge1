CIP - what is it for?

https://wiki.juliusbaer.com/spaces/fkh/pages/431811957/CIP+-+what+is+it+for

It is important to understand what is unique about the client’s personal financial situation. The aim is to get a full picture of the client’s investment needs as well as the client’s service expectations, and then help to create an individual target investment strategy and action plan to meet the client’s future wealth goals. The CIP contains any investment related information in order to determine the clients’ investment objectives, financial risk ability and risk tolerance. As a result, the corresponding investment strategy is determined. The Knowledge & Experience about Julius Baer's asset classes is also a crucial part of the profile and gathered via separate form.

Important information: The following guidance is applicable for the new CIP forms within the following booking centers: BC LUX, MC, CH, GG with all Advisory Locations (excl. UK) as of November 2025. For RML UK and other BC's, please consult this RM Guidance.

CIP – Scope and applicability



When s a CIP required?

All service models which comprise advisory, portfolio management and -monitoring services by BJB require a CIP.

Examples where a CIP is required: JB Advice Premium / JB Advice Exclusive / JB Advice Light / Discretionary Mandates

Examples where a CIP is not required: Non-Trading-Account (NTA) / JB Trade Basic (only K&E required) / End-clients of External Asset Managers (EAM)

How many CIP do I need for a business relation (ZR)?

One profile per ZR with identical account holder constellation, except:

if the CIP is used for setups like life insurance companies, pension solution providers and for vested benefit accounts (‘Freizügigkeitskonti’) or a certain type of fiduciary companies like Julius Baer Fiduciaria S.r.L., please consult the separate guidance 

Who has to sign the CIP form in case of joint/collective accounts?

All account holders

Who has to sign the CIP form in case of a corporate account?

The same signatory rules as indicated in the signature forms of the account opening package apply (e.g. if the signature rules imply a collective signature by three, then three signatures with that authorization are required

What different types of CIP forms exist?

Client investment profile for private individuals (CIPPI* - depending on RML: ESG or non-ESG):

  • Individual account: Fill out one CIP
  • Joint account: Fill out one CIP. One of the account holders must be designated as the representative. The CIP will be based on the representative individual.

Client investment profile for legal entities (CIPLE* - depending on RML: ESG or non-ESG):

  • Corporate account: Fill out one CIP

Client investment profile for life insurance companies, pension solution providers and for vested benefit accounts (‘Freizügigkeitskonti’) or a certain type of fiduciary companies like Julius Baer Fiduciaria S.r.L. (CIPLIC* - depending on RML: ESG or non-ESG):

  • Always fill out one CIP per account holder – beneficial owner constellation

Investment objectives

CIPPICIPLECIPLIC

Investment objectives determine the client’s financial intentions. There are three primary investment objectives.

  • Capital preservation
  • Balanced growth
  • Dynamic growth

As capital preservation and dynamic growth do not have a cash flow component per se, there is a supplementary investment objective cash flow generation which can be combined with 'Capital preservation’ or ‘Dynamic growth’

Cash flow generation in this context refers to income from the invested assets, such as interest payments or dividends.

Risk profile

CIPPICIPLECIPLIC

The risk profile consists of two individual sections, wherein ALL questions must be answered (questions are slightly different between CIPPI, CIPLE & CIPLIC):

Financial risk ability

  • Definition: The client's ability to bear the financial risks in economic terms ()
  • Result: Once all questions have been answered, add the scoring of each question and determine the client’s financial risk ability

Risk tolerance

  • Definition: Assessment of the client's emotions & reaction to the risk
  • Result: Once all questions have been answered, add the scoring of each question and determine the client’s risk tolerance 

Risk Profile

  • Result: The combination of both - the financial risk ability and the risk tolerance - results in the client’s risk profile, whereas the lower of the two – financial risk ability and risk tolerance – has to be chosen

Match investment objectives and risk profile

CIPPICIPLECIPLIC

This section aims to align the risk profile with the investment objectives stated at the beginning of the questionnaire. The risk profile must match the investment objectives.

If the matching ends up in a field with an arrow, a mismatch is given. This means, there is a need to correct in the direction of the arrow (to the left, to the right or up) as follows:

  1. Correct in the direction of the arrow
  2. Tick the according box of the corrected arrow

  3. If a reassessment was done, the newly defined investment objective and risk profile must be stated at the bottom of this section.

Example: 

  1. If the risk profile is considerable and the investment objective is capital preservation, this is an invalid matching. Possible solutions:
    • Reducing the risk to ‘moderate’, or
    • Adapting the investment objectives to ‘Balanced growth'

The revision is exclusively done on this page - NO correction of previous pages allowed! In the summary section at the end of the CIP, the corrected matching must be stated.

Investment strategy

CIPPICIPLECIPLIC

The investment strategy results from the combination of investment objectives and risk profile and it is what finally applies to Julius Baer’s product recommendations.

There is no room to select any other investment strategy than the one which is based on the valid combination of the risk profile and the investment objective (see previous slides). If the client wishes to have another investment strategy than resulted in the CIP, a deviating strategy can be chosen within the dedicated advisory- and/or discretionary mandate in adherence to your local suitability policy (e.g. for RML CH: CH-2070-00 Suitability and Appropriateness v.5 (navexone.eu))

For each investment strategy, a dedicated asset allocation is defined and depending on the selected service level, a periodic monitoring is applied.

Example 1: If the risk profile is considerable and the investment objective is balanced growth, this leads to a balanced strategy.

Example 2: If the risk profile is considerable and the investment objective is dynamic growth, this leads to a growth strategy.

Specialty: Pension solution providers as for vested benefit accounts (‘Freizügigkeitskonti’) have a restricted availability in the selection of the investment strategy. Therefore, the investment strategy ‘Growth’ and ‘Capital gain’ cannot be selected.

Practical guidance for the client investment profilehere

In November 2025 the CIPs (CIPPI, CIPLE & CIPLIC) have been extended with a 2-paged practical guidance providing support and additional information on how to calculate specific questions of the CIP section "Financial Risk Ability". Besides aligning with the requirements of the Swiss Supervisory Authority (FINMA), the guidance will support a uniform interpretation of the CIP. It has only informational character and has been added at the end of the CIP (behind the client’s signature). RMs are required to provide this guidance to the client. As of the November 2025 Release, all new CIP relevant clients will receive the new CIP-form (i.e. CIP + 2-paged extension).


Best practice

CIPPICIPLECIPLIC

For a valid investment profile, the following criteria must be met

  • At least one business relation (ZR) must be available on the profile
  • All questions in the profile must be answered
  • Only one answer per question must be chosen
  • Sum of the scoring points must be correct
  • The summary section must reflect the results of the previous pages. No deviation must occur!
  • All account holders must sign the profile
  • The correct U-number with date and time of the contact note must be stated

Best scanning results are achieved with:

  • For best results, a dark (black or blue) and thick pen (no pencil or felt tip pen) is recommended. Correct examples
  • Print double-sided (duplex) (if duplex printing is not available then single-sided printing may be used)
  • Print on A4 portrait format
  • Print on a quality laser printer
  • In Print menu select actual size in size options (scaling)
  • Print on white quality paper (min. 80g/m2)
