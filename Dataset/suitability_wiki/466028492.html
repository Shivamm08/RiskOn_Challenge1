2. Description of the alert

Clients booked in Germany or Luxembourg can select additional ESG preferences. These additional preferences relate to minimum proportions of sustainable investment according to a) Sustainable Finance Disclosure Regulation (SFDR) or b) the EU Taxonomy (see here for details: →Client ESG Preferences)

If a client selects such additional ESG preferences the client's advisory portfolio alignment to Sustainable Investments according to SFDR / EU Taxonomy will be compared against the corresponding threshold defined by the client's ESG preference. As this alert combines critical and non-critical elements, the different scenarios are explicitly outlined in the following.


3. What triggers this alert?

The alert is triggered when the portfolio proportion of sustainable investments according to SFDR / EU taxonomy worsens below the specified minimum proportion threshold. This may happen either:

a) in the Advisory Session as a result of a newly proposed transaction, or

b) in the Client Book (non-critical only) as a result of overnight portfolio risk monitoring, e.g. due to a change of the financial instrument’s ESG classification or price movements


4. How can this alert be resolved?

You can resolve the alert with one of the following actions:

A. Advisory Session (session alert)

Solicitation Type

Duty

Documentation (Justification / Contact Note)

Actively solicited

Exception:

Under the condition that there is no alternative/equivalent product available within the client’s ESG preferences, RM/IA can provide active advice on a single financial instrument against the client’s ESG preferences provided that the client’s approval to receive investment advice outside of his ESG preferences is received prior to providing such advice

otherwise

RM/IA must not proceed with the transaction as long as the alert is shown, because the investment advice is unsuitable

Exception:

If client/ order giver agrees to purchase the instrument outside his/her ESG preference:

RM/IA to select "client accepts risk" in the alert justification and document the following in the contact note:

1) No alternative/equivalent product is available within the client’s ESG preferences

2) The client/order giver’s approval for receiving such investment advice has been obtained prior to providing the advice (please note that the client’s explicit approval is required for each trade)

3) Client/order giver’s clear acknowledgement that this investment advice will not be in line with the defined ESG preferences but he/she agrees to receive such investment advice (i.e.  to a one-time adaption of the ESG preferences for this trade only, no change of CIP)


Reverse solicited

RM/IA to inform the order giver about the threshold breach for the defined minimum proportion of Sustainable financial instruments in the client’s portfolio. RM/IA may advice the order giver outside the client’s ESG preference universe but must inform about imminent risks and obtain approval before proceeding with the execution.

If client/order giver agrees to purchase the instrument outside his/her defined minimum proportion of Sustainable financial instruments in the client’s portfolio -> RM/IA to select "client accepts risk" and document the Client/order giver’s clear acknowledgement that this investment advice will not be in line with the defined minimum proportion of Sustainable financial instruments in the client’s portfolio.

Unsolicited

RM/IA must inform the order giver of the deviation of the threshold before executing the transaction.

If the order giver accepts the deviation and wants to proceed -> RM/IA to document the warning in the alert justification / contact note.

Important: In the event of any data issues in the system or in dedicated permitted use cases (e.g. workarounds) it is possible to overrule the alert and document it according to the defined process →Hard Block Override Procedure


B. Client Book (overnight portfolio monitoring alert)

ESG SFDR & EU Taxonomy minimum proportion mismatches are displayed as business opportunity (non-critical) and therefore not mandatory to be followed up. However, it is an opportunity to create an investment proposal in Wealth Navigator with an instrument which matches the clients preferences and to propose a switch.

5. Justification examples

 Use case

Example

Session Alert

Example 1 (actively solicited)

I have contacted the client due to this alert (e.g. sell alert or bond rating threshold). As there is no equivalent product for the switch on the shelf for the clients ESG preferences, I asked the client for his approval to receive investment advice outside of his ESG preferences. The client agreed and acknowledged that this investment advice will not be in line with the defined ESG preferences but he/she agrees to receive such Investment Advice. I consequently suggested a switch from financial instrument A to B.


Example 2 (reverse solicited)

The client called me and asked for advice on instrument A (specific instrument). According to our systems, instrument A is 0% EU Taxonomy aligned, and the client has selected minimum Proportion Preference "Moderate". I informed the client about the ESG preference mismatch. The client acknowledged the mismatch but nevertheless wanted to proceed with the transaction.
