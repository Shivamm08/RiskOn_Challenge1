For Advice Premium and ISM accounts, you can now order an investment proposal via WeNa. The proposal will be set up by the newly established Portfolio Reviews Team. 
The Request Proposal functionality will provide the RM with order mask that offers the possibility to outline certain investment characteristics of the client in advance and then 
automatically deliver them to the Portfolio Review Team.
The Portfolio Review Team consists of highly qualified specialists, who then prepare a custom fit proposal based on the client's characteristics and send it back to the RM.

Important to note
Please note that this report can only be requested once a year per portfolio. The next request date will appear next to 'Request Proposal'.

To request such a proposal, please open the portfolio. Then click on 'three dots' and on 'Request Proposal'. You can find further details in the screenshots below.
