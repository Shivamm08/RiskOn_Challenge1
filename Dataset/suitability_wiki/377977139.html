Distribution Rules

https://wiki.juliusbaer.com/spaces/fkh/pages/377977139/Distribution+Rules
  • To whom are mandatory documents distributed?

Mandatory documents are sent to the order giver (AH or PoA) by default, as the order giver needs the information pre-trade in order to make an informed investment decision.

If order giver is not the AH: the information given to the order giver can be attributed to the AH. Therefore, it is not necessary to send mandatory documents to the Account Holder as well, when already provided to the order giver. 

For collective accounts or collective signatory rights an additional PRIIPS-KID should be sent to the collective signatory holder.


  • Timing: When do mandatory documents need to be provided?

Mandatory documents need to be provided before the trade is placed (pre-trade).
