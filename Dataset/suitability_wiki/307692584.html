Contractual Obligation - what is the service promise ?

Clients with the following service models must be provided at least once a year with a portfolio analysis report (PAR) or an equivalent investment proposal. In addition, our advisory clients with below service models can also expect that their portfolio is regularly reviewed with them and any deviations as well as new investment opportunities are discussed.

Service Models in scope:

  • Advice Premium
  • Advice Exclusive (Advanced) – direct / indirect
  • Investment Advisory – direct / indirect

How can I fulfill this yearly obligation ?

1. Proposal (PIR/Proposal Summary) in Wealth Navigator

You can create an investment proposal or a proposal summary in Wealth Navigator for your client meeting and share this investment proposal with your client. Please note, when you share the investment proposal, use the “e-banking” or “e-mail to client” sharing option in Wealth Navigator as it simplifies the archiving duties on your end.

If you download the proposal and send it to your own mailbox, you must ensure, that you record the submission to the client, capture a contact note and archive all evidence.

2. Session (PIR) in Wealth Navigator

You can also create a Personal Investment Recommendation (PIR) in an Advisory Session in Wealth Navigator. If this Session PIR contains advised (solicited) transactions, and the PIR is also shared with the account holder via e-banking or e-mail, the yearly duty is also considered to be fulfilled, as this implies you had an advisory discussion, and the PIR contains the actual portfolio as well and any deviations (monitoring results on the contractually agreed monitoring parameters). 

3. Share a Portfolio Analysis Report (PAR): 

You can also discuss the portfolio on the basis of a “portfolio analysis report” (PAR). There are two options to order such a report:

a) If you do not yet have an alert that reminds you of your yearly duty, you can generate a PAR in the Portfolio view in Wealth Navigator, by clicking on “more” à Portfolio Analysis Report.

You can preview the report if required.


The Portfolio Analysis Report (PAR) report consists of the following elements:

b) If you already have an alert, reminding you of the yearly duty, go to the notification ‘Yearly Analysis Report’ and hover with the mouse until the button ‘Request Report’ appears. Follow the process to generate the report and share it with the client.


Details on how to handle the PAR alert can be found here: https://wiki.juliusbaer.com/spaces/fkh/pages/341477736/Portfolio+Analysis+Report+PAR

Please note, when you share the PAR (ad-hoc or out of an alert), use preferably “e- banking” or “e-mail to client” sharing option in Wealth Navigator as it simplifies the archiving duties on your end.
As of June 2025, distribution of the PAR via an e-channel  (either "e-banking" or "e-mail") is enforced by the system where available for the Partner that has been selected for distribution. If no e-channel is in place the "send to myself" option is still available for distribution of the PAR (ad-hoc or out of an alert).

If you download the proposal and send it to your own mailbox, you must ensure, that you record the submission to the client, capture a contact note and archive all evidence.

If the yearly obligation is duly fulfilled during the year with above option 1, 2  or 3a, the yearly PAR-alert is not generated as the duty is already fulfilled.

How am I notified by Wealth Navigator that the delivery of the report is due ?

You will receive a Portfolio Analysis Report reminder notification 300 days after you last sent it to your client. The notification will be displayed on a single portfolio level as well as on the portfolio overview page. The alert will become critical 30 days before becoming overdue. It is recommended to take this alert into consideration whenever you plan a client meeting.

Which report should be delivered for portfolios with hedges ?

In case the portfolio is hedged the Consolidated Product Risk, Overexposure / Underexposure and Concentration Risk are not taking the change in risk reduction into account in the PAR. Hence a Personal Investment Recommendation (PIR) offers more opportunities to capture additional explanation on recommended instrument level compared to the PAR.

For Advice Premium and ISM the Relationship Manager from all RML (excl. Asia) can also order an investment proposal directly via Wealth Navigator (via the respective entry mask) with the possibility to outline certain investment characteristics of the client in advance. The Portfolio Review Team or Mandate Advisor will then elaborate a proposal.

What are the consequences for me and the Bank if I do not adhere to these obligations?

The consequences of not providing Advice Premium & Exclusive (former Advanced) clients with the mandatory Portfolio Analysis Report at least once per year are threefold:

1) Violation of contractually agreed services
2) Breach of JBG-2049-00 Advisory Service Model Policy for not contacting the client and a breach of Policy JBG-2048-00 Acceptance, Documentation & record-keeping of Client Orders  / 9.2 Documentation requirements for advisory services / product recommendations by the RM

3) Negative impact on the Bank’s reputation in a possible civil liability or an audit/regulatory finding.

It will be regularly reviewed, if the client receives the required document, however, please be reminded, that this requires that the discussion with the client but also the delivery of the document is sufficiently documented (contact note and archiving of evidence). Sharing the document directly via Wealth Navigator (e-banking or e-mail) will ensure that the traceability is given.
