2. Description of the alert

Advisory clients interested in receiving advice on the new Digital Assets offering must sign a dedicated Digital Assets Special Terms & Conditions waiver (STC-DIGITAL) and will receive the Digital Assets Risk Disclosure form (DISC-DIGITAL) and the Digital Assets education document (→Digital Assets - client education) from their RM before any advice may be provided.

This Digital Asset risk waiver alert checks if the account (ZR/BR) has a valid STC-DIGITAL waiver in place that is required depending on the type of Digital Asset. For Indirect Digital Assets the STC-DIGITAL is required for “Actively solicited” and “Reverse solicited” transactions. For Direct Digital Assets the STC-DIGITAL is required for “Actively solicited”, “Reverse solicited” and “Unsolicited” transactions.


3. What triggers this alert?

This alert is triggered in the Advisory Session if the account does not have a valid STC-DIGITAL waiver in CRM which is required for:

  • Direct Digital Assets independent of the solicitation type (actively, reverse and unsolicited)
  • Indirect Digital Assets for active and reverse solicitation
