Worsening/Improvement Concept

https://wiki.juliusbaer.com/spaces/fkh/pages/547101652/Worsening+Improvement+Concept

Alerts can be closed either until the snooze period ends, or until the situation "worsens" or "improves" during the resolution note lifetime. This means that a closed alert is triggered again and before the end of the snooze period, if the "worsening" threshold value is exceeded!

The below table explains the “worsening” and “improvement” thresholds applied for each alert type:

AlertWorsening threshold (from client specific threshold)Improvement threshold (from mandate threshold)
Consolidated Product Risk (CPR)Increase of CPR + 1.0 or moreCPR falls 1.0 below the mandate strategy threshold
Strategic Asset Allocation (SAA)Increase of 10% (absolute) or moreSAA falls 10% (absolute) below the threshold of the allocation
Single Instrument Concentration Increase of +5% (absolute) or moreConcentration falls 5% (absolute) below the max. threshold
Issuer ConcentrationIncrease of +5% (absolute) or moreConcentration falls 5% (absolute) below the max. threshold
Overall investment strategy (OIS)Increase of +0.25 or moren/a

How does this work? (Illustrative example): 



(1) Here the maximum threshold of the for Equities is 60% . As the share of Equities is above 60% an alert is triggered. The RM contacts the client and agrees that the client accepts the risk and would like to be notified only in 6 month again. The RM creates a resolution note (RN). With this the client specific threshold is set (in the example above at 65% at the time of creating the RN).

(2) If the values now move due to regular market movements, no new alert is triggered. 

(3) Only if the value significantly worsens a new alert is triggered - even if there is a snoozing period. Here the worsening threshold is applied → Client specific threshold + 10% (absolute), in the example at 75%. 

(4) The new resolution note (RN) remains valid for the new snoozing period. 

(5) Due to additional market movements, the value now significantly "improves" by falling 10% below the maximum threshold of the strategy → In the example below 50% (60%-10%). Here the RN will be reset. 

(6) A new alert will be triggered, when a new breach occurs. 

→ The same logic applies also to the other checks noted above. 

Summary: 

There are three ways how the resolution note (RN) may be invalidated / reset before the snooze period ends and may trigger a new alert:

1. Worsening: If the values significantly worsens and breaches the worsening threshold. 

2. Improvement: If the exposure decreases and drops below the improvement threshold.

3. Any new buy order in the instrument via a Wealth Navigator Session would also invalidate the resolution note if it increases the deviation from justified level (not worsening threshold). Sell orders invalidate the RN if the exposure decrease below the “improvement” threshold.

What can I do to resolve & avoid these alerts?

  • Rebalance the portfolio to the foreseen bandwidths. 
  • Create a Resolution Note (in the client book) if your client does not want to rebalance the portfolio.
  • Reassess the investment strategy of the mandate if the client’s situation allows it and update the Service Model contract (and CIP, if necessary).
  • On request of the client consider an adaption of the SAA via portfolio configuration - please check the guidance first (Link)
