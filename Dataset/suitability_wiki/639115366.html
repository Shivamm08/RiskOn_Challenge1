Determine Solicitation Type

https://wiki.juliusbaer.com/spaces/fkh/pages/639115366/Determine+Solicitation+Type

The first step when interacting with a client is to determine the correct solicitation type and capture it in the session in Wealth Navigator under "Solicitation".


Important

Depending on the conversation with the client, the initial solicitation type may alter. It may begin with active solicitation and then shift to reverse solicitation - for instance, due to a change in the investment amount or underlying financial instrument; or an initially unsolicited transaction can become advised when the RM spontaneously provides an investment view without being asked.

Active solicitation activesolicitation

  • RM is recommending one or several financial instrument(s) to one or several client(s), i.e. RM is making a selection based on client’s circumstances/portfolio
  • An investment recommendation includes a call for action:
    • buy/increase a position
    • hold a position
    • sell/reduce a financial instrument, or
    • change one financial instrument for another (switch)
    • Also: any recommendation regarding the amount or the underlyings for a structured product


Important

It is not relevant who initiated the contact. The decisive factor is if the RM is providing a recommendation.



Examples:

  • Sending Proposals
  • Making switch recommendations
  • Providing a list with a selection of financial instruments (short list) or forwarding one instrument
  • Forwarding of Marketing Material and expressing an implicit or explicit recommendationexplicitimplicitrecommendation

Example - explicit recommendation: “Dear Mr. Meier, please find attached the newest Investment Views. I would recommend an investment as it would perfectly fit to your portfolio.”
Example - implicit recommendation: “Dear Mr. Meier, please find attached the newest Investment View as this could be interesting for you.”

  • Communicating a WN monitoring alert with a expressing an implicit or explicit recommendation

Example - explicit recommendation: “The Bank has a negative view on Fund XY. Therefore, I (RM) would recommend selling it and replace it with Fund AB.”
Example - implicit recommendation: “Dear client, I am writing to you because your BRC has expired, For your information, the same issuer is issuing another BRC maturing in 2027."
                                                          "Dear client, I am informing you that your treasury bill is maturing in three days, let us know if you want to rollover for the same period." → implicit recommendation to rollover using the same instrument with the same period. 
                                                          "Dear client, I am informing you that your bond is maturing in three days, please advice if you wish to trade again." → implicit recommendation to trade the exact instrument again. 

  • Client is approaching RM with an unspecific request and RM recommends a certain financial instrument

Example: "Dear Lucie (RM), I want to invest CHF 100k, What stocks do you recommend?"

Reverse solicitation reversesolicitation

  • The client is asking for advice on a specific financial instrument or on a specific investment idea, using at least 3 defining criteria:
    • Instrument class or sub-asset class
    • Region/Geography/Country or currency
    • Specific market, industry sector or branch
    • Issuer, provider or manager
    • Investment strategy or topic, e.g. automation & robotics, artificial intelligence etc.
    • Instrument features (e.g. duration, hedging).


Important

Advice Light: Under the Service Model Advice Light, active solicitation is not permitted; therefore, client interaction must be initiated by a specific request of the client for either a specific financial instrument or a specific investment idea.

Full MiFID Standard: "Specific investment idea" classifies as actively solicited under Full MiFID standard, as the client’s request is not sufficiently detailed. Full MiFID standard requires the client to mention either the name of the financial instrument or the ISIN to be treated as reverse solicited case. 

or

  • the client changes parameters (quantity / underlying) of an actively solicited transaction. The RM then needs to perform another suitability check and shares result with the client (→ approve if still suitable or advice against in case the investment becomes unsuitable)

or

  • the client instructs the RM to buy a financial instrument and RM provides negative advice (advice against) based on (an) alert(s) triggered in Wealth Navigator.

Cross-border considerations

Ensure that you thoroughly document the client’s request and discussion in adherence to the rules of the respective country manual.

Not all JB entities have full market access in all countries and advice may only be given on request of the client (= reverse solicitation). The bank is requested to document this accordingly.

Examples:

  • Client asks RM about their opinion on Apple shares
  • Client states that they want to invest in a new issued bond (criteria #1) in European (criteria #2) health care companies (criteria #3) and asks RM about their opinion
  • RM actively proposes to buy 500k Nestle shares. Client wants to buy 1m, which leads to an unsuitable investment (e.g. concentration risk alert is triggered) requiring the RM to advise against the transaction

Unsolicited unsolicited

  • The client trades or instructs the RM to trade a specific financial instrument, without asking for advice and RM does not provide advice

This includes

    • trades carried out based on Marketing Material sent out to client(s), without any further  provided by the RM and/or material is not individualized for one client*
    • trades carried out based on forwarding of complete list with financial instrument to the client, upon client's request, without further recommendation provided by the RM
    • trades carried out based on Factual Information provided to the client, upon client's request, without further recommendation provided by the RM
    • support provided on timing of transaction, transaction specifics (limit vs. market orders) or support provided on mechanism/features of a structured product (even if they have an impact on performance) as long as the RM does not provide a view on the underlying's (→ Technical Advice)

* Forwarding marketing or research material which is prepared and published by the Bank is not considered Investment Advice, provided that the RM does not make reference to the client’s portfolio and/or makes any further call for action. However, if the marketing or research material is prepared or altered for one or multiple designated client(s), then this is considered Investment Advice.

Under MiFID (applicable to RML's in EEA and RML CH, when client is domiciled in Germany), sending research or marketing material at the initiative of the RM is considered "active solicitation". Make sure to perform a proper suitability assessment before sending such materials to a MiFID client, i.e. run a session in Wealth Navigator.

Examples of Marketing Material:

Client asks RM to submit transactions based on the following material:

  • Investment Views
  • Fund/Equity Updates
  • Market Opportunities
  • Targeted Investment Idea
  • Pitchbooks
  • Investment Advisory/Portfolio Management Brochures

Examples of complete lists of financial instruments

  • RM forwards a list with all US-Treasury-Bonds maturing in 3 months.

Examples of Technical Advice

  • Client wants to invest CHF 100k in Nascar Ltd. on Friday. RM recommends waiting until Monday when the US unemployment figures are released, since the information may have an impact on the share price.
  • Client wants to setup a structured product on certain underlying's (Nestle, ABB, Roche) and RM advises on the features of the SP such as barrier level, barrier type, coupon frequency, maturity date etc.
  • The RM informs the client that an existing BRC will expire in 3 days. The client expresses interest in investing in a new BRC and enquires about the maturity date. In response, the RM recommends a BRC with a shorter maturity period.


Important: Advice on a new category of a Financial Instrument is considered as Investment Advice
  • RM advises the client to buy a Structured Product rather than investing in the underlying's directly.
    Example: Client wants to by Nestle shares and RM recommends to rather buy a Structured Product with Nestlé shares as underlying (e.g. KODA) or 
  • RM advises on the underlying's of a Structured Product.
    Example: Client wants to buy a Structured Product with Nestle, ABB and Roche as underlying's and RM recommends to replace Roche with Novartis.
