2.) OTC (Equity) Options

OTC Plain Vanilla Options with the following underlyings can be traded:

  • Equities
  • Mutual Funds
  • ETF’s
  • Indices
  • Hedge Funds

Only on a case by case check as the KID has to be manually created:

  • Futures incl. Commodities, only cash-settled, no physical delivery future allowed irrespective of the underlying
  • Baskets
