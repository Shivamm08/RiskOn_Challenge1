HOW DOES THE NEW K&E FORM LOOK LIKE?  

The CIP and the K&E form do no longer need to be signed by the RM (see also part 2 – how to assess and document K&E of a new client). The form will be a new worldwide standardized Bank form.

Please note: The CIP and K&E form are going to be two different forms, nevertheless both are still mandatory and need to be completed. The CIP underwent a simplification and re-design (e.g. elimination of the leverage section and cover pages). For the CIP renewal, K&E is no longer part of it.  For ESG-related CIP question please refer to the respective separate section in the WIKI. 


           

WHAT ARE THE MAIN CHANGES?

In the new K&E form particularly the categories Fixed Income, Structured Products and Derivatives have a higher granularity. Additionally, also Digital Assets and Real Estate are included. The categorization in “good”, “limited” and “none” regarding Knowledge and the trading frequency to determine the Experience remains the same:

HOW WILL DIAS CHECK THE NEW K&E CATEGORIES?

DiAS will support you in checking the K&E level but going forward there will be a distinction in the assessment depending on the instrument. For direct investments (e.g., bonds, stocks etc.) DiAS checks the corresponding K&E category. For indirect investments (e.g., funds, structured products etc.) DiAS will check the K&E level in the respective instrument but also the underlying.

Example:

  • Direct: For Microsoft Shares -> K&E Category “Equity instruments” is checked
  • Indirect: For a Barrier-Reverse Convertible on Microsoft -> K&E Category “Structured Products – non-principal protected products” and “Equity instruments” is checked

How does DiAS then handle multi-asset class funds? It depends to the fund classification. For multi-asset class funds, where we have sufficient data available, usually the K&E in “Traditional” or “Non-traditional funds” is checked and in addition “Equity Instruments” and “Investment grade bonds”. There is no further drilldown made. If insufficient data is available, only the category “Traditional” or “Non-traditional funds” is applied.


As of today, depending on the solicitation scenario (solicited or unsolicited) and client’s information on the K&E-form, you might need to warn or educate a client in case the client has limited or no knowledge. You might also need to consider further information in case the client has no trading experience with the instrument in scope. This methodology remains the same. (Link). Further details to the education process in case of limited or no knowledge can be found in a separate chapter.
